# Canon Register

This file tracks major setting decisions and their status. It does not replace detailed records in the Campaign Bible.

## Status definitions

- **PROPOSED:** Under consideration; not canon.
- **APPROVED:** Accepted direction; details may remain incomplete.
- **ESTABLISHED:** Confirmed setting fact.
- **REJECTED:** Considered and declined.
- **REVISED:** Previously established material deliberately changed.
- **UNRESOLVED:** An open question that must remain unanswered.

## Established / approved directions

| Topic | Status | Current record |
|---|---|---|
| Rules system | ESTABLISHED | Pathfinder First Edition |
| Setting identity | ESTABLISHED | Original world, not Golarion |
| Technology | ESTABLISHED | High-medieval. No industrial or steampunk technological culture. |
| Alchemy | ESTABLISHED | Exists within Pathfinder's framework as specialized knowledge and craft; it does not imply industrialization or advanced machinery. |
| Setting atmosphere | ESTABLISHED | Grounded high-medieval fantasy and political/dynastic conflict are central; supernatural horror exists but is not the main theme. |
| Gods | ESTABLISHED | The Pathfinder gods are real. |
| Divine magic | ESTABLISHED | Divine spellcasters are uncommon and devote themselves to a god; deeper magic lore remains open. |
| Arcane magic | ESTABLISHED | Arcane spellcasters are rare. Divine spellcasting is also uncommon; an average person may never witness a spell. |
| Ratfolk | ESTABLISHED | Naturally subterranean and decentralized; prejudice against them is widespread across surface societies, with a few tolerant or accepting communities. They are not inherently evil, criminal, diseased, or hostile. |
| Elven great power | ESTABLISHED direction | Elves possess a major political power; one of the six Great Houses is an influential elven dynasty loosely inspired by the Lannister archetype. |
| Dwarves | ESTABLISHED | A dwarven empire has always existed and has never collapsed; it has never been especially massive. Dwarves primarily live in mountains and underground, rarely in conventional surface stone-built settlements. Cultural pride leads them to reproduce only with other dwarves. |
| Other ancestries | ESTABLISHED | Other Pathfinder ancestries may exist; none are designated rare or uncommon by default. The continent remains human-dominated. |
| Player character relationship | ESTABLISHED | The two PCs already know each other before Session One; exact relationship history and dynamic remain to be documented. |
| Continent map | UNRESOLVED | Uploaded atlas is provisional reference artwork, not final geography |
| Legacy names and history | UNRESOLVED | Existing names and direct analogues require original renaming and substantial reworking |
| GM authority | ESTABLISHED | GM approves whether generated events become canon |

## Continuity rules

1. **Originality:** The imported Bible retains names and historical scaffolding from Game of Thrones. New material must use original identities and substantially rework the underlying histories, not merely rename copied material.
2. **Gods and Pathfinder cosmology:** The Pathfinder gods are real. Their deeper lore and the details of divine magic remain open for development.
3. **Map status:** The uploaded atlas is a working visual reference only. Its names, borders, terrain, and exact geography are not final canon.
4. **Technology boundary:** High-medieval technology is the approved baseline. Industrial and steampunk technology are excluded unless the GM explicitly revises this decision.
