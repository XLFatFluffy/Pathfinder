# Canon Register

This file tracks major setting decisions and their status. It does not replace detailed records in the Campaign Bible.

## Status definitions

- **PROPOSED:** Under consideration; not canon.
- **APPROVED:** Accepted direction; details may remain incomplete.
- **ESTABLISHED:** Confirmed setting fact.
- **REJECTED:** Considered and declined.
- **REVISED:** Previously established material deliberately changed.
- **UNRESOLVED:** An open question that must remain unanswered.

## Explicit continuity flags

1. **Tone and technology conflict:** The imported Bible says gothic, steampunk, industrial, and Dishonored influences are not part of the setting. Earlier project brainstorming explored grimdark gothic horror with steampunk/industrial/alchemical elements. Do not resolve this without user approval.
2. **Legacy naming:** The Bible still contains Game of Thrones names and direct analogues. The newer direction is to rename and substantially rework the setting. Do not propagate those names into new canon records as final identities.
3. **Gods and Pathfinder cosmology:** The setting's uncertainty about gods must be preserved even though Pathfinder provides the mechanical and religious framework.
