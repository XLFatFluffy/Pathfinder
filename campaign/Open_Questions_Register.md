# Open Questions Register

Tracks unresolved setting questions so that uncertainty is deliberate rather than accidental. An unresolved question is not a missing fact that should be guessed.

## Priority key
- **P0 — Session critical:** Must be answered or deliberately left open before the next session.
- **P1 — Near-term:** Likely to affect the next few sessions.
- **P2 — Worldbuilding:** Important, but not needed immediately.
- **P3 — Deep mystery:** Keep open until clues or campaign events justify an answer.

## Rules
1. Do not answer an open question by inference unless the GM approves the answer.
2. Record competing in-world beliefs separately from objective GM truth.
3. When a question is answered, move the answer into its proper lore record and mark this entry RESOLVED with a reference.
4. Questions can remain intentionally unresolved indefinitely.
5. Add new questions when a decision creates important consequences.
