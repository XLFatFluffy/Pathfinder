# Worldbuilding Decision Log

This is the audit trail for deliberate setting decisions. It records why a decision was made and what it supersedes. It does not replace the Canon Register or detailed lore records.

## How to use this log

- Record meaningful GM-approved decisions, not every brainstorm.
- Label each entry **APPROVED**, **ESTABLISHED**, **REVISED**, or **REJECTED**.
- Keep proposals out of canon until explicitly approved.
- When revising an earlier decision, identify the prior record and update all affected documents.
- Include a date and links to affected records or commits where practical.

### 2026-11-03 — Great Rebellion historical foundation confirmed
**Status:** ESTABLISHED  
**Decision:** The dragon-riding dynasty held the Imperial Seat for approximately 400 years and possessed exactly twelve dragons at its height. Eligible Targaryen blood was required for dragon bonding; each rider could bond with one dragon through a telepathic connection, and eligibility depended on reproduction within the eligible bloodline. Six Great Houses rebelled for two connected objectives: to preserve their political independence against increasing centralization and to eliminate dragon-riding as the foundation of Targaryen political and military supremacy. The rebellion lasted a few years. The dwarven Great House was its principal mastermind and developed specialized dragon-slaying ballistas. Most dragons were killed; surviving dragons fled after losing their riders and their fate remains unknown. The Targaryen ruling family was nearly exterminated, the Imperial Seat fell to a different dynasty, and the dwarven victors claimed Dragonstone. One nine-year-old Crown Prince survived the destruction of Dragonstone; his survival is concealed and his personal name belongs to the player.
**Reason:** The GM explicitly confirmed the previously questioned historical details and clarified that the rebellion's two objectives were both intentional: independence and elimination of dragon-riding so the Targaryens could no longer hold power.
**Affected records:** `World_History.md`, `Campaign_Bible.md`, `Canon_Register.md`, `Current_World_State.md`, `Open_Questions_Register.md`.
**Still unresolved:** Exact dates, original names, individual roles of the six houses, battle chronology, new dynasty, capital, and postwar settlement.


### 2026-11-03 — Post-rebellion ruling sequence and puppet monarch established
**Status:** ESTABLISHED sequence; ruling house identity and monarch's controller unresolved

**Reason:** The GM confirmed the compromise nature of the human candidate, his status as a puppet, the dwarven successor's reluctant acceptance and return to Dragonstone, and the current relationship between the dwarven empire and human monarchy.

### 2026-11-03 — Revised continental scale, geographic principles, and minor houses
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / APPROVED DIRECTION
**Decision:** The continent will be substantially smaller than Westeros, using approximate working dimensions of 1,700 miles north to south and 1,300 miles east to west at its widest point. The revised map should preserve the uploaded atlas's recognizable geographic identity while making its physical geography coherent. The former scale bar is superseded. The central inland sea must support substantial shipping and naval commerce; the southern reaches remain politically fragmented; and the dwarven empire should appear comparatively limited on the surface while its true reach is primarily subterranean and poorly understood by outsiders.

**Minor houses:** Established as a category of wealthy lords and ladies holding relatively small territories and local holdings. They have local wealth and standing but are nearly politically insignificant in continent-wide affairs. They do not rival the Great Houses or automatically hold seats on the Imperial Council. Their names, number, holdings, and allegiances remain open.

**Reason:** The GM wants to retain the atlas's geographic identity while reducing the scale to suit the campaign, and wants wealthy minor nobility without adding more continent-level political powers.
**Still unresolved:** Final map dimensions after image revision; exact regions, rivers, roads, borders, Great House seats, minor-house names, territories, and allegiances. The specific previously proposed Great House placements remain proposals pending map review.

### 2026-11-04 — Stage 6: Imperial Court and Government approved
**Status:** ESTABLISHED
**Decision:** The GM approved the proposed structure of the Crown, royal household, Imperial Council, five court factions, and current political climate.

The established royal offices are Royal Chancellor, Master of Coin, Master of Laws, Master of War, Royal Spymaster, and Lord Chamberlain. These officials serve the Crown; Great Houses and factions seek influence over appointments. The Crown maintains a permanent household guard and a distinct royal military force, sufficient for protecting Valedorn and ordinary royal authority but not assumed capable of defeating all six Great Houses together.

The Imperial Council has six permanent seats, one appointed by each Great House. The monarch presides without an additional Great House seat. The Council addresses inter-house disputes, extraordinary taxation, major military levies and wars, significant treaties, recognition of royal succession, relations with the dwarven empire, and laws affecting Great House privileges. It is an aristocratic negotiating body, not a modern parliament. Exact procedures and legal powers remain unresolved.

Five overlapping court movements are established: Royal Court, Traditionalists, Royal Expansionists, Merchant Interests, and Religious Establishment. Membership is not determined by Great House affiliation, and these movements need not be formal organizations.

The present political climate includes a monarch seeking greater authority without provoking rebellion; Great Houses competing privately while upholding the settlement publicly; Kharadorn's independence and distrust; Vaelorian-Merrowen commercial competition; Edarth and Veyr's different concerns about centralization; growing influence of the Religious Establishment; and weak Crown control in the southern reaches.

**Reason:** The GM approved the proposed Stage 6 government and court framework and requested that it be canonized before development continued.
**Still unresolved:** Monarch and officeholder identities; royal force composition; Council procedures; representatives; faction leadership, membership, and relative influence; and the religious institutions and doctrines represented by the Religious Establishment.
**Affected records:** Campaign Bible, Canon Register, Current World State, and future court/setting references.


### 2026-11-04 — Stage 7: Religion, Faith, and Religious Institutions approved
**Status:** ESTABLISHED
**Decision:** The GM approved the Stage 7 framework. The continent broadly recognizes the Pathfinder pantheon, while worship varies across regions, cultures, communities, and individuals; no single god is universally dominant. Major faiths maintain independent hierarchies rather than answering to one supreme religious authority. Clergy and divine spellcasters are distinct, and divine spellcasters remain uncommon. Religious institutions may wield significant social and political influence without universally governing secular territories or automatically overruling secular law. The Religious Establishment has a recognized and growing presence at the royal court but no automatic Imperial Council seat. An independent scholarly tradition exists outside religious hierarchies, though it may cooperate with them. The Great Houses have broad religious tendencies but no exclusive patron deities or uniform doctrines. Religious tensions may involve secular authority, competing interpretations, wealth, heresy, and divine power.

**Reason:** The GM approved the Stage 7 proposal and instructed that worldbuilding continue with approved lore recorded in GitHub.
**Still unresolved:** Divine magic's precise mediation; major religious institutions and leaders; regional prominence of gods; scholarly order's name and organization; religious courts' legal authority; and religion's precise influence over secular law, marriage, inheritance, and coronation.
**Affected records:** Campaign Bible, Canon Register, Current World State, and the new `Religion_and_Faith.md` reference.
