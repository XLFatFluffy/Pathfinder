# Worldbuilding Decision Log

This is the audit trail for deliberate setting decisions. It records why a decision was made and what it supersedes. It does not replace the Canon Register or detailed lore records.

## How to use this log

- Record meaningful GM-approved decisions, not every brainstorm.
- Label each entry **APPROVED**, **ESTABLISHED**, **REVISED**, or **REJECTED**.
- Keep proposals out of canon until explicitly approved.
- When revising an earlier decision, identify the prior record and update all affected documents.
- Include a date and links to affected records or commits where practical.

## Decisions

### 2026-11-03 — High-medieval technology
**Status:** ESTABLISHED  
**Decision:** The setting uses a high-medieval technological baseline. Industrial and steampunk technological cultures are excluded. Alchemy remains a specialized Pathfinder-based craft and does not imply industrialization or advanced machinery.  
**Reason:** The GM explicitly confirmed that the setting is high-medieval. Gothic horror and supernatural elements remain compatible with this boundary.  
**Supersedes:** Any earlier brainstorming that implied industrial or steampunk technology.  
**Affected records:** `Canon_Register.md`, `Current_World_State.md`, `Worldbuilding_Direction.md`.

### 2026-11-03 — Elven wealthy great-house archetype
**Status:** APPROVED direction; details unresolved  
**Decision:** The wealthy, politically influential great-house archetype previously compared to the Lannisters will be an elven dynasty.  
**Unresolved:** Name, seat, territory, heraldry, culture, resources, ruler, alliances, rivalries, and history.  
**Originality constraint:** Do not copy the source house's name, sigil, family, history, or plotline.

### 2026-11-03 — Subterranean ratfolk
**Status:** ESTABLISHED broad direction  
**Decision:** Ratfolk are subterranean, decentralized, and marginalized or hated in some surface societies. They are not inherently evil, criminal, diseased, or hostile. Their communities are diverse, and no single organization speaks for all ratfolk.  
**Unresolved:** Specific settlements, political structures, prejudice origins, organizations, and the PC's family history.

### 2026-11-03 — Grounded medieval fantasy and real Pathfinder gods
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / REVISED  
**Decision:** The campaign's central identity is grounded high-medieval fantasy with political and dynastic conflict. Supernatural horror remains part of the world but is not the main theme. The Pathfinder gods are real. Arcane spellcasters are rare; divine spellcasters are also uncommon and devote themselves to a god. An average person may live their entire life without witnessing a spell cast.  
**Reason:** The GM clarified that the original gothic steampunk campaign concept was superseded when the players requested a different direction. The GM also corrected the earlier uncertainty about the gods.  
**Supersedes:** Gothic steampunk as the campaign's central concept; prior records stating that the gods' existence is uncertain.  
**Affected records:** `Campaign_Bible.md`, `Canon_Register.md`, `Current_World_State.md`, `Worldbuilding_Direction.md`, `Open_Questions_Register.md`.

### 2026-11-03 — Ancestry and civilization revisions
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / REVISED  
**Decision:** Ratfolk prejudice is widespread across surface societies, though a few communities tolerate or accept them. Elves possess a major political power. The dwarven empire has always existed and has never collapsed; it has never been especially massive. Dwarves primarily live in mountains and underground and rarely inhabit conventional surface stone-built settlements. Dwarven pride leads them to reproduce only with other dwarves as a cultural choice, not an established biological incompatibility. Other Pathfinder ancestries may exist without rare/uncommon designation; the continent remains human-dominated.  
**Reason:** The GM corrected the earlier ancestry summary and replaced the inaccurate fallen-dwarven-empire history.  
**Supersedes:** The prior account of a collapsed, formerly much larger dwarven empire with surviving successor territories; the prior description of ratfolk prejudice as limited to some societies.  
**Affected records:** `Campaign_Bible.md`, `Canon_Register.md`, `Current_World_State.md`, `Worldbuilding_Direction.md`.


### 2026-11-03 — Dwarven imperial structure and Dragonstone
**Status:** ESTABLISHED

**Reason:** The GM clarified the relationship between the dwarven clans, imperial authority, and Dragonstone.  
**Affected records:** `Campaign_Bible.md`, `Canon_Register.md`, `Current_World_State.md`, `Worldbuilding_Direction.md`, `Open_Questions_Register.md`.


### 2026-11-03 — Great Rebellion historical foundation confirmed
**Status:** ESTABLISHED  
**Decision:** The dragon-riding dynasty held the Imperial Seat for approximately 400 years and possessed exactly twelve dragons at its height. Eligible Targaryen blood was required for dragon bonding; each rider could bond with one dragon through a telepathic connection, and eligibility depended on reproduction within the eligible bloodline. Six Great Houses rebelled for two connected objectives: to preserve their political independence against increasing centralization and to eliminate dragon-riding as the foundation of Targaryen political and military supremacy. The rebellion lasted a few years. The dwarven Great House was its principal mastermind and developed specialized dragon-slaying ballistas. Most dragons were killed; surviving dragons fled after losing their riders and their fate remains unknown. The Targaryen ruling family was nearly exterminated, the Imperial Seat fell to a different dynasty, and the dwarven victors claimed Dragonstone. One nine-year-old Crown Prince survived the destruction of Dragonstone; his survival is concealed and his personal name belongs to the player.
**Reason:** The GM explicitly confirmed the previously questioned historical details and clarified that the rebellion's two objectives were both intentional: independence and elimination of dragon-riding so the Targaryens could no longer hold power.
**Affected records:** `World_History.md`, `Campaign_Bible.md`, `Canon_Register.md`, `Current_World_State.md`, `Open_Questions_Register.md`.
**Still unresolved:** Exact dates, original names, individual roles of the six houses, battle chronology, new dynasty, capital, and postwar settlement.
