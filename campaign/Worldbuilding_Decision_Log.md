# Worldbuilding Decision Log

This is the audit trail for deliberate setting decisions. It records why a decision was made and what it supersedes. It does not replace the Canon Register or detailed lore records.

## How to use this log

- Record meaningful GM-approved decisions, not every brainstorm.
- Label each entry **APPROVED**, **ESTABLISHED**, **REVISED**, or **REJECTED**.
- Keep proposals out of canon until explicitly approved.
- When revising an earlier decision, identify the prior record and update all affected documents.
- Include a date and links to affected records or commits where practical.

## Decisions

### 2026-11-03 — High-medieval technology
**Status:** ESTABLISHED  
**Decision:** The setting uses a high-medieval technological baseline. Industrial and steampunk technological cultures are excluded. Alchemy remains a specialized Pathfinder-based craft and does not imply industrialization or advanced machinery.  
**Reason:** The GM explicitly confirmed that the setting is high-medieval. Gothic horror and supernatural elements remain compatible with this boundary.  
**Supersedes:** Any earlier brainstorming that implied industrial or steampunk technology.  
**Affected records:** `Canon_Register.md`, `Current_World_State.md`, `Worldbuilding_Direction.md`.

### 2026-11-03 — Elven wealthy great-house archetype
**Status:** APPROVED direction; details unresolved  
**Decision:** The wealthy, politically influential great-house archetype previously compared to the Lannisters will be an elven dynasty.  
**Unresolved:** Name, seat, territory, heraldry, culture, resources, ruler, alliances, rivalries, and history.  
**Originality constraint:** Do not copy the source house's name, sigil, family, history, or plotline.

### 2026-11-03 — Subterranean ratfolk
**Status:** ESTABLISHED broad direction  
**Decision:** Ratfolk are subterranean, decentralized, and marginalized or hated in some surface societies. They are not inherently evil, criminal, diseased, or hostile. Their communities are diverse, and no single organization speaks for all ratfolk.  
**Unresolved:** Specific settlements, political structures, prejudice origins, organizations, and the PC's family history.
