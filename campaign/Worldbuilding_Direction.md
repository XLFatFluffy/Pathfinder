# Current Worldbuilding Direction

**Status:** APPROVED direction / details unresolved  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-03

This file records newer creative direction that must be considered alongside the imported Campaign Bible. It does not silently rewrite older source material.

## Originality
- Use broad fantasy archetypes and political structures as inspiration, not copied names, maps, house identities, histories, sigils, or plotlines.
- Rename and substantially rework legacy Game of Thrones scaffolding before treating it as final setting lore.
- The existing continent atlas is a provisional visual reference, not the final geography.

## Major ancestries and societies
- The wealthy, politically influential great-house archetype previously compared to the Lannisters should be an elven dynasty. Its name, territory, culture, heraldry, and exact power base remain undecided.
- Ratfolk are a marginalized and hated people in some surface societies, with established subterranean societies and communities.
- Ratfolk are not inherently criminal, evil, diseased, or hostile. Prejudice is a feature of the societies that practice it, not an objective truth about ratfolk.
- Ratfolk communities are decentralized and diverse; no single settlement or organization speaks for all ratfolk.

## Tone and technology: unresolved conflict
The imported Bible describes a high-medieval setting and explicitly excludes gothic, steampunk, industrial, and Dishonored influences. Earlier campaign brainstorming explored grimdark gothic horror, decaying institutions, vampires, cults, and steampunk/industrial/alchemical atmosphere.

**This conflict remains unresolved.** Do not treat either direction as final until the GM explicitly chooses how they fit together. One possible future distinction is medieval social structure with selective gothic/alchemical technologies, but that is only a proposal, not canon.

## Working principles
- Preserve the uncertainty surrounding the gods.
- Divine magic exists mechanically and does not prove the gods exist.
- Civilization survives behind walls; wilderness is dangerous.
- Keep mysteries unresolved until deliberately answered.
- GM approval is required before proposed material becomes established canon.
