# Creature and Supernatural Records

Store setting-specific creature lore, supernatural phenomena, undead, vampires, dragons, monsters, and regional threats here.

- [Creature Registry](Creature_Registry.md) — campaign-wide index, status vocabulary, ecology standards, and initial established/prepared references.
- [Creature Record Template](Creature_Record_Template.md) — reusable structure for new creature and supernatural records.
- [Regional Occurrence Template](Regional_Occurrence_Template.md) — tracks evidence for a creature's presence and frequency in a named region without duplicating its master record.
- [Troll](Troll.md) — GM-only dossier for the provisional Session One troll encounter.

Pathfinder First Edition mechanics remain the default. Distinguish objective setting facts from folklore, religious claims, rumors, and mistaken beliefs. Preserve unresolved origins and mysteries.

## Record standards

- Do not treat every published Pathfinder creature as part of the setting.
- Record regional presence and frequency only when supported by evidence.
- Separate established canon, direct observations, reliable documentation, reports, rumors, and GM proposals.
- Keep public knowledge, specialist knowledge, PC knowledge, and GM-only truth distinct.
- Link encounters to master creature records without allowing encounter notes to overwrite creature canon.
- Use stable record IDs and preserve source references for future app import.
- Do not reproduce full published stat blocks in campaign records; cite the Pathfinder source and record only approved campaign-specific rules changes.
