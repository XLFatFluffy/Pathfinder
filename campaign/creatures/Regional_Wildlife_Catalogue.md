# Tallowmere Region — Proposed Ordinary Wildlife Catalogue

**Snapshot:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC  
**Status:** PROPOSED / GM reference  
**Visibility:** GM-ONLY  
**Purpose:** Provide ordinary ecological texture without establishing unsupported monster populations.

This catalogue does not change the established regional ecology baseline. Species listed here are reasonable candidates for ordinary background life and should be treated as proposed until observed or explicitly approved in play.

## Northfields

Potential ordinary fauna:
- Red deer and smaller deer where woodland and field margins permit.
- Rabbits and hares.
- Field mice, voles, and other small rodents.
- Foxes.
- Badgers.
- Crows, rooks, sparrows, finches, and other common farmland birds.
- Frogs and other wetland amphibians near drainage ditches and ponds.

Human use:
- Poultry, sheep, goats, cattle, pigs, horses, and working dogs are ordinary domesticated animals.
- Grain stores attract rodents and scavenging birds.

## Brackenwood

Potential ordinary fauna:
- Deer.
- Wild boar.
- Foxes.
- Badgers.
- Rabbits and hares around clearings.
- Owls, hawks, crows, woodpeckers, and songbirds.
- Small woodland mammals and amphibians.

Ecological note:
Charcoal burning, coppicing, hunting, gathering, and timber work mean the woodland is heavily influenced by human activity. Wildlife is not uniformly deep-wilderness fauna.

## River Vale

Potential ordinary fauna:
- Carp and other common freshwater fish.
- Pike or similar predatory freshwater fish where suitable.
- Eels.
- Frogs and aquatic amphibians.
- Ducks, geese, herons, and other waterfowl.
- Beavers or similar water-engineering mammals may occur in less intensively managed reaches, but should remain uncommon unless established.
- Otters may occur along quieter banks.

Ecological note:
Mills, ferries, barges, fishing, and settlements make the lower river a heavily used working environment.

## Greyback Ridges and quarry country

Potential ordinary fauna:
- Mountain goats or similar sure-footed grazers where terrain permits.
- Foxes.
- Badgers.
- Ravens and other carrion/scavenging birds.
- Snakes and lizards in warmer exposed areas.
- Bats in suitable caves and workings.

Cave safeguard:
The existence of caves, quarry cuts, and abandoned workings does not establish a monster lair, ruin, undead site, or supernatural presence.

## Eastern Shore

Potential ordinary fauna:
- Gulls and other seabirds.
- Cormorants or similar diving birds.
- Shore crabs and shellfish.
- Coastal fish.
- Seals or other coastal mammals may appear if consistent with the broader setting's ecology.

Storm safeguard:
Sudden inland-sea storms are established. A storm does not imply a supernatural cause.

## Tallowmere itself

Common urban fauna can include:
- Rats and mice.
- Pigeons and other city birds.
- Stray cats and dogs.
- Flies and other insects.
- Stable-associated animals and pests.

The presence of ordinary rats does not imply a connection to the ratfolk Underways.

## Dangerous-animal policy

Do not establish recurring dangerous predators, vermin infestations, magical beasts, undead populations, or monster lairs merely because the habitat could support them.

A consequential creature record should identify:
- habitat,
- reason for presence,
- food/shelter,
- evidence,
- seasonality where relevant,
- human interaction,
- and whether the creature is native, passing through, captive, escaped, summoned, or otherwise introduced.

## First-session creature note

The troll is a specific planned encounter, not evidence that trolls are common around Tallowmere. Its presence should be explained by the actual encounter circumstances and should not automatically establish a local troll population.
