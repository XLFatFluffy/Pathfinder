## Established rules reference

Use the published Pathfinder First Edition troll statistics without alteration unless the GM explicitly approves a change. Its regeneration is overcome by acid or fire. Consult the published stat block at the table; this campaign record is not a replacement stat block.

Do not add templates, class levels, special abilities, altered regeneration, or custom defenses as an unannounced difficulty adjustment.

## Campaign-specific preparation

- The troll is a planned opening-session threat associated with a damaged merchant wagon belonging to Selwick & Company.
- The encounter is provisional until the GM has reviewed both completed player-character sheets and the proposed companion's final statistics.
- The party consists of two level-five PCs, with a proposed companion whose final build remains subject to approval. A standard CR 5 troll can still be dangerous for a small party; assess the actual characters and available support before play.
- The encounter should communicate the regeneration mechanic through observable clues or consequences rather than relying on players already knowing the creature's statistics.
- Fire and acid should be discoverable or obtainable through the scene if the encounter is expected to be resolved through combat. Do not assume the PCs carry either.
- The PCs are not required to kill the troll. Protecting the wagon crew, preserving cargo, retreating, negotiating, or using the environment may all matter to the outcome.
- Do not convert the prepared encounter into proof that trolls are native, common, or otherwise established in the Tallowmere region.

## Knowledge boundaries

- **Public knowledge:** No specific local folklore or reliable public account has been established.
- **Specialist knowledge:** A character may identify the troll or recall relevant traits through appropriate Pathfinder skills and checks. The exact check and DC should be selected at the table based on the character and information sought.
- **PC knowledge:** Not predetermined. Use the completed sheets, backgrounds, skills, and discoveries in play.
- **GM-only:** The encounter's precise location, circumstances, and outcome remain open until play. No broader troll ecology has been established.

## Post-session update

After the session, record only what actually occurred:

1. Whether the troll appeared and whether it was defeated, driven off, escaped, or otherwise resolved.
2. The actual location and date, if established during play.
3. What the PCs and NPCs observed.
4. Any injuries, losses, damage, discoveries, or evidence left behind.
5. Whether anyone made a credible report and who received it.

An encounter outcome may establish that one troll was present at one location. It does not, by itself, establish a regional population or its frequency.
