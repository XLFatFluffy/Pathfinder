# Economy Records

Store shops, merchants, goods, services, currencies, trade routes, resources, production, markets, and economic relationships here.

When recording shop inventories, track item, quantity, price, availability, restock conditions, and visibility to players. Do not invent prices or stock as established facts until they are set in play or approved by the GM.
