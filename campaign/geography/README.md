# Geography Records

Store continent, territory, regional, physical-geography, settlement, and geographic-reference records here. Location-specific dossiers belong in `../locations/`; map artwork and calibrated map metadata belong in `../maps/`.

## Geographic authority

The authoritative master continental map is [../maps/continent_atlas.png](../maps/continent_atlas.png). Its status, orientation, working dimensions, Tallowmere anchor, and map-authority rules are documented in [../maps/continent_reference.md](../maps/continent_reference.md) and [../maps/README.md](../maps/README.md).

The continental coastline and broad geographic features are fixed unless the GM explicitly approves a revision. Regional and city maps must fit the master atlas, not the other way around. The obsolete scale bar in the original artwork is not a valid measurement reference.

## Record convention

Each substantial geographic record should include a stable name or ID, canon status, visibility, parent location, geographic description, population (if known), government (if known), connected routes, notable features, source references, and unresolved questions. Do not invent exact coordinates, distances, borders, or populations where they have not been established.
