# Geography Records

Store the continent, kingdoms, territories, regions, cities, settlements, landmarks, roads, rivers, and other geographic records here.

## Current status
- The continent's final name and geography are unresolved.
- The uploaded atlas in `campaign/The Continent_ A Fantasy Atlas Map.png` is provisional reference artwork.
- Do not treat its labels, borders, scale, or exact geography as final canon without review.

## Record convention
Each location record should include a stable ID, canon status, visibility, parent location, geographic description, population (if known), government (if known), connected routes, notable features, sources, and unresolved questions.
