# Campaign Timeline

**Status:** Initial chronological framework established  
**Current campaign date:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC  
**Calendar:** [Campaign Calendar](Calendar.md)

This timeline records established events in chronological order while preserving uncertainty. Approximate periods remain approximate; no exact historical dates are invented to make the timeline look tidier.

## Eras

The following era labels provide a usable historical framework. Some are descriptive working labels rather than formal names used consistently by every culture.

1. **Age Before Records** — history before surviving or reliable written accounts.
2. **Age of First Kingdoms** — the rise of early settled kingdoms and enduring noble lines; exact chronology is unknown.
3. **Age of Dragons** — the emergence and expansion of dragon-riding power before its full consolidation over the continent; exact chronology is unknown.
4. **Age of Targaryen Supremacy** — legacy working label for the period in which the dragon-riding dynasty held the Imperial Seat for approximately 400 years. The dynasty's final original name remains unresolved.
5. **The Great Rebellion** — the multi-year revolt of the six Great Houses, approximately 200–300 years before the present campaign.
6. **Age of Crowns** — the post-Rebellion political settlement, beginning with the Crowning and Year 1 AC.
7. **Present Age** — the current period of the post-Rebellion monarchy and competing Great House powers.

## Established Chronology

| Relative date | Event | Established details |
|---|---|---|
| Before the Great Rebellion | Dragon-riding dynasty | Held the Imperial Seat for approximately 400 years. At its height it possessed exactly twelve dragons. Its growing centralization threatened the independence of the Great Houses. |
| Approximately 200–300 years before 243 AC | Great Rebellion | Six Great Houses rebelled to preserve political independence and destroy dragon-riding as the basis of dynastic supremacy. The war lasted a few years. House Kharadorn was the principal architect and developed specialized dragon-slaying ballistas. |
| End of the Great Rebellion | Fall of the old dynasty | The ruling family was nearly exterminated, the Imperial Seat fell, and most dragons were killed. Surviving dragons fled after losing their riders; their fate remains unknown. |
| Post-Rebellion settlement | Dragonstone and the Imperial Seat | House Kharadorn took Dragonstone by conquest. House Calderan came to occupy the Imperial Seat under the post-Rebellion settlement. The exact date and detailed succession sequence remain unresolved. |
| Year 1 AC | The Crowning | The first full year after the Crowning that established the post-Rebellion order. |
| 225 AC (approximate) | Beginning of King Aldric's reign | King Aldric Calderan has ruled for approximately 18 years as of the campaign's opening date. This is a derived approximate year, not a separately established coronation date. |
| 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC | Campaign begins | The two level-five PCs arrive in Tallowmere and begin Session One. |

## Current Political Baseline

- King Aldric Calderan is the current monarch and has a legitimate hereditary claim and substantial formal authority.
- The Imperial Council has six permanent seats, one appointed by each Great House. The monarch presides but holds no additional Great House seat.
- House Kharadorn retains its dwarven empire and its conquered possession of Dragonstone; Khar-Dum remains its ancestral homeland.
- The six Great Houses and their broad territorial spheres, principal seats, current rulers, and heirs are established in their dedicated records.
- The exact borders, local jurisdictions, Council procedures, detailed succession chronology, and many historical dates remain open.

## Protected Unknowns

Do not fill these gaps by inference:

- The original name and heraldry of the fallen dragon-riding dynasty.
- The final original name of Dragonstone.
- Exact dates of the Great Rebellion, the fall of the old dynasty, the Crowning, and the first monarch's accession.
- The fate of dragons that fled after the rebellion.
- The identity and circumstances of surviving members of the fallen dynasty.
- The builders and purpose of the Ancient Barrier.
- The origins and limits of vampirism and other deep supernatural mysteries.

After each session, add only events that actually occurred at the table or decisions the GM explicitly approves. Record the in-world date, event, involved characters and NPCs, consequences, and whether the entry is public, player-discovered, or GM-only.
