# Tallowmere: Civic NPCs

**Status:** Initial cast established; details may be expanded through play.

These NPCs have offices, interests, limits, and relationships. They are not mandatory quest-givers. See `House_Denvor.md` for Lord Oswin Denvor, Lady Catrin, Ser Edwyn, and Lady Anwen.

## Civic Officers

### Reeve Marrec Holt
**Office:** Town Reeve | **Ancestry:** Human  
Methodical, restrained, and protective of administrative records. Marrec believes cities are governed through dependable routines more than grand speeches. He values accurate records, predictable dues, road and market maintenance, and the Denvors' confidence. He has more work than staff can comfortably manage and fears that a recordkeeping error could become a political scandal.

### Harbor Master Ysella Vane
**Office:** Harbor Master | **Ancestry:** Human  
Direct, experienced, and difficult to intimidate. Ysella rose through harbor work and knows the difference between a legitimate delay and a manufactured excuse. She wants safe traffic, fair quay allocation, reliable fees, and a strong harbor reputation. Shipowners demand priority, warehouse holders demand predictability, and the Denvors expect results.

### Chief Magistrate Odran Pell
**Office:** Chief Magistrate | **Ancestry:** Half-elf  
Patient, observant, and exacting about testimony. Odran prefers cases that survive scrutiny. He values orderly hearings and consistent procedure but sees how wealthy litigants can afford delay while poorer residents cannot. He cannot repair the entire system alone.

### Captain Brinna Salk
**Office:** Captain of the Watch | **Ancestry:** Human  
Practical and blunt, with a strong sense of responsibility to her patrols. Brinna knows which streets are neglected and which merchants expect special treatment. She wants better coverage, discipline, staffing, and less preventable violence. The watch is expected to cover too much ground with limited personnel.

### Castellan Hadrik Voss
**Office:** Castellan of Greywatch | **Ancestry:** Dwarf  
A veteran professional who values preparation, stores, and clear command. Hadrik serves the Denvors under a long-standing contract, not because all dwarves are naturally loyal to them. He wants Greywatch ready and the walls and armories maintained. He believes defenses need investment, while the lord and merchants disagree over priorities.

## Commercial Figures

### Meret Sorn
**Role:** Warehouse Guild factor | **Ancestry:** Human  
Polished, cautious, and good at remembering obligations. She values predictable trade, secure cargo, fire prevention, and reliable records. New firms challenge established privileges while the cost of improving old warehouses rises.

### Dagna Coppervein
**Role:** Shipwrights' Brotherhood master | **Ancestry:** Dwarf  
An exacting craftswoman with little patience for impossible deadlines. She protects apprentices, skilled labor, quality standards, and timber supply. Shipowners want faster repairs at lower cost; the Brotherhood cannot expand its workforce overnight.

### Pellan Reed
**Role:** Bargemen's Company organizer | **Ancestry:** Halfling  
Sociable, sharp, and politically perceptive. He knows the city cannot eat imported luxuries if inland grain stops moving. He wants fair river tolls, safe landings, and recognition for river workers. Sea merchants dominate council attention despite the city's dependence on river traffic.

### Ilyne Marris
**Role:** Independent moneychanger and broker | **Ancestry:** Human  
Careful, discreet, and scrupulous about written agreements. She serves merchants who cannot afford to be tied to one guild. She wants reliable clients and commercial introductions, but must avoid dependence on one patron or provoking established moneychangers.

## Religious and Charitable Figures

### Sister Alwen
**Role:** Hospice administrator | **Ancestry:** Human  
Calm, stubborn, and practical. She treats charity as work, not performance. She wants food, shelter, care for the sick, burial assistance, and support for abandoned children. Her institution is chronically short of funds and must decide whom it can realistically help.

### Brother Caldus
**Role:** Keeper of the sailors' shrine | **Ancestry:** Half-orc  
A thoughtful listener who records the names of the missing and lost. He does not promise every voyage will be safe. He values memorials, maritime rites, reconciliation, and sailors' accounts. Families sometimes demand certainty where none exists; wealthy shipowners prefer losses to remain private.

## Ratfolk Community Contacts

These are local contacts, not rulers of all Tallowmere ratfolk.

### Nix-of-the-Cistern
**Role:** Maintainer of a shared water chamber and neighborhood mediator | **Ancestry:** Ratfolk  
Elderly, patient, and attentive to practical needs. Nix prefers maintainable agreements over dramatic victories. Nix wants clean water, safe routes, repairs, and protection for families. Younger residents accuse Nix of accepting too little from surface authorities; Nix fears retaliation against vulnerable households.

### Tikka Vell
**Role:** Repairer and small-goods trader | **Ancestry:** Ratfolk  
Resourceful, fast-talking, and commercially ambitious. Tikka wants ratfolk workshops to sell openly in the Market Ward rather than through intermediaries. Tikka seeks trade access, workshop space, fair prices, and reliable suppliers, but some surface merchants exploit ratfolk sellers' weaker legal position.

### Serren Coil
**Role:** Courier and route guide | **Ancestry:** Ratfolk  
Quiet, observant, and protective of route knowledge. Serren guides trusted travelers through selected passages but refuses to sell access to family shelters or unsafe deeper routes. Serren wants safe passage and fair payment, while surface clients often assume that knowing one route means controlling the entire Underways.

## Use of the Cast
These are initial motivations, not complete biographies. Family ties, secrets, mechanical statistics, resources, and relationships can be developed when needed. NPCs respond to PC conduct and the passage of time rather than remaining static quest dispensers.


### Miraq Ashwhisk
**Role:** Alchemist and proprietor of Tallowmere Alchemical Supply | **Ancestry:** Ratfolk  
Miraq is a capable, independent craftswoman who has built a small but respected surface business through skill, careful accounts, and persistence. She is observant, dry-humored, technically curious, and slow to trust people who treat her as a novelty or expect her to speak for all ratfolk. She knows Tikka Vell through local trade and occasionally buys repaired glassware and small fittings from Tikka.

Miraq may become a professional contact, supplier, rival, confidante, or genuine friend to the ratfolk PC. Their shared ancestry and alchemical interests create natural common ground, but the relationship must develop through play. Miraq has her own customers, obligations, and views on how ratfolk should pursue acceptance. She will not automatically become a sidekick or hand over her trade secrets.

See [Shopkeepers and Stock](Shopkeepers_and_Stock.md) for her shop's inventory, prices, and commercial practices.
