# Tallowmere: Criminal Operators and Informal Networks

**Status:** ESTABLISHED GM-FACING FRAMEWORK — 2026-11-04  
**Parent record:** `Crime_and_Informal_Power.md`

Tallowmere has several identifiable criminal operators, but no unified thieves' guild, citywide criminal boss, or secret council. The people below are established individuals and small networks. Their relationships are practical, limited, and subject to change.

## 1. The Grey Net

**Activity:** Cargo diversion, dockside theft, false handling claims, and resale of misdirected goods.  
**Primary area:** Old Quays and the older warehouse lanes.  
**Current organizer:** Noll Tarrow, human, former tally clerk.

Noll knows how cargo moves through the harbor and understands the gaps between a vessel's declaration, a warehouse receipt, and the final delivery. The Grey Net is a loose arrangement of handlers, two or three carters, occasional lookouts, and buyers. Participants do not all know one another.

Noll prefers misdirection and paperwork to violence. He targets shipments where ownership is disputed, records are delayed, or a merchant is unlikely to pursue a modest loss. He avoids conspicuous attacks that would bring the Harbor Master and Watch down on the quays.

**Limits:** The Grey Net cannot control harbor operations, guarantee access to every warehouse, or move large quantities without attracting attention. Its members sometimes steal from one another.

**Current pressure:** Increased attention to warehouse fires and cargo records makes familiar methods riskier. Noll suspects that someone has been exaggerating losses to cover unrelated theft, but has no proof.

## 2. The Bent Seal

**Activity:** Forged cargo marks, altered receipts, false ownership papers, and commercial fraud.  
**Primary area:** Market Ward, Harbor Office approaches, and counting rooms.  
**Principal operator:** Edrik Vale, human, a copyist and occasional contract clerk.

Edrik produces convincing copies of ordinary commercial documents and arranges introductions to people willing to use them. He is not a master forger capable of reproducing every seal or official hand. His work is strongest where a rushed clerk checks only the visible details.

He sometimes works for merchants seeking to evade a fee, sometimes for thieves moving stolen cargo, and sometimes for people who simply want a dispute delayed. He does not disclose clients unless paid, threatened, or betrayed.

**Limits:** Edrik cannot create genuine legal authority, forge every guild mark, or make a false document survive careful examination. He avoids direct violence and has little control over what clients do after receiving a document.

**Current pressure:** The Chief Magistrate's office has begun comparing certain disputed cargo records. Edrik does not know whether the scrutiny is directed at him.

## 3. The Quiet Account

**Activity:** Debt collection, coercive lending, intimidation, and arranging private settlements.  
**Primary area:** Market Ward, Old Quays lodging houses, and the edges of the New Quays.  
**Principal broker:** Hessa Quill, halfling, former household steward.

Hessa knows which small traders are short of coin, which ship crews are paid late, and which households will accept a private loan rather than approach a moneychanger. She arranges loans between private parties and employs two collectors when repayment becomes contentious.

Some of the loans are lawful agreements with harsh terms. Others involve concealed fees, threats, or pressure on relatives and employers. Hessa is careful to keep her own name away from written instruments.

**Limits:** The Quiet Account is not a bank, does not possess a monopoly on lending, and cannot compel the Chief Magistrate to enforce an unlawful agreement. Hessa relies on reputation and intimidation more than organized force.

**Current pressure:** Ilyne Marris's secured 120 gp loan to a small merchant partnership is overdue. Hessa has heard that the partnership may seek private refinancing, but she is not established as the lender or owner of that debt.

## 4. Independent operators

### Sella Vane
**Ancestry:** Human  
**Activity:** Smuggling small quantities of undeclared goods through minor quays and river landings.  
Sella is a boat owner with a small crew, not a smuggling fleet. She moves goods when the risk and payment justify it. She prefers avoiding inspection to bribing officials, and she does not accept cargo she cannot identify well enough to price the risk.

### Othric Pell
**Ancestry:** Half-elf  
**Activity:** Information brokerage.  
Othric trades schedules, overheard conversations, debt rumors, and introductions. He exaggerates when it benefits him and sometimes sells the same information to competing buyers. He is not omniscient and cannot know private conversations without a plausible source.

### Dannel Rusk
**Ancestry:** Human  
**Activity:** Private debt enforcement and intimidation.  
Dannel works for several creditors, including some who operate lawfully. He has a reputation for pushing beyond the terms of an agreement when he believes a debtor has no powerful patron. He is not employed by Ilyne Marris and is not her agent.

## 5. Relationships and boundaries

- Noll Tarrow occasionally buys access to forged papers from Edrik Vale but does not trust him.
- Edrik has arranged documents for Sella Vane, though neither considers the other a partner.
- Hessa Quill and Dannel Rusk sometimes work together when a private creditor wants pressure applied. Their relationship is contractual and unstable.
- Othric Pell sells information to legitimate merchants, civic officers, and criminals. He does not belong to any one network.
- None of these operators commands the others.
- No named operator controls the Underways, and ratfolk residents are not a default source of criminal labor or routes.

## 6. Enforcement and exposure

The Watch knows that cargo theft, forged documents, undeclared shipments, and coercive debt collection occur. It does not possess enough evidence to arrest every suspected participant. An accusation is not proof.

The Chief Magistrate's office can pursue cases where witnesses, records, seized goods, or other evidence support a warrant or hearing. The Harbor Master can restrict access to harbor facilities within her authority. Guilds may discipline members under their own rules but cannot impose criminal sentences.

The PCs can investigate, bargain, compete, expose, hire, or ignore these operators. Any consequences should follow evidence, law, capability, and the choices made by the people involved.

## 7. GM safeguards

- Do not connect every theft, fire, debt, and forged record to one conspiracy.
- Do not make every operator hostile to the PCs.
- Do not make every operator violent or irredeemable.
- Do not assume the Watch is either wholly corrupt or wholly competent.
- Do not assign criminality to an ancestry or neighborhood.
- Do not let an operator know facts without a credible information path.
- Use Pathfinder rules for stealth, detection, social checks, combat, and other mechanics. These NPCs receive no special plot immunity.

## Open details

The exact number of associates, specific safe locations, criminal revenue, and which suspicions are supported by evidence remain situational. Do not establish a permanent headquarters or a hidden master organization unless later campaign events justify it.
