# Tallowmere: Current Situation and Rumors

**Status:** Initial campaign-era situation. These are active pressures and circulating claims, not a single central plot.

## Current Situation

Tallowmere is prosperous, but its prosperity is under strain. Trade continues and the harbor remains busy, yet several pressures are converging.

### 1. The Harbor Allocation Dispute
The Harbor Fellowship wants more predictable access to the newer quays and argues that established shipowners should receive priority during crowded seasons. Smaller traders and river interests fear that the largest firms will turn temporary priority into permanent privilege. Harbor Master Ysella Vane is caught between commercial pressure and the need to keep traffic moving.

### 2. Warehouse Fire Precautions
A recent series of small fires and near-misses has made warehouse safety a public issue. No major warehouse has burned, but the Warehouse Guild argues that improvements are overdue. Some owners suspect the guild is using safety concerns to force smaller competitors out of business. The cause of the incidents is not established.

### 3. The Northfield Harvest
The coming harvest is expected to be adequate but uneven. Several farms have reported damage and labor shortages. Grain merchants are watching the situation closely. There is no established famine, but a poor result could raise prices and sharpen disputes over storage and export.

### 4. Ratfolk Market Access
Ratfolk traders have renewed efforts to secure regular stalls and recognized leases in the Market Ward. Some merchants support the proposal; others object to changing established practice. The Denvor administration has not issued a comprehensive ruling. Tikka Vell is among those pushing for open trade, while Nix-of-the-Cistern urges caution about exposing vulnerable households to retaliation.

### 5. Denvor Succession and Household Influence
Lord Oswin remains the recognized ruler, but age and the future of the house are topics of quiet discussion. Ser Edwyn is increasingly involved in defensive planning and civic affairs. Lady Anwen has cultivated relationships outside the household's traditional circle. Neither development amounts to an open succession crisis.

### 6. Kharadorn's Strategic Attention
House Kharadorn has requested updated accounts of harbor defenses and the Denvors' obligations under the hereditary charter. This is a legitimate exercise of strategic oversight, but it has made the household and merchants nervous about possible new demands.

## Independent Momentum
These pressures continue whether the PCs intervene or not. They may resolve, worsen, change ownership, or be overtaken by new events. Do not force the PCs into them. Their actions can alter outcomes, create new opportunities, or cause consequences beyond their immediate intentions.

## GM Use
Keep the truth behind each rumor separate from what people believe. A rumor may be true, false, distorted, planted, or accurate but misunderstood. Decide its truth before making it a clue. Avoid retroactively making every rumor about the PCs or one overarching conspiracy.

## Open Details
The precise calendar date, recent fire locations, harvest figures, named merchant consortium, Kharadorn envoy, and truth behind each rumor remain to be established before play.
