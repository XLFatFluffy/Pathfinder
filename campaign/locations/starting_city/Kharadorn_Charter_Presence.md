# Tallowmere: Kharadorn Charter Presence

**Status:** ESTABLISHED LOCAL FRAMEWORK — 2026-11-04  
**Authority:** House Kharadorn and the hereditary Denvor charter.

## Purpose

House Kharadorn's authority in Tallowmere is strategic and charter-based. The Denvors govern the city in ordinary matters. Kharadorn monitors whether the obligations that justified the charter are being honored: tribute, harbor defense, and the provision of ships, supplies, or transport when lawfully called upon.

Kharadorn does not ordinarily appoint the Town Reeve, decide market stall disputes, run the city watch, or hear petty criminal cases. Its officials are present to protect the Great House's recognized interests, not to replace House Denvor's administration.

## The Charter House

Kharadorn maintains a modest office known locally as the Charter House, situated near the Harbor Office and accessible from the principal harbor road. It contains:
- A public receiving room for petitions concerning charter obligations and disputes involving Kharadorn rights.
- A secure records room for copies of the charter, tribute accounts, harbor-defense inventories, and formal correspondence.
- A small office for the resident charter representative and clerk.
- Limited quarters for visiting inspectors and escorts.

The Charter House is not a fortress and has no independent prison or general court.

## Resident representative

### Vardek Flintmark
**Office:** Kharadorn Charter Warden in Tallowmere  
**Ancestry:** Dwarf  
**Appointment:** Serves under the authority of High King Durnak and the Kharadorn administration.

Vardek is a precise, restrained official with a strong preference for written obligations and verifiable accounts. He is not a battlefield commander and does not pretend to be the city's ruler. He believes Kharadorn's rights are strongest when exercised predictably and without needless humiliation of the Denvors.

**Responsibilities:**
- Review tribute accounts and report arrears or irregularities.
- Inspect harbor-defense stores and the condition of works relevant to Kharadorn's strategic rights.
- Confirm whether ships, provisions, and transport promised under the charter are available when lawfully requested.
- Receive petitions alleging that the Denvors have breached a charter obligation or that Kharadorn officials have exceeded their authority.
- Send reports and recommendations to the relevant Kharadorn authorities.

Vardek cannot unilaterally rewrite the charter, seize private property without lawful authority, command the city watch, or adjudicate ordinary civil and criminal disputes.

### Clerk Belda Ironmark
**Ancestry:** Dwarf  
Belda maintains correspondence, copies, accounts, and inspection records. She is more approachable than Vardek but exacting about discrepancies. She does not have independent authority to make binding decisions.

## Escort and inspection party

The Charter House has no permanent field army. Vardek's ordinary personal escort consists of four trained guards when security warrants it. A larger inspection party may arrive from Kharadorn for a scheduled review, with its size and duration determined by the assignment.

The guards protect the representative, records, and visiting officials. They do not patrol Tallowmere streets or exercise general police powers. Any armed Kharadorn force entering the city beyond this limited presence would be a significant political event.

Castellan Hadrik Voss is a dwarf employed by House Denvor. He is not Vardek's subordinate and does not automatically share Kharadorn's position on every issue.

## Current defense review

A review of harbor defenses and charter obligations is expected. Its practical scope includes:
- Condition of the Harbor Chain and Boom and its operating equipment.
- Readiness of harbor-side defensive positions.
- Records of ships and transport that could be called upon.
- Condition and availability of stores held for defense.
- Denvor plans for repairs and maintenance.
- Whether recent trade growth has outpaced defensive arrangements.

The review is not a pretext for immediate occupation. It may reveal neglected maintenance, disagreements over cost, or genuine weaknesses. Findings and any required remedy must be based on inspection rather than assumed in advance.

## Relationship with House Denvor

Lord Oswin treats Vardek formally and prefers to resolve concerns before they become a charter dispute. Lady Catrin understands the value of maintaining a workable relationship. Ser Edwyn is likely to support serious defensive investment, though he may disagree over who pays. Lady Anwen is attentive to the economic and social consequences of any demands.

Vardek respects the Denvors' local authority but will not conceal a genuine failure to meet charter obligations. He also recognizes that a prosperous, cooperative Tallowmere is more useful to Kharadorn than a resentful city whose trade has been damaged by overreach.

## Political boundaries

- Kharadorn's strategic rights do not make every dwarf in the city a Kharadorn agent.
- The Charter Warden is not a secret police chief or city governor.
- The Charter House does not control the Underways or possess a complete map of them.
- Kharadorn does not automatically know the private identities or ambitions of the PCs.
- The secret dwarven underground route to Dragonstone remains GM-only and is not part of Vardek's routine local duties.

## Open details

The exact tribute amount, inspection date, formal reporting chain, charter wording, and historical precedents remain unresolved. No new tax, seizure power, military obligation, or restriction on lawful trade is created by this document.
