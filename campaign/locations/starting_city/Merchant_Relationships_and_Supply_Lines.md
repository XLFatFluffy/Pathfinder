# Tallowmere: Merchant Relationships and Supply Lines

**Status:** Initial commercial relationships established for campaign use. These are ordinary business ties, not a hidden unified conspiracy. Relationships may change as trade, debts, competition, and player actions develop.

## Why the City's Shops Depend on One Another

Tallowmere is a port and river market, not a self-sufficient town. Shops rely on farms, mills, regional workshops, ship arrivals, caravan traffic, and one another. A delay or shortage can affect several businesses without every merchant sharing the same interests.

## Commercial Relationships That Can Create Play

These are active points of contact, not mandatory quests:

- **Bread and provisioning:** Hesta Pellor and Ollo Venn compete for some bulk food contracts. They are not enemies; both can profit when ships arrive in numbers, and both lose when river traffic is interrupted.
- **The garrison contract:** Brunna Keld's work for House Denvor gives her predictable income but frustrates private customers who want quick armor commissions. Jorren Vale occasionally receives overflow weapon orders.
- **Alchemical sourcing:** Miraq's suppliers are fragmented. She can obtain ordinary reagents through trade, but uncommon components may require advance notice, a specialist contact, or a trip to the surrounding region. No universal extra crafting prerequisite is implied.
- **Harbor repairs:** Nessa Tor, the shipwrights, and warehouse owners all depend on a functioning harbor. They may cooperate during an emergency while disagreeing over who should pay.
- **Credit and reputation:** Ilyne Marris may introduce reliable merchants to one another, but an introduction is not a guarantee, endorsement, or loan.

## Business Entry for the PCs

The PCs can enter commerce by:
- Buying or renting a suitable premises.
- Starting with direct sales, commissions, or a small workshop.
- Purchasing wholesale inputs from existing suppliers or finding alternatives.
- Hiring workers and negotiating wages.
- Seeking guild recognition, remaining independent, or bargaining for a limited privilege.
- Building a customer list through quality, reliability, price, and reputation.
- Taking contracts from households, merchants, adventurers, temples, nobles, or civic offices.

Existing businesses may compete, cooperate, imitate successful products, or ignore the PCs depending on their interests. No merchant is automatically hostile because the PCs become successful. Market response should follow actual stakes and established relationships.

## Prices, Capacity, and Restocking

- The shop catalog's listed quantities are typical opening stock, not infinite inventory.
- A shop's production capacity is separate from its displayed stock. A smith may have raw materials but no free time; a food seller may have ingredients but limited daily output.
- Wholesale prices are negotiated and may depend on volume, quality, delivery, payment timing, and reliability.
- Do not assume every supplier has stock simply because an item is mundane. Check the relevant route, season, and current events when a purchase is unusually large.
- Record meaningful transactions and commitments. A deposit, exclusive contract, loan, or promised delivery is an obligation for both sides.
- Standard Pathfinder prices remain the baseline for equipment. Local differences should have an identifiable commercial reason and be stated before the PCs commit.

## Unresolved Details

Specific wholesale price sheets, supplier names beyond those listed, delivery timetables, guild dues, property rents, wage scales, and the exact capacity of each business remain open. Establish these when the PCs pursue a transaction that needs them rather than inventing numbers in advance.


## Selwick & Company — Opening Campaign Contact

**Master Tavren Selwick** is a wealthy Tallowmere merchant and shipowner based near the Old Quays. His firm trades in finished cloth, household wares, lamp oil, metal fittings, and provisions. He has a reputation for paying obligations and keeping accurate cargo accounts. He is established as a useful commercial contact, not a guaranteed patron.

A wagon belonging to Selwick & Company is the planned target of the troll attack on the PCs' approach to Tallowmere. If the PCs protect the crew or recover the consignment, Selwick offers 30 gp, a meal and one night's lodging at the Blue Heron, and a signed introduction to Meret Sorn of the Warehouse Guild. This recommendation provides modest credibility and a meeting opportunity, not guild membership, credit, legal privilege, or universal trust. Details are in `Selwick_Merchant_Contact.md`.
