# Tallowmere Ratfolk Community Networks and NPCs

**Status:** Established Tallowmere community networks and named contacts; no player-character relationships are established.  
**Visibility:** GM reference. Publicly visible activities may be shared with players; private routes, household details, and personal beliefs are not automatically public.  
**Authority:** Builds on the established decentralized Underways framework. This document does not create a ratfolk government or a single leader.

## 1. Foundational structure

Tallowmere's ratfolk community is a network of neighborhoods, households, work groups, and practical relationships. It has no single ruler, council with universal authority, or unified political platform. People coordinate when their interests overlap and disagree when they do not.

The groups below are informal networks, not formal guilds or political parties. Membership is fluid. A ratfolk may participate in several, none, or only a family network.

## 2. The Cistern Keepers

**Type:** Informal maintenance and water-access network  
**Known contact:** Nix-of-the-Cistern  
**Additional contact:** Vessa Reedtail, elder and practical mediator

**Purpose**
- Maintain shared wells, cisterns, drainage channels, and known water routes.
- Warn households about contamination, flooding, and unsafe passages.
- Negotiate access to surface repairs and materials when underground infrastructure fails.
- Settle ordinary disagreements over shared maintenance and water use.

**Nix's position:** Nix favors cautious negotiation with surface authorities. Nix believes that a practical agreement protecting water and family safety is worth more than a public confrontation that residents cannot control.

**Vessa's position:** Vessa is more protective of neighborhood autonomy. She believes surface officials remember ratfolk only when they need labor or information, and she distrusts promises not backed by written terms or repeated conduct.

**Leverage:** Local knowledge, maintenance labor, access to certain water points, and the ability to coordinate repairs. The Keepers do not control every cistern or underground water source.

**Internal tension:** Nix is willing to negotiate; Vessa wants guarantees and community consent before any surface access agreement.

## 3. The Open Stall Petition

**Type:** Informal merchant and workshop advocacy network  
**Known contact:** Tikka Vell  
**Additional contact:** Miraq Ashwhisk

**Purpose**
- Secure regular market stalls and recognized leases for ratfolk traders.
- Reduce dependence on surface intermediaries who control prices and customer access.
- Establish fair treatment in market inspections, measures, and contract enforcement.
- Build a record of reliable ratfolk businesses that can be used in negotiations.

**Tikka's position:** Commercially ambitious and willing to negotiate directly with the Market Guild. Tikka wants visible, durable access rather than informal permission that can be withdrawn without explanation.

**Miraq's position:** Miraq supports better market access but is cautious about putting every household's livelihood behind a public petition. Miraq prefers several small leases and dependable customers over a single symbolic market victory.

**Leverage:** Existing customers, craft and repair services, reliable deliveries, and the ability to organize a petition. The network cannot compel the Market Guild or landlords to grant leases.

**Internal tension:** Tikka favors a public and organized campaign; Miraq favors incremental commercial gains and minimizing exposure to retaliation.

## 4. The Underway Couriers

**Type:** Informal courier, guide, and route-safety network  
**Known contact:** Serren Coil  
**Additional contact:** Pellit Quickstep

**Purpose**
- Carry messages and small goods between trusted households.
- Maintain knowledge of safe, unsafe, flooded, sealed, or unstable passages.
- Warn residents about dangerous surface patrols, structural failures, and unfamiliar people entering known routes.
- Arrange introductions when trust and purpose justify them.

**Serren's position:** Route knowledge is a responsibility, not a commodity to sell indiscriminately. Serren will not expose family shelters or sensitive passages merely because someone offers money.

**Pellit's position:** Younger and more commercially minded. Pellit believes the couriers could earn more by offering selected route services to trusted surface clients, but accepts that careless disclosure could endanger households.

**Leverage:** Information about particular passages, trusted messengers, and knowledge of how to move through parts of the Underways. This is not a citywide intelligence service, and the network does not know every tunnel.

**Internal tension:** Serren prioritizes secrecy and safety; Pellit sees carefully controlled surface work as a way to improve ratfolk standing and income.

## 5. The Quiet Hearths

**Type:** Loose household and mutual-aid network  
**Known contact:** Vessa Reedtail  
**Additional contact:** Dorrik Sootpaw

**Purpose**
- Protect children, elders, and households that avoid surface attention.
- Share food, tools, medicine, and temporary shelter when families face hardship.
- Keep disputes from exposing vulnerable residents to retaliation.
- Preserve neighborhood autonomy and the right not to participate in public campaigns.

**Vessa's position:** Vessa is a strong advocate for consent and household safety. She does not oppose every reform, but believes public visibility can put people at risk when legal protection is weak.

**Dorrik's position:** Dorrik is a young salvage worker who has little patience for repeated discrimination. He favors direct public pressure and organized refusal to work for merchants who mistreat ratfolk. He is not an established violent extremist and does not command a gang.

**Leverage:** Mutual aid, household trust, labor coordination, and the ability to withhold cooperation. The network has no unified treasury or armed force.

**Internal tension:** Vessa prioritizes protection and consent; Dorrik believes caution has allowed discrimination to persist.

## 6. Named NPC dossiers

### Nix-of-the-Cistern

**Role:** Respected mediator and water-maintenance contact.  
**Network:** Cistern Keepers; trusted by several Quiet Hearth households.  
**Public manner:** Patient, observant, careful with promises.  
**Immediate goal:** Secure dependable water access and repairs without exposing private routes or inviting surface control.  
**Leverage:** Trust, practical knowledge, and relationships with maintainers.  
**Limits:** No authority over all ratfolk households; no ability to guarantee community-wide compliance.  

### Tikka Vell

**Role:** Ratfolk trader and market-access advocate.  
**Network:** Open Stall Petition.  
**Public manner:** Direct, commercially sharp, persistent.  
**Immediate goal:** Obtain regular stalls and recognized leases for ratfolk businesses.  
**Leverage:** Customers, craft relationships, and a growing petition network.  
**Limits:** Cannot force landlords or the Market Guild to agree.  

### Serren Coil

**Role:** Courier and guide with knowledge of selected Underways routes.  
**Network:** Underway Couriers.  
**Public manner:** Quiet, guarded, practical.  
**Immediate goal:** Keep trusted routes safe and prevent sensitive passages from becoming common knowledge.  
**Leverage:** Specific route knowledge and trusted introductions.  
**Limits:** Does not control all passages or possess a comprehensive map.  

### Miraq Ashwhisk

**Role:** Repairer and small trader.  
**Network:** Open Stall Petition, with customers across several neighborhoods.  
**Public manner:** Friendly but cautious about commitments.  
**Immediate goal:** Build a stable business with repeat customers and fair contracts.  
**Leverage:** Craft skill, reliability, and commercial relationships.  
**Limits:** Has no formal political office and cannot speak for all workshops.  

### Vessa Reedtail

**Role:** Elder, household mediator, and mutual-aid organizer.  
**Network:** Quiet Hearths; respected by some Cistern Keepers.  
**Public manner:** Firm, protective, difficult to impress.  
**Immediate goal:** Ensure any public reform effort includes safeguards for households that cannot afford retaliation or exposure.  
**Leverage:** Trust among families, memory of local agreements, and ability to coordinate aid.  
**Limits:** Her influence is personal and neighborhood-based, not a formal office.  

### Pellit Quickstep

**Role:** Young courier and messenger.  
**Network:** Underway Couriers.  
**Public manner:** Energetic, curious, eager to prove useful.  
**Immediate goal:** Expand legitimate courier work and earn more reliable income.  
**Leverage:** Speed, contacts, and familiarity with selected routes.  
**Limits:** Inexperienced; does not know every passage and may underestimate the risks of disclosure.  

### Dorrik Sootpaw

**Role:** Young salvage worker and outspoken critic of surface discrimination.  
**Network:** Quiet Hearths, though his views create tension with more cautious members.  
**Public manner:** Blunt, angry at repeated indignities, protective of friends.  
**Immediate goal:** Organize a refusal to accept exploitative work and force merchants to negotiate openly.  
**Leverage:** Personal relationships among younger workers and knowledge of salvage labor.  
**Limits:** No armed following, no citywide mandate, and limited patience for negotiation.  

## 7. Relationship and political map

| Network | Shared concern | Likely allies | Internal tension |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cistern Keepers | Water, repairs, route safety | Quiet Hearths; selected surface maintainers | Negotiation vs. strict autonomy |
| Open Stall Petition | Market access, leases, fair trade | Reform-minded merchants; some workshops | Public campaign vs. incremental gains |
| Underway Couriers | Safe routes, trusted messages | Cistern Keepers; households | Controlled access vs. commercial expansion |
| Quiet Hearths | Family safety, mutual aid, consent | Cistern Keepers; cautious households | Protection vs. public pressure |

No network controls the others. A public ratfolk victory may be welcomed by some residents and feared by others if it increases exposure or retaliation.

## 8. Existing community priorities and political opportunities

The networks already have distinct goals and possible avenues for civic change:

- Traders seek regular leases, market access, and consistent treatment in inspections and contract enforcement.
- Community maintainers seek safe water, repair access, and protection for shared infrastructure.
- Couriers seek to preserve safe routes while considering carefully limited paid work for trusted clients.
- Mutual-aid households seek reliable support, consent in public campaigns, and protection from retaliation.
- Residents may pursue reform through petitions, negotiated agreements, commercial leverage, public action, mutual aid, or legal challenges. No single strategy represents the whole community.

Outside patrons or allies may offer resources, but residents judge them by actual conduct and outcomes. No allegiance to a noble house, political movement, or individual is guaranteed.

## 9. Knowledge boundaries

- Surface officials may know individual ratfolk traders, repairers, and couriers without understanding the networks behind them.
- Nix, Tikka, Serren, Miraq, Vessa, Pellit, and Dorrik do not share identical knowledge.
- No named NPC is assumed to know a player's private identity or history without a plausible source and an established in-play discovery.
- No named NPC is assumed to know a PC's complete family history.
- Sensitive passages, household locations, and private relationships remain GM-only until disclosed in play.
