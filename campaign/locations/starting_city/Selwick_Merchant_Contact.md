# Master Tavren Selwick and the Roadside Wagon

**Status:** ESTABLISHED NPC AND BUSINESS; WAGON/CREW/REWARD SCENARIO PROVISIONAL — 2026-11-09  
**Role:** Wealthy Tallowmere merchant, shipowner, and commercial patron  
**Business:** Selwick & Company  
**District:** Old Quays, Tallowmere

Master Tavren Selwick is a prosperous merchant whose wealth comes from dependable trade, several shipping interests, and a reputation for paying obligations. He is not a noble and holds no civic office, but his business is large enough that guild officers, shipmasters, warehouse owners, and local officials know his name.

## 1. Business and Standing

Selwick & Company trades in regional and imported goods, including finished cloth, household wares, lamp oil, metal fittings, and provisions. The firm leases warehouse space near the Old Quays and has interests in two coastal vessels. It is substantial by Tallowmere standards, though it is not one of the great merchant dynasties of the continent.

Tavren's commercial standing rests on three habits:
- He pays agreed sums on time.
- He expects accurate cargo accounts and does not tolerate casual theft by his own employees.
- He remembers people who protect his people and property, but he does not mistake one useful deed for proof of a person's entire character.

He is measured, observant, and more generous in private than in public. He prefers to reward competent action without turning every favor into a lifelong obligation.

## 2. The Wagon on the Road

The wagon the PCs may encounter during their approach to Tallowmere belongs to Selwick & Company. It is carrying a mixed consignment intended for the city's warehouses and customers: finished wool cloth, lamp oil in sealed containers, household metal fittings, and several smaller crates of trade goods.

The wagon is part of an ordinary commercial journey, not a secret mission. Its exact approach road should be selected to match the PCs' chosen route into Tallowmere and the final placement of the troll encounter.

### People travelling with it

- **Hobb Tern:** Senior carter, practical and cautious. He knows the cargo account and can identify what is missing or damaged.
- **Lysa Fen:** Assistant carter and teamster, young but experienced with the road. She is the most likely survivor to reach the PCs' attention if the troll attacks.
- **Two hired guards:** Their names and exact fates are not fixed until the encounter is prepared. Do not add unnecessary deaths simply to make the scene dramatic.

The wagon's crew are employees, not disposable props. Their reactions depend on what the PCs do, whether they are injured, and whether the PCs treat them decently.

## 3. The Merchant's Response

If the PCs kill or drive off the troll and help the surviving crew or cargo reach Tallowmere, Selwick will thank them personally or through his factor if he is temporarily away from the city.

His initial response is deliberately meaningful but not extravagant:

- **Payment:** 30 gp for protecting his people and preventing the loss of the consignment, assuming the wagon or a credible account of the incident reaches Tallowmere. If the PCs only bring proof of the troll's death and no crew or cargo is recovered, he may offer a smaller payment after verifying the report.
- **Hospitality:** A meal and one night's respectable lodging at the Blue Heron for the PCs and any surviving crew, paid by Selwick.
- **Written introduction:** A signed letter introducing the PCs to Meret Sorn, a Warehouse Guild factor, and recommending that they be treated as people who acted reliably in a dangerous situation.
- **Personal recognition:** Selwick will remember their names and the circumstances of the help. He will not automatically employ them, sponsor their business, guarantee them credit, or vouch for matters he has not witnessed.

The exact payment may be adjusted if the wagon is destroyed, the crew are harmed, cargo is lost, or the PCs choose not to escort or recover anything. The reward is for real assistance, not simply for claiming a kill.

## 4. If This Opening Option Is Selected

Selwick's recommendation gives the PCs a modest foothold in Tallowmere:
- Meret Sorn is more likely to grant them a brief meeting and hear a commercial proposal.
- The Warehouse Guild's clerks are less likely to dismiss them as unknown troublemakers when they present Selwick's letter.
- A few Old Quays merchants may have heard that Selwick's wagon was saved.
- The surviving crew can corroborate the PCs' actions.

This is limited social credibility, not fame. Some people will still be cautious, prejudiced, busy, or uninterested. The letter does not grant guild membership, legal privilege, automatic access to restricted warehouses, or universal trust.

Selwick judges people by their conduct and reliability. Ancestry prejudice may affect how other merchants respond to a newcomer, but Selwick does not treat one person as representative of an entire ancestry. No PC identity, ancestry, or private history is assumed to be known to him; disclosure and any resulting relationship belong to play.

## 5. Selwick's Limits and Future Use

Selwick is a useful contact, not a patron who solves problems for the PCs. He may later offer paid work, introductions, or information if their interests align. He has existing obligations and competing concerns, including cargo contracts, shipping schedules, warehouse costs, and the need to protect his reputation.

No connection between the proposed wagon encounter and any unconfirmed player-character backstory is established.

## GM Continuity

- The wagon, crew, cargo, route, reward, and introduction are provisional encounter details; use them only if the GM selects this opening.
- The wagon belongs to Selwick & Company.
- It is carrying ordinary commercial cargo to Tallowmere.
- Its route is selected to fit the PCs' actual approach.
- The encounter should include a fair chance for the PCs to help at least one crew member if they act promptly and effectively.
- The 30 gp payment assumes meaningful protection or recovery, not merely a claim.
- Selwick's letter provides a first commercial introduction, not instant citywide reputation.
- No character sheet abilities or alchemical items are assumed.
