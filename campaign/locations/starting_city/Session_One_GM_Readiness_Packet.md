**Status:** GM running packet. Encounter mechanics remain provisional until character sheets and companion build are available.

## 1. Session One Goals

1. Show the PCs working together as an established pair.
2. Give them a meaningful first challenge on the road to Tallowmere.
3. Let their decisions affect the survival of a merchant wagon crew and its cargo.
4. Give them a credible introduction through Master Tavren Selwick.
5. Establish Tallowmere as a living place with multiple directions to pursue.
6. End with the players free to choose what they do next.

The troll is an opening encounter, not the beginning of a mandatory quest chain. After arrival and an initial introduction, the campaign opens into sandbox play.

## 2. Party and Rules Check

Current concepts:
- Human vampire mesmerist, formerly the nine-year-old Crown Prince of the fallen dragon-riding dynasty; survived the destruction of Dragonstone and wants to rebuild his dynasty, wealth, and influence.
- Male ratfolk alchemist, raised by the vampire after being rescued as a child; his travelling-merchant parents were murdered by House Relland retainers approximately ten years ago. He wants to improve the standing of ratfolk.
- A companion NPC is expected, provisionally estimated around CR 8. The companion's exact identity, statistics, role, and equipment must be confirmed.

Before finalizing the encounter, check both PC sheets and the companion's actual statistics: level, hit points, AC, saves, attacks, feats, spells, discoveries, extracts, bombs, equipment, consumables, damage, control abilities, mobility, and defenses.

A standard Pathfinder 1e troll is CR 5. Its regeneration is overcome by fire or acid. It can be dangerous for two level-five PCs; a CR 8 ally substantially changes the group's capability, but CR values are not added together to calculate party level. Assess the actual group. Do not inflate statistics or add enemies merely to counter the companion. If necessary, choose a different creature or encounter before play. Use published rules and provide fair clues to regeneration and its vulnerability. Retreat, concealment, negotiation, or bypassing the creature remain valid choices.

## 3. Roadside Encounter

The PCs encounter a damaged wagon belonging to Selwick & Company, owned by Master Tavren Selwick of Tallowmere. A troll has attacked the wagon and its crew. Place it on a suitable approach road within roughly half a day's travel of the city, matching the PCs' actual route and established geography.

Cargo: finished wool cloth, sealed lamp oil, household metal fittings, and smaller crates of ordinary trade goods.

Crew:
- Hobb Tern, senior carter; practical, cautious, knows the cargo account.
- Lysa Fen, assistant carter and teamster; young but experienced, likely to be the first survivor encountered.
- Two hired guards; determine names and fate when staging the encounter. Do not kill them just to manufacture drama.

Clues may include a wounded animal whose injuries are closing, a survivor who saw the troll recover from severe wounds, tracks, or evidence of repeated feeding. Do not assume the alchemist has a particular formula or item before the sheet confirms it.

Outcomes should follow player choices and actual events. If they save the crew and wagon, Selwick pays the full reward. If cargo is damaged or lost, adjust payment proportionately and explain why. If they bring only proof of the troll's death, Selwick may verify the report and offer a smaller payment. There is no separate public bounty.

## 4. Reward and Introduction

For meaningful protection of his people or recovery of the consignment, Selwick offers:
- 30 gp.
- A meal and one night's lodging for the PCs and surviving crew at the Blue Heron.
- A signed letter introducing them to Meret Sorn, a Warehouse Guild factor, recommending that they be heard as people who acted reliably under danger.

This is a useful foothold, not instant fame. It grants no guild membership, credit, legal privilege, guaranteed employment, or universal trust. Selwick is assumed to be in Tallowmere and able to meet them later that day or the following morning. If unavailable, his factor can arrange payment, lodging, and delivery of the letter.

## 5. Arrival and First City Scene

Use the gate and approach matching the PCs' route. Do not force them through a particular gate. Watch officers may ask ordinary questions about purpose, cargo, weapons, and business when circumstances warrant. There is no blanket requirement to surrender weapons or pay an invented entry tax.

A damaged wagon and credible account of a troll attack attract practical interest, not instant celebrity. Ratfolk may face individual prejudice, but there is no universal citywide ban. The vampire's identity is not automatically known; the PCs decide what they disclose.

Useful destinations if asked:
- Blue Heron: respectable inn and meal, Old Quays.
- Reeve's Hall: petitions, records, local administration, notices.
- Harbor Office: ship declarations, berths, harbor procedures.
- Bellmarket and Market Ward: shops, guildhalls, moneychanging.
- Warehouse Guild: introduction to Meret Sorn through Selwick's letter.
- Lantern Ward: hospice, shrines, healers, burial grounds.
- Underways: ratfolk communities and routes; access depends on introductions and local knowledge.

## 7. City at a Glance

Population approximately 18,000. Tallowmere is a walled port on the northwestern shore of the central inland sea, at the mouth of the River Vale. House Denvor governs under a hereditary charter confirmed by House Kharadorn. Kharadorn retains strategic rights and harbor interests but does not administer ordinary city life.

Districts:
- Greywatch Rise: Greywatch, Denvor family, garrison, local authority.
- High Ward: wealthy homes, legal offices, established temples.
- Old Quays: historic harbor, shipwrights, chandlers, inns, warehouses.
- New Quays: expanded docks, bulk cargo, newer merchants.
- Market Ward: Bellmarket, guildhalls, shops, moneychangers, inns.
- Rivergate: bargemen, mills, laborers, river commerce.
- Lantern Ward: temples, hospice, healers, burial grounds.
- Underways: patchwork subterranean passages and decentralized ratfolk neighborhoods.

Principal streets include Harbor Road, Market Street, King's Road, West Road, North Road, Lantern Way, Greywatch Stair, Rise Road, Quay Lane, New Wharf Road, and River Road. Regional continuations include River Vale Road, Western Track, Quarry Road, Eastern Shore Road, and South Road. Exact map geometry remains provisional.

Walking times: one compact district 5–10 minutes; adjacent districts 5–15 minutes; cross-city 25–40 minutes. Carts are slower in the old city.

The Underways' initial informal networks are the Cistern Keepers, Open Stall Petition, Underway Couriers, and Quiet Hearths. They have distinct priorities and no single leader. See `Ratfolk_Community_Networks_and_NPCs.md` before improvising ratfolk political responses.

## 8. Active City Situation

These are independent pressures, not one central plot:
1. Harbor berth allocation dispute between established shipowners and smaller traders.
2. Warehouse fire precautions after small fires and near-misses; cause unknown.
3. Northfield harvest expected adequate but uneven.
4. Ratfolk traders seek regular market stalls and leases.
5. House Denvor household discusses succession, influence, and priorities without an open family crisis.
6. House Kharadorn reviews harbor defenses and charter obligations.
7. Skilled labor and merchant credit are under pressure; supply and debt disputes remain fragmented.

The PCs may engage with any of these, ignore all of them, or pursue unrelated goals.

## 9. Sandbox Opportunity Board

These are possibilities to discover, not mandatory quests.

**Commerce:** Rent a workshop, shop, stall, office, or warehouse; build relationships with Miraq, the Artificers' Hall, suppliers, and buyers; trade, repair, commission, transport goods; pursue alchemical production or Pathfinder-rule-compliant magic item crafting; negotiate contracts and hire workers.

**City and politics:** Learn the Denvor charter and Kharadorn oversight; engage with harbor allocation, warehouse safety, market access, or civic works; build relationships with officials, guilds, merchants, and nobles; seek property, recognition, a charter, or a formal role through play.

**Ratfolk community:** Meet different Underways households and representatives; pursue market access, safe passage, employment, and civic recognition; discover disagreements within the community. Ratfolk are not a unified political bloc.

**Exploration:** Visit Millhaven, Brackenholt, Stonehollow, Ashcombe Manor, Fordmere, Saltwick, and Harthbridge; explore Deep Bracken, Upper Vale, Greyback Heights, Siltfen, and the Outer Eastern Shore; investigate Rook's Watch, Alder Chapel, the Broken Mile, the Shepherd's Fold, and the Drowned Storehouse; gather resources subject to actual rights, access, season, and Pathfinder rules.

**Personal ambitions:** The vampire may conceal or reveal his identity, build wealth and influence, investigate Dragonstone, and pursue his dynastic goals. The ratfolk may develop his alchemical talent and pursue greater standing and safety for ratfolk. Neither arc has a predetermined solution or timetable.

## 10. World-State Rhythm

At campaign start, harbor negotiations continue, warehouse safety measures are debated, harvest expectations remain uncertain, ratfolk market access is unresolved, Kharadorn's defense review is underway, and merchant credit/supply disputes remain private and fragmented.

When several days pass, update negotiations, deliveries, stock, and NPC activity. Ignored problems progress plausibly, not automatically toward disaster. PC interventions can alter outcomes. Do not preserve a planned result against player action.

## 11. Practical GM Standards

- Published Pathfinder 1e rules govern mechanics; no hidden house rules.
- Shop inventories are finite and restock through actual deliveries.
- Published hireling and crafting rules remain the baseline.
- Local scarcity concerns access to knowledge, services, materials, suppliers, and buyers, not invented prerequisites.
- Ratfolk prejudice is common but not universal; individual reactions vary.
- Underways are not a universally safe shortcut.
- The Watch is neither omniscient nor universally corrupt.
- Nobles, guilds, and merchants are not monolithic.
- Do not reveal GM-only histories or NPC knowledge unless discovered.
- The companion follows the same world rules and should not decide the PCs' actions.
