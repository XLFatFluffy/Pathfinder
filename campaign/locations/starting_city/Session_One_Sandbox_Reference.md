# Tallowmere — Session One Sandbox Reference

**Snapshot:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC  
**Visibility:** GM-ONLY  
**Purpose:** Fast table-side reference for the first session after the opening encounter. This does not create a quest chain or force a route.  
**Status:** PROVISIONAL LEGACY PREPARATION. The party level, relationship, troll encounter, Selwick introduction, reward, and companion assumptions must be confirmed against the players' final concepts and the GM's chosen opening before use. The city sandbox and established local pressures remain useful independently of that opening.

## 1. Opening state

The legacy opening assumes two level-five newcomers approaching Tallowmere who already know and trust one another. It also assumes a troll attack on Selwick & Company's wagon. These are provisional character and encounter assumptions, not current requirements. Confirm party size, level, prior relationships, route, opening event, and any companion from the final character concepts before using this sequence.

The city sandbox, local institutions, active pressures, and NPC motivations can be used regardless of which opening the GM ultimately selects. After the party arrives, play remains open-ended.

## 2. Immediate consequences

Use the outcome rather than a predetermined result.

| Outcome | Immediate consequence |
|---|---|
| Crew and cargo largely saved | Selwick pays the full 30 gp reward, provides a meal and one night's lodging at the Blue Heron, and gives a signed introduction to Meret Sorn. |
| Crew saved, cargo damaged | Reward is adjusted proportionally; Selwick remains grateful for protecting people. |
| Some crew lost | Selwick's response depends on what the PCs did and what could reasonably have been saved. Do not punish the PCs for losses they could not prevent. |
| Troll killed but crew/cargo abandoned | Selwick may verify the kill and offer a smaller reward. |
| PCs bypass or retreat from the troll | No automatic penalty. The wagon's fate becomes part of the world state only after the actual outcome is known. |
| PCs use an unusual solution | Resolve it by the rules and circumstances; do not force a combat result. |

## 3. First-day destinations

### Blue Heron
Useful for rest, food, local conversation, and low-pressure introductions.

### Reeve's Hall
Useful for petitions, notices, records, local administration, and questions about civic procedure.

### Harbor Office
Useful for shipping declarations, berth procedures, harbor rules, and commercial disputes.

### Bellmarket
Useful for ordinary purchases, public notices, merchants, moneychanging, and observation of the ratfolk market-access issue.

### Warehouse Guild
The most direct institutional contact after Selwick's introduction. Meret Sorn can explain the commercial environment without becoming a quest dispenser.

### Lantern Ward
Useful for religious, charitable, healing, and burial questions.

### Underways
Not a single faction or automatically accessible dungeon. Entry depends on introductions, local relationships, and the specific neighborhood.

## 4. Opening rumors

The ten established Tallowmere rumors remain available through ordinary conversation and observation. Use the GM-only truth register to separate what is true, unresolved, misleading, or exaggerated.

Do not hand the players the entire rumor list. Let information travel through people and circumstances.

## 5. Active issues the PCs can encounter

The following pressures are already active at campaign start:

- Harbor berth allocation.
- Warehouse fire precautions.
- Uneven but currently adequate Northfield harvest.
- Ratfolk market access.
- Denvor household priorities.
- Kharadorn harbor-defense and charter review.
- Skilled labor, credit, and supply pressure.
- Longmead boundary dispute, which is distant and unlikely to appear unless the PCs pursue wider politics.

These are world conditions, not assignments.

## 6. Useful NPC motivations

- **Lord Oswin Denvor:** Preserve local stability and autonomy while satisfying legitimate charter obligations.
- **Ser Edwyn Denvor:** Favor stronger defense and infrastructure investment.
- **Lady Anwen Denvor:** Build useful civic and commercial relationships.
- **Lady Catrin Denvor:** Maintain social and noble relationships.
- **Reeve Marrec Holt:** Keep petitions and civic administration orderly.
- **Harbor Master Ysella Vane:** Keep harbor traffic moving under established rules.
- **Meret Sorn:** Protect warehouse commerce and reduce fire risk without destroying smaller operators.
- **Tikka Vell:** Seek regular market access for ratfolk traders.
- **Nix-of-the-Cistern:** Prioritize household safety and avoid exposing vulnerable ratfolk.
- **Miraq Ashwhisk:** Favor gradual commercial integration and dependable customers.
- **Vessa Reedtail:** Insist that market access not come at the cost of household consent or safety.
- **Master Tavren Selwick:** Reward reliable action and protect his trade business.

Use existing dossiers for full characterization. These quick motivations are not replacement canon.

## 7. Player-choice response rules

If the PCs:
- **Help a faction:** Improve that actor's disposition only as far as the actual assistance justifies.
- **Harm or expose an actor:** Record what the actor actually learns and what retaliation or legal response is realistically available.
- **Ignore an issue:** Do not immediately escalate it into disaster. Let actors continue their established work.
- **Investigate:** Give evidence appropriate to the location, witness, records, and skill checks. Do not reveal GM-only truth merely because the players ask.
- **Travel elsewhere:** Use established roads, settlements, and ecology. Do not invent a new settlement simply to fill empty map space.
- **Create a new relationship:** Add it to the actor register after the session.
- **Take over a holding or faction:** Recalculate its obligations, rivals, resources, and dependent actors rather than treating ownership as a cosmetic title.

## 8. No-railroading safeguards

There is no required sequence after the wagon encounter.

The GM may let the players:
- pursue commerce,
- explore the city,
- investigate rumors,
- seek work,
- build relationships,
- pursue personal goals,
- leave Tallowmere,
- ignore local politics,
- or create a problem of their own.

Tallowmere and the wider setting exist independently of the PCs. The Living World Engine v2 provides a GM-reviewed framework for deciding what established actors do as time passes, based on their motives, information, resources, obligations, and the PCs' actions. It does not run an autonomous simulation or mandatory escalation procedure. An ignored issue may remain stable, change quietly, or worsen; it does not automatically advance toward disaster.

## 9. Table tracking after each meaningful event

Record:
1. In-world date and approximate time.
2. Location.
3. PCs involved.
4. NPCs/factions involved.
5. What actually happened.
6. Information learned or revealed.
7. Promises, debts, injuries, losses, or gains.
8. Changes to relationships.
9. New issues or projects created.
10. Whether the change is public, player-known, or GM-only.

Do not record proposed outcomes as history.

## 10. Opening boundary

The campaign's current opening date is 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC. This is a recorded starting state, not the result of a required world turn. No automated world turn is run before or during play. Advance the calendar as in-world time passes; the GM determines which NPC and faction activities or consequences occur and records only what is actually established.
