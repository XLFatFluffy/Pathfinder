# Tallowmere: Shops, Stock, and Prices

**Status:** Initial named businesses and sample opening inventories established. Prices and stock are campaign-facing reference values, not a promise that every item is always available.

Tallowmere's shops are independent businesses. They do not share identical stock, prices, or service. A merchant's location, suppliers, quality, customer base, debts, and appetite for risk all affect what they carry and what they charge.

## How to Read the Inventories
- Prices are listed in gp, sp, and cp using Pathfinder's standard currency.
- Quantities describe a typical opening stock, not an infinite supply.
- Prices are the shop's normal asking prices before negotiation.
- Where an item has a standard Pathfinder price, that price is the baseline. A listed variation is an intentional local difference.
- Items not listed may sometimes be ordered, sourced, or found elsewhere.
- Availability changes through sales, deliveries, seasons, disruptions, and events.
- These are mundane businesses. They do not quietly stock rare magic items just because the PCs have money.

**Limitations:** Miraq does not sell unlimited quantities. Hazardous goods are stored separately, and large orders require notice. She may decline a sale if she believes the buyer intends to endanger the workshop or city. This is a business judgment, not a new crafting rule.

**Personality and relationships:** Miraq is observant, dry-humored, technically curious, and slow to trust people who treat her as a novelty or assume she represents all ratfolk. She has earned her position through skill, careful accounts, and stubborn persistence. She knows Tikka Vell through the local trading community and occasionally buys repaired glassware and small fittings from her. She has no automatic loyalty to the ratfolk PC. Their shared species and alchemical interests provide natural common ground, but friendship would develop through respect, honest dealing, and shared experience. Miraq has her own customers, obligations, and opinions about how ratfolk should pursue acceptance.

**Possible PC connection:** Miraq could become a professional contact, supplier, rival, confidante, or genuine friend to the ratfolk alchemist. The relationship is open and should emerge through play. She may be interested in comparing techniques, discussing reagent sourcing, or collaborating on a commission, but will not automatically hand over trade secrets or become a permanent sidekick.

## 8. Unlisted and Specialist Goods
No single shop sells everything. Armor, fine weapons, mounts, ships, livestock, poisons, rare spell components, unusual alchemical reagents, and magical items require specialist suppliers, commissions, or introductions.

A buyer may need to:
- Visit another district or nearby settlement.
- Wait for a shipment.
- Pay a deposit for a commission.
- Prove they can be trusted with a restricted or dangerous good.
- Find a broker who knows a suitable seller.

These are commercial circumstances, not blanket prohibitions. If the PCs have a legitimate way to obtain an item, adjudicate the transaction fairly.

## Inventory Changes
Shop stock changes as goods sell, deliveries arrive, seasons turn, and events disrupt trade. Do not restock every item automatically between visits. Record meaningful purchases and unusual orders. Common mundane goods can usually be replenished through normal trade; specialist goods may take days or weeks.

## Price Changes
A listed price may change when:
- A shipment is delayed or lost.
- Demand rises because of a local event.
- A merchant has excess stock or needs cash.
- The buyer is a reliable regular, a bulk purchaser, or a known risk.
- A competing seller opens or closes.
- Quality, provenance, or workmanship differs.

Do not apply arbitrary price changes to punish player success. State the asking price before purchase and keep negotiated prices consistent with the agreement.
