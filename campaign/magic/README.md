# Magic Records

These records define the campaign's treatment of arcane practice, wizardly learning, magic-item production, and the social scarcity of magic. Pathfinder First Edition remains the mechanical ruleset.

- [Arcane Arts and Wizardry](Arcane_Arts_and_Wizardry.md) — the rarity of arcane practitioners, Great House patronage, private apprenticeship, self-directed study, lost knowledge, and the distinction between the Collegium and wizard training.
- [Magic Items and Enchantment](Magic_Items_and_Enchantment.md) — the restricted magic-item market, sources of production, scarcity, acquisition, political consequences, and rules boundaries.

**Core rule:** Most commoners have never witnessed a spell. There are no magical academies. Most recognized wizards serve powerful patrons, especially Great Houses. Magic items are exceptionally scarce and expensive. These facts do not alter Pathfinder First Edition character, spellcasting, or crafting rules.
