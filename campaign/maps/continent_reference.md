# Authoritative Continent Atlas

**Status:** ESTABLISHED / AUTHORITATIVE MASTER MAP  
**Visibility:** GM_ONLY unless a player-safe derivative is prepared  
**Artwork:** continent_atlas.png  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-04

The GM has designated this atlas as the authoritative continental map. Its coastline, broad terrain layout, mountain systems, central inland sea, island chains, and visible river systems are the geographic reference for all subordinate maps. Do not redraw or relocate continental features to make a regional or city map fit.

The working continental extent is approximately **1,700 miles north to south** and **1,300 miles east to west at the widest point**. These are approved working dimensions, not surveyed measurements. The previous 0–1,000-mile scale bar is obsolete and must not be used for distance calculations. Map orientation is north up. Artwork dimensions are 1,161 × 1,355 pixels.

## Established geographic anchors

- Frozen northern wilderness and northern mountain/forest systems.
- Western highlands and forests.
- Central plains and river valleys.
- Large central inland sea.
- Northeastern mountain system, including Khar-Dum's broad region.
- Eastern forests and river delta.
- Southwestern arid region.
- Southern peninsulas and island chains.
- Dragonstone remains an island within the central inland sea, with its narrow natural land bridge. The dwarven underground route is GM-only.

## Tallowmere anchor

Tallowmere is established on the **northwestern shore of the central inland sea**, at a sheltered inlet where the River Vale reaches the sea. Its harbor opens east and southeast toward the inland sea; the River Vale approaches from the western uplands through the inhabited basin.

Provisional normalized map anchor on the full artwork canvas, measured from the upper-left corner: **X=44%, Y=43%**. This is a cartographic anchor for the city/harbor area, not a surveyed coordinate. The regional map must resolve the precise estuary, harbor outline, river bends, and surrounding settlements while preserving the continental relationship.

## Map authority and restrictions

Continent → Kingdom → Region → City → District / Site → Interior map. Every subordinate map must record its parent and anchor coordinates, preserve north orientation unless explicitly stated otherwise, match the parent coastline, river direction, terrain transitions, and relative location, and distinguish established features from provisional detail. Do not expose the master atlas or GM-only annotations to players by default. Do not infer exact political borders from terrain alone.
