# Continent Atlas Reference

**Status:** UNRESOLVED / PROVISIONAL REFERENCE  
**Visibility:** GM_ONLY until reviewed for player release  
**Source artwork:** [The Continent_ A Fantasy Atlas Map.png](../The%20Continent_%20A%20Fantasy%20Atlas%20Map.png)

## Purpose
This is the currently uploaded fantasy atlas image. It is a visual starting point for developing an original continent, not a finalized map.

## Review requirements
Before treating the map as canon:
- Replace borrowed or placeholder names with original names.
- Review coastlines, terrain, rivers, mountain ranges, climate, scale, and travel distances.
- Reconcile geography with the northern wilderness, Frozen Expanse, and Ancient Barrier.
- Establish political borders only after territories and political history are developed.
- Record map coordinates and parent/child map relationships for the interactive atlas.
- Decide which regions, labels, and markers are known to players.

## Restrictions
Do not expose the master atlas or GM-only annotations to players by default. Player maps should represent only information discovered, purchased, received, or deliberately revealed.
