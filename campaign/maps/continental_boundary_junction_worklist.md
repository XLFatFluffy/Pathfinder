# Continental Political Boundary Junction Worklist

**Snapshot:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC  
**Status:** GM planning aid; not canon boundary geometry  
**Authority:** Derived only from approved broad territorial placements, named regional divisions, the authoritative continental artwork, and the political authority register.

## Purpose

This worklist identifies where political boundary decisions will be required before any Great House polygons can be drawn. It does not establish borders, territorial adjacency, ownership, enclaves, or disputed claims.

A named region's placement does not prove that two houses share a border. A mountain range, river, road, watershed, or coast is a possible boundary reference only when the GM approves that use. Do not convert these notes into coordinates or polygons.

## Southern Reaches: preserve fragmentation

The following are geographic-cultural zones, not unified states:

- **Southwestern Marches:** arid highlands, dry valleys, plateaus, and oases.
- **Southern Principalities:** fertile lowlands, forests, rivers, small hereditary kingdoms, and independent cities.
- **Southern Islands:** island chains, sheltered harbors, fishing settlements, and maritime principalities.

Known independent authorities and local powers remain separate records. Do not draw a single southern sovereign polygon. In particular, the independent southern lordship, Brightwater Civic Compact, House Veylan, and Duskport Shipmasters' Compact must not be silently absorbed into a Great House territory.

## Boundary drafting sequence

When the GM is ready to settle boundaries:

1. Confirm which pairs of Great Houses actually share land borders.
2. Confirm whether any independent lordships, Crown vassals, chartered jurisdictions, or other lesser authorities intervene.
3. Select only canon-supported natural or jurisdictional anchors for each shared border.
4. Draft the six Great House polygons as non-overlapping provisional geometry.
5. Keep maritime influence separate from land sovereignty.
6. Preserve disputed or locally ambiguous jurisdictions as explicit exceptions, not accidental polygon overlaps.
7. Review the whole set against the authoritative artwork before any player-facing promotion.

## Current decision gate

**No new political border is established by this worklist.** The current canon supports broad territorial spheres and regional divisions, but it does not support exact border lines or all pairwise adjacencies. The next required GM decisions are the actual neighboring pairs and the boundary anchors that should govern each interface.
