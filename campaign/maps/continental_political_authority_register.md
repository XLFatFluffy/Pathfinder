# Continental Political Authority Register

**Snapshot:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC  
**Status:** Consolidated authority and holdings reference; exact continental borders remain unresolved.  
**Purpose:** Establish who exercises which kind of authority before any political territory polygon is drawn.

This register distinguishes sovereign or liege authority, direct holdings, vassal estates, chartered rights, clan obligations, maritime influence, and independent jurisdictions. A relationship in this register does not automatically establish a continuous polygon around every associated location.

## 4. Rights that must not be mistaken for territorial sovereignty

- **Kharadorn Central Inland Sea dominion:** maritime sphere. It does not mean every shoreline settlement is directly administered by Kharadorn.
- **Merrowen maritime reach:** naval and commercial reach. It does not imply sovereignty over every eastern port.
- **Fordmere:** a crossing charter, not Denvor ownership and not proof of a large Merewick territory.
- **Stonehollow:** quarry extraction rights held by local families under charter, not a new noble house or exclusive sovereignty over all surrounding land.
- **Brackenholt and Pineward woodland use:** customary rights must remain distinct from exclusive ownership.
- **Saltwick:** shore-charter authority exists, but superior jurisdiction is unresolved.
- **Rookwell:** direct Crown vassalage and road-service rights do not establish a continuous Crown demesne polygon around the estate.
- **Duskport Shipmasters' Compact:** port association and open-port privileges do not create island-wide sovereignty.
- **Clan Emberdelve:** clan membership within the Kharadorn imperial union does not make it a separate Great House or a surface territorial power.

## 5. Political boundary drafting rules

1. Keep the six Great House polygons distinct and non-overlapping unless the GM later establishes a dated dispute, occupation, or war.
2. Do not draw borders solely by connecting Great House seats or filling all land around a seat.
3. Use natural barriers and established local jurisdictions only where the canon supports them; a river, ridge, or road is not automatically a border.
4. Keep minor-house holdings as separate regional records until their extents are known. Do not imply that every vassal holding is a neat contiguous polygon.
5. Do not resolve the Longmead dispute through map geometry. Its precise location and boundaries remain deliberately unmapped.
6. Keep the Southern Principalities fragmented. The Southwestern Marches, Southern Principalities, and Southern Islands are geographic zones, not single political states.
7. Keep Kharadorn's inland-sea maritime dominion and Merrowen's maritime reach on water-only overlays, separate from land sovereignty.
8. Any proposed Great House boundary line must be labelled a GM proposal, identify the supporting canon, and remain hidden from players until approved.
