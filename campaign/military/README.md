# Military Records

This folder contains GM references for the armed institutions, military obligations, and operational realities of the setting. It distinguishes established canon from working portrayal guidance and deliberately leaves unsupported force totals and readiness figures undefined.

## Current references

- [Continental Military Institutions and Operational Doctrine](Continental_Military_Institutions_and_Operational_Doctrine.md) — Crown and Great House forces, military categories, command authority, vassal obligations, senior commanders, logistics, and the opening military baseline.\n- [Soldier Life and Military Service](Soldier_Life_and_Military_Service.md) — recruitment, training, garrison routines, equipment, food, service obligations, discipline, campaigning, wounds, veterans, and the human realities of armed service.

## Record standards

- Keep household guards, professional forces, garrisons, vassal contingents, levies, watches, clan forces, naval forces, and auxiliary support distinct.
- Command authority depends on law, charter, custom, oath, or an explicit GM decision.
- Readiness is not mobilization, and commercial shipping is not automatically a war fleet.
- Do not invent troop totals, fleet sizes, budgets, casualty figures, or readiness percentages.
- A military record does not imply that a force is currently deployed or that war is imminent.
- Preserve the distinction between established canon and working GM guidance.
