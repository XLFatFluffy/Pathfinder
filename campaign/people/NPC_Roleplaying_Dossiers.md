# NPC Roleplaying Dossier Collection

**Snapshot:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC  
**Visibility:** GM ONLY  
**Purpose:** A table-facing roleplaying reference for established named NPCs. These are people to portray, not quest dispensers.

> **Canon discipline:** This collection consolidates established NPCs from the campaign records and adds practical portrayal guidance. It does not silently create new history, relationships, crimes, powers, or secrets. Fields marked **Portrayal recommendation** are GM tools, not immutable canon. Alignment is a suggested Pathfinder 1e roleplaying shorthand, not a substitute for personality and not a moral verdict. Change it when play establishes a more accurate characterization. **Unspecified** means the repository has not established enough to make a responsible call.

## How to use a dossier at the table

For each NPC, prioritize:
1. **What they want now:** their immediate concern, not an abstract life ambition.
2. **What they want long-term:** the condition they are trying to create or preserve.
3. **What they fear or avoid:** the cost they are trying not to pay.
4. **How they act:** their default social approach and pressure response.
5. **How to play their voice:** rhythm, word choice, and a sample line. Do not imitate an accent unless useful and respectful.
6. **What changes their mind:** evidence, leverage, trust, risk, or a concrete offer.
7. **What they know:** only information available through their role and credible sources.

**Provisional legacy dossiers:** Lady Mereth Relland and Ser Corren Relland are tied to the unconfirmed House Relland PC incident; Hobb Tern and Lysa Fen are tied to the provisional Selwick wagon opening. Their specific incident knowledge, opening-scene roles, and related motives are not current campaign facts until the relevant PC concepts and encounter are confirmed.

A suggested alignment should never force an NPC to behave irrationally. Lawful does not mean kind; chaotic does not mean foolish; evil does not mean constantly cruel. Most NPCs are primarily concerned with their own obligations, livelihood, relationships, safety, pride, and ambitions.

# I. Tallowmere: ruling household and civic authority

## Lord Oswin Denvor
**Role:** Lord of Tallowmere; head of House Denvor.  
**Ancestry:** Human. **Suggested alignment:** Lawful Neutral.  
**Established core:** Measured, observant, cautious, committed to the hereditary charter and the city's security. Prefers negotiated solutions and predictable obligations.

- **Wants now:** Keep the city functioning, meet obligations to Kharadorn, and avoid a local dispute becoming a crisis that damages trade or Denvor authority.
- **Wants long-term:** Leave Tallowmere more secure and prosperous than he inherited it; preserve his family's legitimacy without surrendering ordinary civic autonomy.
- **Fears / avoids:** A costly defense failure, public disorder, a breach of charter, or a decision that forces the city into dependence on a greater power.
- **Contradiction:** His caution protects stability but can become an excuse to delay action until the available choices are worse.
- **Social method:** Listens longer than people expect. Asks practical questions. Rarely makes a promise in public before knowing what it will cost.
- **Pressure response:** Becomes more formal and asks for records, witnesses, and advice. Anger makes him quieter, not louder.
- **Voice cue:** Calm, economical sentences; speaks of duties, consequences, and what can actually be maintained.
- **Sample line:** “A promise is easy to make in this room. I am asking who will still be able to keep it when winter comes.”
- **Can be moved by:** Credible evidence, a plan with costs and responsibilities stated plainly, proof that delay is more dangerous than action.
- **Do not play as:** A secret tyrant, a fool, or a patron who instantly adopts the PCs. No open succession crisis is established.

## Lady Catrin Denvor
**Role:** Lady of House Denvor; civic and noble relationship-builder.  
**Ancestry:** Human. **Suggested alignment:** Lawful Good (provisional).  
**Established core:** Politically capable; maintains ties with charitable institutions, established families, and visiting dignitaries. Less patient than Oswin with officials who mistake courtesy for weakness.

- **Wants now:** Ensure the household is treated with respect and that the city's civic and charitable relationships remain useful rather than ceremonial.
- **Wants long-term:** Preserve Denvor influence through personal trust, reputation, and competent relationships.
- **Fears / avoids:** Being politely ignored, having charitable work used as decoration, and allowing small humiliations to become accepted custom.
- **Contradiction:** Her courtesy is genuine, but it is also a tool. People who mistake it for softness may discover she has already counted the room's loyalties.
- **Social method:** Warm welcome, precise questions, remembers names and family details. Offers help in ways that create reciprocal obligations without making them crude.
- **Pressure response:** Drops the social padding and states exactly what she expects.
- **Voice cue:** Polished, composed, with pointed questions hidden inside gracious phrasing.
- **Sample line:** “You have explained why it is difficult. Now tell me why it should remain so.”
- **Can be moved by:** Demonstrated competence, care for vulnerable residents, discretion, and people who keep their word.
- **Do not play as:** Merely the lord's wife or a default court schemer. Her independent priorities are real; specific secret ambitions are not established.

## Ser Edwyn Denvor
**Role:** Heir to Tallowmere.  
**Ancestry:** Human. **Suggested alignment:** Lawful Neutral.  
**Established core:** Mid-twenties; has inspected harbor, garrison, and estates. Favors investment in defenses and infrastructure; more willing than Oswin to spend money and test boundaries.

- **Wants now:** Make the city and Greywatch better prepared before a weakness becomes an emergency.
- **Wants long-term:** Become a lord whose authority rests on visible competence, not only inherited rights.
- **Fears / avoids:** Inheriting a city whose defenses are neglected and whose options have been spent through excessive caution.
- **Contradiction:** He sees the cost of inaction clearly but can underestimate the political and commercial cost of acting too quickly.
- **Social method:** Direct, practical, impatient with ceremony that delays a decision. Respects people who understand logistics.
- **Pressure response:** Pushes for a decision and may speak too sharply; later can recognize a sound objection.
- **Voice cue:** Concrete nouns, short proposals, questions about materials, staffing, time, and readiness.
- **Sample line:** “If the wall fails, no one will care that the accounts were balanced.”
- **Can be moved by:** A better practical plan, proof of hidden costs, and people who can distinguish readiness from vanity.
- **Do not play as:** A warmonger or an inevitable rival to his father. Their disagreement is policy, not a succession crisis.

## Lady Anwen Denvor
**Role:** Younger child of Oswin and Catrin; informal civic and commercial influence.  
**Ancestry:** Human. **Suggested alignment:** Neutral Good (provisional).  
**Established core:** Perceptive, socially skilled, interested in commercial and civic networks; has genuine relationships outside the nobility. Extent of her ambitions is not publicly understood.

- **Wants now:** Understand what is changing in the city and connect capable people who would not normally meet.
- **Wants long-term:** Have influence that comes from knowing how Tallowmere works, not simply from being born a Denvor.
- **Fears / avoids:** Being confined to household ceremony or being used as a decorative intermediary.
- **Contradiction:** She values relationships beyond noble circles but cannot pretend her family name carries no weight.
- **Social method:** Curious, observant, remembers small details, gives people room to talk. Often asks a question that sounds casual but is not.
- **Pressure response:** Becomes more guarded and tests whether the other person understands the consequences for ordinary residents.
- **Voice cue:** Light conversational openings followed by unusually perceptive follow-ups.
- **Sample line:** “Who benefits if this arrangement stays exactly as it is?”
- **Can be moved by:** Honest information, practical reform, and people who treat non-nobles as participants rather than scenery.
- **Do not play as:** A confirmed rebel, spy, or secret claimant. Her ambitions remain undefined.

## Reeve Marrec Holt
**Role:** Town Reeve; civic records, routine administration, dues, maintenance.  
**Ancestry:** Human. **Suggested alignment:** Lawful Neutral.

- **Wants now:** Keep the records accurate, petitions moving, and routine obligations from becoming disputes.
- **Wants long-term:** Be regarded as dependable by both the Denvors and the people who must live under the resulting decisions.
- **Fears / avoids:** A clerical mistake becoming a political scandal; being forced to certify something he cannot verify.
- **Contradiction:** He believes routine administration protects everyone, yet routine can also preserve unfair arrangements.
- **Social method:** Patient, procedural, asks for dates, names, copies, and witnesses. Not easily impressed by grand claims.
- **Pressure response:** Retreats to process and asks for time to check the record.
- **Voice cue:** Precise, dry, slightly overburdened.
- **Sample line:** “I can enter what you have shown me. I cannot enter what you merely suspect.”
- **Can be moved by:** Complete documentation, clear responsibility, and a request that does not demand he falsify a record.
- **Do not play as:** A universal gatekeeper or secret corrupt official.

## Harbor Master Ysella Vane
**Role:** Harbor Master; berth allocation and harbor procedures.  
**Ancestry:** Human. **Suggested alignment:** Lawful Neutral.

- **Wants now:** Safe traffic, fair quay allocation, reliable fees, and a harbor that keeps its reputation.
- **Wants long-term:** Keep the port working without allowing the loudest shipowners to capture its rules.
- **Fears / avoids:** Manufactured delays, unsafe vessels, favoritism that causes an accident, and blame for decisions made by others.
- **Contradiction:** She wants fair allocation but must work with shipowners who have more money and influence than smaller traders.
- **Social method:** Direct and experienced; distinguishes genuine problems from excuses quickly.
- **Pressure response:** Becomes blunt, cites harbor rules, and refuses to negotiate while a safety issue remains unresolved.
- **Voice cue:** Crisp, practical, little patience for flattery.
- **Sample line:** “The tide does not care whose seal is on the cargo.”
- **Can be moved by:** Evidence, a workable schedule, and people who understand that safety and throughput are linked.
- **Do not play as:** A smuggler or a servant of the Harbor Fellowship. Neither is established.

## Chief Magistrate Odran Pell
**Role:** Chief Magistrate; civil and criminal hearings.  
**Ancestry:** Half-elf. **Suggested alignment:** Lawful Neutral.

- **Wants now:** Hear cases that can survive scrutiny and keep procedure consistent.
- **Wants long-term:** Preserve public confidence that a dispute can be judged without wealth alone deciding the result.
- **Fears / avoids:** Weak testimony, political pressure, and a ruling that looks decisive but cannot be defended.
- **Contradiction:** His care for procedure can protect the vulnerable, but delay is easier for wealthy litigants to endure.
- **Social method:** Patient, observant, asks the same question in different ways to test consistency.
- **Pressure response:** Narrows the discussion to evidence and jurisdiction.
- **Voice cue:** Measured, exact, never wastes a question.
- **Sample line:** “Tell me what you saw. Leave what you think it means for later.”
- **Can be moved by:** Corroborated evidence, reliable witnesses, and lawful process.
- **Do not play as:** A detective who knows every crime or a judge who can ignore evidentiary limits.

## Captain Brinna Salk
**Role:** Captain of the Watch.  
**Ancestry:** Human. **Suggested alignment:** Lawful Neutral.

- **Wants now:** Improve patrol coverage, discipline, staffing, and response to preventable violence.
- **Wants long-term:** Keep the Watch credible despite being asked to cover more streets than its personnel can comfortably manage.
- **Fears / avoids:** A patrol injured because of bad orders, a preventable incident, and merchants buying special treatment.
- **Contradiction:** She wants consistent enforcement but knows the Watch must prioritize when it lacks people.
- **Social method:** Blunt, practical, judges people by conduct under pressure.
- **Pressure response:** Takes control of immediate safety, then asks questions.
- **Voice cue:** Short commands and plain assessments.
- **Sample line:** “You can argue about blame after everyone is out of the street.”
- **Can be moved by:** Useful witnesses, credible information, and offers that improve public safety without demanding private favors.
- **Do not play as:** Universally corrupt or universally available as the PCs' personal guards.

## Castellan Hadrik Voss
**Role:** Castellan of Greywatch.  
**Ancestry:** Dwarf. **Suggested alignment:** Lawful Neutral.

- **Wants now:** Keep walls, stores, armories, and command routines ready.
- **Wants long-term:** Ensure Greywatch can perform its defensive purpose without depending on last-minute improvisation.
- **Fears / avoids:** Empty stores, unclear orders, neglected maintenance, and political promises unsupported by supplies.
- **Contradiction:** Professional discipline makes him reliable but can make him resistant to unconventional solutions.
- **Social method:** Formal, restrained, respects competence over status.
- **Pressure response:** Checks readiness, assigns responsibilities, and refuses vague assurances.
- **Voice cue:** Clipped statements, often framed as requirements.
- **Sample line:** “A wall is not defended by speeches. It is defended by men, stone, food, and time.”
- **Can be moved by:** Evidence of a real vulnerability and a funded, executable remedy.
- **Do not play as:** Automatically loyal to every Denvor opinion; he serves under a long-standing contract.

# II. House Denvor's regional peers and local noble actors

## Lady Ysabet Avenholt
**Role:** Head of House Avenholt; Denvor vassal at Ashcombe Hall.  
**Suggested alignment:** Lawful Neutral. **Established core:** Cautious with expenditure; protects estate and standing.

- **Wants:** Preserve the estate, tenant productivity, and reliable relations with Greywatch.
- **Fears:** Costly improvements that create debt or obligations the estate cannot sustain.
- **Method:** Careful negotiation; asks who pays, who maintains, and who benefits.
- **Pressure:** Becomes more conservative and insists on written terms.
- **Voice:** Reserved, practical, courteous without warmth being assumed.
- **Sample:** “An improvement that cannot be maintained is only a more expensive problem.”
- **Move her with:** Credible costings, tenant evidence, and a plan that protects the estate's future.
- **Avoid:** Portraying her as cowardly or opposed to all change.

## Ser Corvin Avenholt
**Role:** Heir to House Avenholt.  
**Suggested alignment:** Lawful Neutral. **Established core:** Wants better defenses and higher family standing.

- **Wants:** Make the family more visible and respected; improve local security.
- **Fears:** Being remembered as the heir who inherited a small estate and did nothing with it.
- **Method:** Ambitious, status-conscious, eager to demonstrate capability.
- **Pressure:** May commit too quickly to visible projects.
- **Voice:** Confident, speaks of opportunity and reputation.
- **Sample:** “If we wait until every neighbor approves, we shall still be waiting when the road is taken.”
- **Move him with:** A chance to prove competence without humiliating his mother.
- **Avoid:** Treating ambition as proof of treachery.

## Lord Beric Velleran
**Role:** Lord of Harthbridge; holder of market and toll privileges.  
**Suggested alignment:** Lawful Neutral. **Established core:** Cautious, protects the town's existing position.

- **Wants:** Preserve Harthbridge's market, toll income, and regional standing.
- **Fears:** Losing traffic to Tallowmere or a new route without compensation.
- **Method:** Courteous bargaining, protects precedent, rarely gives away a privilege casually.
- **Pressure:** Becomes formal and invokes charter rights.
- **Voice:** Measured, commercial, uses the language of custom and mutual obligation.
- **Sample:** “Trade follows convenience. A lord must make certain convenience remembers his town.”
- **Move him with:** A deal that preserves Harthbridge's relevance and gives him something measurable.
- **Avoid:** Making him an established enemy of House Denvor.

## Lady Maud Velleran
**Role:** Heir to House Velleran; manages much of Harthbridge's commercial correspondence.  
**Suggested alignment:** Neutral. **Established core:** More commercially ambitious than Beric.

- **Wants:** Expand Harthbridge's commercial influence and make its dealings more adaptable.
- **Fears:** Her father's caution allowing rivals to define the region's trade.
- **Method:** Quick, commercially literate, tests a proposal for hidden costs.
- **Pressure:** Seeks alternative partners rather than arguing endlessly.
- **Voice:** Brisk, precise, often asks for figures.
- **Sample:** “I understand the custom. What I want to know is whether it still earns its keep.”
- **Move her with:** Evidence of a new market or a practical partnership.
- **Avoid:** Making her reckless or automatically hostile to Tallowmere.

## Sir Davren Merewick
**Role:** Holder of Fordmere crossing, ferry, toll office, inn, and market yard.  
**Suggested alignment:** Lawful Neutral. **Established core:** Protects the crossing charter and its profitability.

- **Wants:** Keep the crossing reliable, profitable, and indispensable to traffic.
- **Fears:** A competing bridge, poor maintenance, flood damage, or a toll dispute that drives traffic elsewhere.
- **Method:** Transactional hospitality; negotiates with travelers and merchants as recurring customers.
- **Pressure:** Defends charter rights and insists on the cost of maintenance.
- **Voice:** Practical, slightly defensive about tolls.
- **Sample:** “The river is free. The ferry, rope, timber, and men who keep it working are not.”
- **Move him with:** Reliable traffic, repair support, or a route agreement.
- **Avoid:** Inventing a bridge project or feud.

## Lady Mereth Relland
**Role:** Head of House Relland; minor house under Velleran.  
**Suggested alignment:** Lawful Neutral (provisional). **Established core:** Preserves family standing; knows retainers were privately accused and quietly removed after an incident, but not the full truth.

- **Wants:** Protect the family name, maintain estate income, and avoid old misconduct becoming a public scandal.
- **Fears:** A credible inquiry revealing that her household failed to control its retainers and concealed wrongdoing.
- **Method:** Formal, cautious, protects the distinction between accusation and proof.
- **Pressure:** Seeks counsel, limits statements, and may demand evidence before acting.
- **Voice:** Controlled, dignified, careful with names.
- **Sample:** “I will not condemn a household on rumor. Nor will I ask you to forget evidence.”
- **Move her with:** Credible testimony, documentary proof, and a path to accountability that does not presume guilt beyond evidence.
- **Important secret boundary:** She did not personally order the murders as established canon. Do not make her omniscient about the ratfolk PC's survival.
- **Avoid:** Making the whole house uniformly guilty or innocent.

## Ser Corren Relland
**Role:** Heir to House Relland.  
**Suggested alignment:** Neutral. **Established core:** Wants to restore estate income and reputation; knows only that several retainers were dismissed after an incident.

- **Wants:** Repair the house's standing and financial position.
- **Fears:** Inheriting an estate weakened by old decisions he does not fully understand.
- **Method:** Practical, somewhat defensive, wants to be seen as capable rather than sheltered.
- **Pressure:** May protect the household before he knows what he is protecting.
- **Voice:** Earnest, formal, occasionally overexplains.
- **Sample:** “I cannot answer for every man who wore our colors. I can answer for what I choose to do now.”
- **Move him with:** Evidence, a chance to demonstrate responsibility, and a credible alternative to reflexive denial.
- **Secret boundary:** His knowledge of the old road incident is incomplete; he does not know the ratfolk child survived.

## Vardek Flintmark
**Role:** Kharadorn Charter Warden in Tallowmere.  
**Ancestry:** Dwarf. **Suggested alignment:** Lawful Neutral.  
**Established core:** Conducts legitimate review of harbor defenses, tribute accounts, and charter obligations.

- **Wants now:** Complete an accurate review and report whether the Denvors are meeting the charter's terms.
- **Wants long-term:** Preserve Kharadorn's strategic rights and ensure the charter remains enforceable.
- **Fears:** A neglected harbor becoming a strategic liability; local officials treating oversight as optional.
- **Method:** Formal inspection, precise questions, distinguishes written obligation from local custom.
- **Pressure:** Documents noncompliance rather than making theatrical threats.
- **Voice:** Exact, official, unhurried.
- **Sample:** “I am not here to invent an obligation. I am here to determine whether the one already sworn is being met.”
- **Move him with:** Accurate records, visible readiness, and reasoned interpretation of the charter.
- **Avoid:** Playing him as an occupying commander. No occupation plan or expanded demand is established.

## Belda Ironmark
**Role:** Clerk supporting Vardek's charter review.  
**Ancestry:** Dwarf. **Suggested alignment:** Lawful Neutral.

- **Wants:** Keep the review's figures, inventories, and correspondence internally consistent.
- **Fears:** Being made responsible for an inaccurate report or pressured to omit a discrepancy.
- **Method:** Quietly observant, organized, notices missing entries and inconsistent dates.
- **Pressure:** Becomes meticulous and asks for copies.
- **Voice:** Concise, factual, rarely comments on politics.
- **Sample:** “The total is correct. The supporting entries are not.”
- **Move her with:** Records and explanations that can be independently checked.
- **Avoid:** Giving her authority or political agenda beyond her clerical role.

# III. Tallowmere's merchants, guilds, and working people

## Master Tavren Selwick
**Role:** Wealthy merchant, shipowner, head of Selwick & Company.  
**Suggested alignment:** Neutral Good. **Established core:** Measured, observant, more generous in private than in public; pays obligations and expects accurate accounts.

- **Wants now:** Protect his crew, cargo, reputation, and commercial relationships.
- **Wants long-term:** Keep the firm dependable and profitable without becoming dependent on a single patron.
- **Fears:** A preventable loss, dishonest employees, and being drawn into obligations he cannot honor.
- **Method:** Rewards competence quietly; remembers favors but does not confuse one good deed with lifelong loyalty.
- **Pressure:** Verifies facts before committing money or reputation.
- **Voice:** Calm, understated, respectful but not effusive.
- **Sample:** “You helped my people. I will see that you are paid. What comes after that depends on what we each choose.”
- **Move him with:** Reliability, clear terms, and protection of his employees.
- **Avoid:** Treating him as an unlimited patron or connecting him secretly to House Relland or the ratfolk tragedy.
- **Opening consequence:** Meaningful protection of the wagon crew/cargo earns 30 gp, a meal and one night's Blue Heron lodging, and a written introduction to Meret Sorn.

## Hobb Tern
**Role:** Senior carter for Selwick & Company.  
**Suggested alignment:** Neutral. **Established core:** Practical and cautious; knows the cargo account.

- **Wants:** Get the wagon, cargo, and crew to Tallowmere with as little loss as possible.
- **Fears:** Being blamed for damage he could not prevent.
- **Method:** Checks loads, animals, straps, and figures; trusts demonstrated competence.
- **Pressure:** Focuses on immediate survival and preserving the cargo record.
- **Voice:** Plain, workmanlike, little interest in grand explanations.
- **Sample:** “Count it before you move it. If we cannot account for it, someone will say I stole it.”
- **Move him with:** Practical help and honesty about losses.
- **Avoid:** Making him a secret agent or expendable prop.

## Lysa Fen
**Role:** Assistant carter and teamster for Selwick & Company.  
**Suggested alignment:** Neutral Good (provisional). **Established core:** Young but experienced with the road; likely to be the first survivor to reach the PCs' attention.

- **Wants:** Survive, protect the team, and prove she can do the job.
- **Fears:** Freezing under pressure or being treated as incompetent because she is young.
- **Method:** Alert, quick to act, may speak before she has fully thought through a danger.
- **Pressure:** Looks for a practical escape or someone who can take charge.
- **Voice:** Direct, breathless under stress, apologizes less once safe.
- **Sample:** “The thing got back up. I saw it. Don't tell me it was dead.”
- **Move her with:** Clear instructions and being taken seriously.
- **Avoid:** Assuming her exact injuries or survival before the encounter is staged.

## Meret Sorn
**Role:** Warehouse Guild factor.  
**Suggested alignment:** Lawful Neutral. **Established core:** Polished, cautious, remembers obligations; values predictable trade, secure cargo, fire prevention, reliable records.

- **Wants now:** Protect the Guild's credibility and keep warehouse risks manageable.
- **Wants long-term:** Preserve the Guild's role in cargo security and commercial coordination.
- **Fears:** Fire, bad records, costly improvements, and a reform that shifts costs onto members without agreement.
- **Method:** Courteous, exact, keeps track of who owes whom.
- **Pressure:** Requests documentation and narrows commitments.
- **Voice:** Smooth, restrained, commercially literate.
- **Sample:** “A good recommendation earns you a meeting. What you say in that meeting earns the next one.”
- **Move her with:** Selwick's letter, accurate accounts, practical safety proposals.
- **Avoid:** Making her duplicate Harlan Tebb or a criminal conspirator. Existing references to Harlan and Meret should not be duplicated.

## Dagna Coppervein
**Role:** Master of the Shipwrights' Brotherhood.  
**Ancestry:** Dwarf. **Suggested alignment:** Lawful Neutral.

- **Wants:** Protect apprentices, skilled labor, quality standards, and timber supply.
- **Fears:** Rushed work causing failure and employers treating skilled labor as interchangeable.
- **Method:** Demanding, technically exact, respects craft competence.
- **Pressure:** Refuses deadlines that cannot be met safely.
- **Voice:** Blunt craft language; corrects inaccurate terminology.
- **Sample:** “You can have it quickly, cheaply, or properly. Choose which promise you want me to keep.”
- **Move her with:** Fair schedules, sound materials, and respect for apprentices.
- **Avoid:** Making her stubborn for its own sake.

## Pellan Reed
**Role:** Organizer of the Bargemen's Company.  
**Ancestry:** Halfling. **Suggested alignment:** Neutral Good.

- **Wants:** Fair river tolls, safe landings, and recognition for river workers.
- **Fears:** Sea merchants setting policy for a city that depends on inland grain.
- **Method:** Sociable, politically sharp, uses humor and practical examples to build support.
- **Pressure:** Organizes workers and makes the cost of ignoring them visible.
- **Voice:** Conversational, quick, good at turning a complaint into a memorable comparison.
- **Sample:** “The grandest ship in the harbor still eats bread brought downriver.”
- **Move him with:** Respect for river traffic and concrete improvements to landing safety.
- **Avoid:** Treating him as a comic sidekick or leader of all labor.

## Ilyne Marris
**Role:** Independent moneychanger and broker; The Brass Scale.  
**Suggested alignment:** Lawful Neutral. **Established core:** Careful, discreet, scrupulous about written agreements; serves merchants who cannot be tied to one guild.

- **Wants now:** Protect her reputation and resolve an overdue secured loan without destroying a useful commercial relationship.
- **Wants long-term:** Remain independent and trusted by multiple commercial interests.
- **Fears:** Becoming dependent on one patron, guaranteeing another person's honesty, or being pulled into coercive debt collection.
- **Method:** Analytical, discreet, asks what is being offered as security.
- **Pressure:** Becomes more formal and insists on written terms.
- **Voice:** Precise, calm, never promises more than she controls.
- **Sample:** “I can arrange an introduction. I cannot arrange the other person's good character.”
- **Move her with:** Collateral, transparent terms, and a credible repayment plan.
- **Avoid:** Treating her as a bank with unlimited funds or as an agent of Hessa Quill.

## Beren Harlowe
**Role:** Proprietor of Harlowe's General Stores.  
**Suggested alignment:** Neutral Good (provisional).

- **Wants:** Keep ordinary goods moving at fair prices and preserve enough capital to restock.
- **Fears:** A wholesaler tightening terms until his shop cannot operate.
- **Method:** Practical, careful with money, more generous with familiar customers than strangers.
- **Pressure:** Will not sell below replacement cost.
- **Voice:** Plain, quietly wry, knows the value of every shelf.
- **Sample:** “I can sell you what is here. I cannot sell you what the river has not delivered.”
- **Move him with:** Prompt payment, repeat business, and reasonable orders.
- **Avoid:** Treating his shop as an inexhaustible warehouse.

## Mara Venn
**Role:** Proprietor of The Salted Tack; former caravan quartermaster.  
**Suggested alignment:** Lawful Neutral.

- **Wants:** Reliable travel equipment and suppliers who meet stated quality.
- **Fears:** Defective gear causing injury and her reputation taking the blame.
- **Method:** Blunt, tests equipment, keeps repair records.
- **Pressure:** Withdraws questionable stock and confronts suppliers with evidence.
- **Voice:** Direct, practical, occasionally dry.
- **Sample:** “If you intend to trust your weight to it, I intend to test it first.”
- **Move her with:** Evidence of supplier fault and reliable replacement stock.
- **Avoid:** Mistaking bluntness for hostility.

## Orsik Feld
**Role:** Smith; Orsik Ironworks.  
**Ancestry:** Dwarf. **Suggested alignment:** Lawful Neutral.

- **Wants:** Maintain quality and repeat contracts while securing reliable charcoal.
- **Fears:** Custom work becoming unprofitable or apprentices being pushed beyond competence.
- **Method:** Unsenti­mental, values consistency over showmanship.
- **Pressure:** Raises prices rather than quietly lowering quality.
- **Voice:** Few words, precise judgments.
- **Sample:** “The metal will do what the work allows. It will not do what you wish.”
- **Move him with:** Reliable fuel, clear specifications, fair payment.
- **Avoid:** Playing him as an irritable stereotype.

## Nessa Tor
**Role:** Proprietor of Gull & Anchor Chandlery.

- **Wants:** Recover a 46 gp account from a shipyard without losing a useful customer.
- **Fears:** A solvent debtor using a delayed shipowner as an excuse indefinitely.
- **Method:** Sociable when useful, remembers who pays and who does not.
- **Pressure:** Sets a schedule and follows up persistently.
- **Voice:** Conversational, sharp memory for names and invoices.
- **Sample:** “A mast does not care whose purse is late. I still need paying for the canvas.”
- **Move her with:** A credible payment schedule or verified proof of inability to pay.
- **Avoid:** Making her a source of every harbor secret.

## Hesta Pellor
**Role:** Proprietor of Three Grain Bakery.  
**Suggested alignment:** Neutral Good (provisional).

- **Wants:** Stable flour supply and safe, fair working conditions for apprentices.
- **Fears:** Cheap inconsistent flour ruining batches and wasting labor.
- **Method:** Energetic, proud, remembers customers and their habits.
- **Pressure:** Protects the bakery's quality and cuts waste.
- **Voice:** Lively, decisive, talks in practical measures.
- **Sample:** “Cheap flour is only cheap if it makes bread.”
- **Move her with:** Reliable grain quality and steady orders.
- **Avoid:** Making her a universal gossip source.

## Ollo Venn
**Role:** Proprietor of Salt & Root Provisioners.

## IX. Roleplaying and continuity standards

## Alignment policy
- Alignment suggestions are provisional roleplaying aids.
- Do not treat alignment as a complete personality or a prediction of every decision.
- Update alignment only when a sustained pattern of conduct supports it.
- A character can be lawful and exploitative, chaotic and compassionate, neutral and deeply principled.

## Information boundaries
- An NPC knows only what their experience, office, relationships, and credible information channels permit.
- A rumor is not objective truth. Consult the Tallowmere Rumor Truth Register.
- The House Relland incident is provisional legacy PC material. If the GM confirms it, the previously drafted knowledge limits for Lady Mereth and Corren apply; do not use them as current facts beforehand.
- No NPC automatically knows a player's secret identity or personal history; any earlier vampire-heir association remains provisional until the revised concept is confirmed.
- The secret underground route to Dragonstone is not known to ordinary actors.
- No NPC automatically knows the PCs' class abilities, equipment, history, or intentions.

## Relationship tracking
After a meaningful interaction, record:
- **Disposition:** hostile / wary / neutral / cordial / trusting / loyal (use judgment, not a numeric meter by default).
- **Trust basis:** what the NPC actually witnessed or was told.
- **Obligation:** favor, debt, promise, contract, or unresolved grievance.
- **Knowledge gained:** exact fact learned and source.
- **Changed motive:** what event changed their priorities, if anything.

## At-table portrayal shortcut
When unsure how an NPC responds, ask:
1. What are they trying to protect?
2. What do they know for certain?
3. What would this request cost them?
4. What would make them say yes?
5. What would make them walk away?
6. What would they do if the PCs never returned?

Do not force a scene to produce a quest. A conversation can end with a refusal, a small favor, a useful fact, a new obligation, or nothing at all.

- Update alignment only when a sustained pattern of conduct supports it.
- A character can be lawful and exploitative, chaotic and compassionate, neutral and deeply principled.

## Information boundaries
- An NPC knows only what their experience, office, relationships, and credible information channels permit.
- A rumor is not objective truth. Consult the Tallowmere Rumor Truth Register.
- The House Relland incident is provisional legacy PC material. If the GM confirms it, the previously drafted knowledge limits for Lady Mereth and Corren apply; do not use them as current facts beforehand.
- No NPC automatically knows a player's secret identity or personal history; any earlier vampire-heir association remains provisional until the revised concept is confirmed.
- The secret underground route to Dragonstone is not known to ordinary actors.
- No NPC automatically knows the PCs' class abilities, equipment, history, or intentions.

## Relationship tracking
After a meaningful interaction, record:
- **Disposition:** hostile / wary / neutral / cordial / trusting / loyal (use judgment, not a numeric meter by default).
- **Trust basis:** what the NPC actually witnessed or was told.
- **Obligation:** favor, debt, promise, contract, or unresolved grievance.
- **Knowledge gained:** exact fact learned and source.
- **Changed motive:** what event changed their priorities, if anything.

## At-table portrayal shortcut
When unsure how an NPC responds, ask:
1. What are they trying to protect?
2. What do they know for certain?
3. What would this request cost them?
4. What would make them say yes?
5. What would make them walk away?
6. What would they do if the PCs never returned?

Do not force a scene to produce a quest. A conversation can end with a refusal, a small favor, a useful fact, a new obligation, or nothing at all.
