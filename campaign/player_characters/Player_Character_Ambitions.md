# Player Character Ambitions and Established Background

**Status:** ESTABLISHED PLAYER-CHARACTER INFORMATION  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-04  
**Important boundary:** These are player-character goals and backstory facts supplied by the GM. Do not invent, revise, or expand personal histories, motives, identities, or relationships beyond what the GM and players provide. The PCs are not assumed to be heroes.

## 1. Tyler: The Vampire Mesmerist and Surviving Heir

**These are one character, not two separate PCs.** Tyler is the vampire mesmerist and is also the surviving Crown Prince of the fallen dragon-riding dynasty.

**Primary ambition:** Rebuild the fallen dynasty.

Established goals:
- Restore his family's dynasty and political position.
- Build the wealth, influence, and military strength necessary to make restoration possible.
- Eventually confront House Kharadorn, whose leadership was instrumental in the Great Rebellion that destroyed his family's rule.
- Retake Dragonstone.
- Restore his family's rule.

This is a long-term project of dynastic restoration, requiring resources, allies, political leverage, and military power. It is not automatically a heroic cause, and the character is not required to pursue it through honorable or benevolent means.

**Existing established background:** One surviving Crown Prince escaped the destruction of Dragonstone. He was nine years old at the beginning of the Great Rebellion. His survival is concealed, and the six Great Houses believe the old dragon-riding dynasty extinct. The PC is this surviving heir. His further personal history remains the player's domain.

## 2. The Ratfolk Alchemist

**Primary ambition:** End the persecution and hatred directed at ratfolk, and establish ratfolk as equals to, or even greater than, the other peoples of the continent.

Established background and goals:
- His family was killed because of anti-ratfolk persecution.
- The vampire PC saved him after the loss of his family.
- He was raised by the vampire PC.
- He wants to end the widespread hatred and persecution of ratfolk.
- He wants ratfolk to be able to live openly and safely, with equal standing.
- His ambition goes beyond mere tolerance: he wants ratfolk to become a respected and powerful people, potentially standing above the societies that have oppressed them.

This is a people-wide political and social ambition rooted in personal loss. It may involve changing laws, public attitudes, economic power, political representation, security, and ratfolk communities' ability to organize and defend themselves. These are possible avenues, not additional established goals or prescribed methods.

## 3. Tyler's Relationship with the Ratfolk Alchemist

Tyler, the vampire mesmerist and surviving dragon-dynasty heir, saved the ratfolk alchemist after the ratfolk's family was killed by persecution and raised him. Tyler's own further personal history and any ambitions beyond dynastic restoration remain for the player to establish.

## 4. Party-Level Campaign Identity

The party is not defined as a group of heroes. Their ambitions are personal, political, and potentially ruthless:
- Tyler seeks to restore the fallen dragon-riding dynasty.
- The ratfolk alchemist seeks to end the oppression of ratfolk and elevate their standing across the continent.

These goals can intersect, reinforce one another, or eventually conflict. Do not force them into a unified moral mission. The campaign world should respond to their actions and consequences rather than presuming they will save kingdoms, defend the innocent, or act for the greater good.

## 5. Boundaries for Future Worldbuilding

- Do not create names, personal histories, family details, personality traits, motivations, or secrets for PCs unless supplied by the GM/player.
- Do not determine how a PC pursues their ambition. The goal is established; the methods belong to the player.
- The GM will provide the vampire PC's backstory and the other PC's backstory when received. Incorporate those details faithfully.
- The setting may contain relevant factions, institutions, historical events, and social structures that create opportunities or obstacles for these ambitions, but do not predetermine the PCs' choices.


## 6. Clarification: The Vampire's Restoration Programme

**ESTABLISHED PLAYER INTENT:** The vampire PC does not merely possess a possible hereditary claim. He intends to take up the mantle of the fallen dragon-riding dynasty, restore his house, and rebuild it to its former glory, authority, and power.

His strategic ambition includes:
- Re-establishing the fallen dynasty and reclaiming the throne taken from his family.
- Accumulating wealth, political influence, allies, and military strength as the immediate, practical work toward restoration.
- Reclaiming Dragonstone, his dynasty's ancestral stronghold, which is now held by House Kharadorn.
- Eventually confronting House Kharadorn, whose leadership was instrumental in the Great Rebellion and whose power is tied to the loss of the dynasty's ancestral seat.
- Restoring the dynasty as a major ruling power, not merely preserving its name or securing a symbolic title.

**Current approach:** For now, he is building wealth and power. This is preparation for the long-term restoration, not a change or abandonment of that goal. The player has not yet specified the exact methods, allies, timetable, or political and military strategy. Those remain player decisions.

**Terminology:** “Targaryen” is the player's/legacy source label for the fallen dynasty. The final official in-world name remains unresolved in the campaign's naming records. Likewise, House Baratheon is a superseded legacy label; the current setting counterpart relevant to the rebellion and Dragonstone is House Kharadorn. Preserve the player's intended dynastic restoration while using current campaign canon names in setting-facing records.

The character is not required to be heroic or benevolent in pursuit of this ambition. His vampire template and alignment do not dictate his personality, values, or chosen methods.
