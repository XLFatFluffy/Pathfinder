# Player Character Records

This directory contains GM-managed records about player characters, their approved backgrounds, campaign ambitions, and opening-session preparation.

## Current records

- [Player Character Ambitions and Established Background](Player_Character_Ambitions.md) — GM-recorded character goals and background facts.
- [Player Character Concepts and Opening Adventure](Player_Character_Concepts_and_Opening.md) — provisional concepts, shared backstory notes, opening encounter preparation, and companion-related planning.

## Privacy and authority

Treat these files as GM-facing unless a section is explicitly marked for player release. Do not copy GM-only secrets into player-facing exports.

The players' character sheets are authoritative for names, statistics, equipment, spells, and other mechanical details. Do not infer missing character information or expand personal histories, motives, identities, or relationships beyond what the GM and players provide. Proposed concepts and unchosen details are not established character facts.
