# Player Character Records

Store player-approved character records here. Keep private character information and GM-only secrets access-controlled; never include them in player-facing exports by default.

Current campaign start has two level-5 characters:
- Human vampire mesmerist, last known surviving member of a fallen dragon-riding bloodline.
- Male ratfolk alchemist, raised by the vampire after his mother died.

Names, full statistics, equipment, spells, and additional biography details should be imported from the players' character sheets rather than inferred.
