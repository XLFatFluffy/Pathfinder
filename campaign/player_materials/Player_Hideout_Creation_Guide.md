# Your Hideout

Your campaign begins with something that belongs to you.

Not a castle. Not a kingdom. Not a suspiciously convenient mansion containing every room you could possibly want.

A hideout.

You and your companion will decide what kind of place you have, what it contains, and what you have spent your limited resources making useful.

## Starting Budget

You begin with:

**20 Base Development Points**

and

**10 Starting Resource Points**

The points are for the hideout itself. Your character's normal Pathfinder starting equipment is handled separately.

Basic necessities such as ordinary walls, floors, beds, simple cooking arrangements, and mundane household storage are normally assumed.

## Important Rule

**You are buying the facility, not its entire mechanical effect.**

For example, choosing an Arcane Study establishes that you have a proper place for magical study. It does not tell you exactly what bonuses, special equipment, rare books, or other advantages the room may eventually provide.

Some facilities may prove more useful than ordinary rooms. Some may eventually provide bonuses, improve what you can acquire, reduce the difficulty or cost of certain activities, or unlock things that would otherwise be unavailable.

The exact mechanics are part of the GM's campaign design and will be discovered as the base develops.

## Your 10 Resource Points

Your Resource Points represent the things you have actually stocked, purchased, salvaged, or otherwise obtained for the hideout.

Examples include:

- food stores;
- tools;
- mundane supplies;
- medical supplies;
- books;
- research materials;
- alchemical supplies;
- construction materials;
- trade goods;
- rare materials;
- magical components;
- other useful supplies.

Character equipment is separate.

## Your Starting Choices

When creating the hideout, decide:

1. **Where is it?**
   - wilderness;
   - abandoned property;
   - town or city;
   - old ruin;
   - underground;
   - frontier;
   - another reasonable location.

2. **What does it look like?**

3. **What is its primary purpose?**
   - home;
   - sanctuary;
   - laboratory;
   - training facility;
   - intelligence headquarters;
   - workshop;
   - something else.

4. **What are its most important features?**

5. **What have you deliberately left out?**

6. **What problem does the property already have?**

The last question matters. A hideout that is completely perfect, completely secure, completely supplied, and completely free of complications would be suspiciously close to a video-game menu rather than a place in a living world.

## Growing the Hideout

Your hideout can grow over the course of the campaign.

You can gain additional Base Development Points through major campaign milestones and meaningful accomplishments.

Gold is used to actually pay for construction, improvements, equipment, labor, materials, and other expenses.

So you may have:

**Points without enough gold**

or

**Gold without enough development capacity**

and both situations matter.

You may also acquire existing buildings, property, facilities, or useful resources during the campaign rather than constructing everything from scratch.

Your hideout is not just a place to sleep.

It can eventually become a headquarters, estate, stronghold, political center, military base, magical research center, or the foundation of something much larger.
