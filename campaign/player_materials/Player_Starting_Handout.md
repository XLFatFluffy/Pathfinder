# Player Starting Handout — The Realm and Tallowmere

**For:** Session One  
**Campaign date:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC  
**Ruleset:** Pathfinder First Edition  
**Visibility:** PLAYER_SHARED

This guide contains public information that a reasonably informed person could know. Your character's upbringing, education, travels, ancestry, class, and personal history may give them more specific knowledge. It does not include private motives, hidden histories, or secrets.

## The Setting

The campaign takes place in a high-medieval fantasy world based on the Westeros and *Game of Thrones* setting framework: noble houses, hereditary claims, feudal obligations, warfare, trade, and political intrigue. The campaign uses Pathfinder First Edition rules and its established names, geography, and history.

Humans are the largest population across much of the continent. Elves and dwarves hold major political power, while many other ancestries live throughout the settled lands. Ratfolk communities are often subterranean. Prejudice against ratfolk is widespread in surface society, but it is not universal and reactions vary by place and individual.

Magic exists, but it is uncommon in everyday life. Many people will live their entire lives without seeing a spell cast. The gods of the Pathfinder pantheon are real and widely recognized, although worship and religious customs vary between communities. Divine spellcasters are uncommon; a temple or shrine does not necessarily have someone capable of magical healing.

## The Crown and the Six Great Houses

The current monarch is **King Aldric Calderan**, of House Calderan. The capital is **Valedorn**, where the Imperial Seat stands. The monarch presides over a six-seat Imperial Council, with one seat appointed by each Great House. The Council is an aristocratic negotiating body concerned with matters affecting the realm and the privileges of the Great Houses; it is not a modern elected parliament.

| Great House | Public standing | Principal seat | Current ruler |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Calderan** | Human ruling dynasty; holds the Imperial Seat | Valedorn | King Aldric Calderan |
| **Vaelorian** | Elven commercial and financial power in the western highlands | Aurelith | Lord Theren Vaelorian |
| **Kharadorn** | Dwarven imperial dynasty; holds Dragonstone and dominates the central inland sea | Dragonstone; ancestral homeland at Khar-Dum | High King Durnak Kharadorn |
| **Edarth** | Primarily human landed and martial power in the western heartlands | Stoneguard | Lord Garrick Edarth |
| **Veyr** | Human northern dynasty responsible for the northern frontier and the Ancient Barrier | Frosthold | Lord Edran Veyr |
| **Merrowen** | Primarily human maritime and naval power along the eastern coast | Tidemark | Lady Maris Merrowen |

The six houses united in the Great Rebellion, which overthrew the old dragon-riding dynasty roughly two to three centuries ago. The rebellion ended that dynasty's rule from the Imperial Seat. Most dragons were killed; those that survived the war disappeared from public view, and their ultimate fate is unknown. House Kharadorn was the rebellion's principal military and political architect. The victorious houses established the present post-Rebellion order, with House Calderan occupying the throne.

## Major Places Across the Continent

The following cities, ports, principal seats, and fortresses are widely known. Their existence is common knowledge; detailed routes, defenses, local conditions, and political arrangements may require more specific information.

**Great House seats and principal strongholds**
- **Valedorn:** The inland capital and seat of the current monarchy.
- **Aurelith:** Principal seat of House Vaelorian in the Western Highlands.
- **Stoneguard:** Principal seat of House Edarth in the Western Heartlands.
- **Frosthold:** Principal seat of House Veyr in the northern territories.
- **Tidemark:** Principal seat and major harbor of House Merrowen on the eastern coast.
- **Dragonstone:** The conquered island stronghold of the former dragon-riding dynasty, now held by House Kharadorn.
- **Khar-Dum:** The Kharadorns' ancestral homeland in the northeastern mountains.

**Other major ports and fortified places**
- **Tallowmere:** Walled port on the northwestern shore of the central inland sea.
- **Ironharbor:** Dwarven port associated with the central inland sea.
- **Deepwatch:** Dwarven fortified settlement associated with House Kharadorn.
- **Greywatch:** A northern settlement associated with House Veyr. This is distinct from Greywatch, the Denvor seat within Tallowmere.
- **Eastwatch:** A settlement in House Merrowen's northern coastal territory.
- **Deltaport:** Port in the eastern river delta.
- **Stormhaven:** Port on the southern/eastern coast.
- **Duskport:** Port in the Southern Islands and Southern Reaches.

The southern reaches are politically fragmented. Smaller principalities, independent towns, island powers, and minor noble houses exist there; they do not form one unified kingdom.

## Tallowmere

Tallowmere is a walled port city on the northwestern shore of the central inland sea, where the navigable River Vale reaches the sea. Its population is estimated at about 18,000. It is an important working harbor and trade center, but it is neither the continental capital nor the largest port on the inland sea.

**House Denvor** governs Tallowmere from **Greywatch**, its fortified seat on the high ground above the harbor. Lord Oswin Denvor is the city's lord. House Denvor is a locally important minor house, not a seventh Great House. Its hereditary charter was confirmed by House Kharadorn. The Denvors govern ordinary city life; Kharadorn retains strategic rights and harbor interests but does not administer the city's daily affairs.

### City districts

- **Greywatch Rise:** House Denvor's fortified seat, garrison, and local authority.
- **The High Ward:** Wealthier homes, legal offices, and established temples.
- **The Old Quays:** Historic waterfront, shipwrights, chandlers, inns, and older warehouses.
- **The New Quays:** Expanded docks, bulk cargo, and newer merchant concerns.
- **Market Ward:** Bellmarket, guildhalls, shops, moneychangers, and public notices.
- **Rivergate:** Bargemen, mills, laborers, and river commerce.
- **Lantern Ward:** Temples, hospice, healers, and burial grounds.
- **The Underways:** A network of subterranean spaces containing decentralized ratfolk neighborhoods, homes, workshops, and routes. It is not one unified settlement or political faction.

### Useful public destinations

- **The Blue Heron:** A respectable inn in the Old Quays.
- **Reeve's Hall:** Local administration, petitions, records, and notices.
- **The Harbor Office:** Shipping declarations, berths, and harbor procedures.
- **Bellmarket:** The principal public market in Market Ward.
- **The Warehouse Guild:** A major commercial institution in the city.
- **Lantern Hospice:** Charity and care for people in need.
- **The Underways:** Ratfolk communities and routes; access and introductions depend on local knowledge.

### Roads and trade

Tallowmere connects to the wider realm through the River Vale, inland-sea shipping, and overland roads. Important continental trade corridors include the Northern Road, Western Mountain Road, King's Road, Inland Sea Route, Eastern Coastal Road, and Southern Trade Road.

Local routes include River Vale Road, Western Track, Quarry Road, Eastern Shore Road, and South Road. Within the city, principal streets include Harbor Road, Market Street, King's Road, West Road, North Road, Lantern Way, Greywatch Stair, Rise Road, Quay Lane, New Wharf Road, and River Road. Exact courses and travel times remain approximate; use the GM's current map and guidance for actual journeys.

## Starting Circumstances and Resources

The campaign begins as the two player characters arrive in Tallowmere. The characters already know one another; they are newcomers to the city, and no prior local contacts should be assumed unless their own backstories establish them.

Both characters begin at **level 5**. Your completed character sheet is the authority for your character's actual statistics, equipment, carried currency, consumables, and possessions. No additional shared starting stipend, special item, or unlisted equipment has been assigned. Any proposed companion character is subject to GM approval and will be handled separately.

## What This Guide Does Not Establish

This guide does not decide your character's private history, relationships, motives, personal knowledge, or secrets. It also does not reveal hidden political objectives, concealed identities, undiscovered history, or GM-only information. Those details belong to the characters' stories and to what the party discovers through play.
