# The Six Great Houses: Current Rulers and Succession

**Status:** ESTABLISHED — Stage 8 approved 2026-11-04.

These are setting NPCs. They do not establish or alter any player character's identity, ancestry, history, or personal relationships.

## House Calderan — The Crown
- **King Aldric Calderan:** Human, approximately 56; ruled 18 years. Intelligent, educated, patient, politically cautious, and increasingly frustrated by the limits placed on the Crown. Publicly defends peace and stability; privately believes the monarchy must gradually reclaim authority to avoid becoming irrelevant.
- **Queen Elowen Calderan:** Politically perceptive; maintains relationships with noble families and religious authorities.
- **Crown Prince Edric Calderan:** Approximately 27; recognized heir. Responsible, dutiful, cautious; believes the Crown must preserve the post-Rebellion settlement.
- **Princess Maerelle Calderan:** Approximately 23; intelligent, ambitious, and frustrated by Edric's caution. She favors a more assertive royal strategy.
- **Tension:** Edric is the recognized heir. Maerelle is not the designated successor but may become a political force. Aldric's desire to strengthen the Crown creates disagreement over how aggressively to pursue reform.
- **Agenda:** Expand royal authority through appointments, administration, taxation, and strategic alliances rather than open confrontation.

## House Vaelorian — The Western Highlands
- **Lord Theren Vaelorian:** Elf, approximately 240; ruled nearly 90 years. Cultured, patient, calculating, and difficult to surprise. Prefers financial and diplomatic leverage to direct threats.
- **Lady Seralyth Vaelorian:** Theren's eldest daughter and designated heir. Highly capable in finance and diplomacy, more openly ambitious than her father, and willing to adapt Vaelorian influence to changing commercial realities.
- **Lord Caelion Vaelorian:** Younger son, interested in scholarship, history, and religious philosophy. Considered politically unambitious, perhaps mistakenly.
- **Lady Virella Vaelorian:** Theren's sister and influential among older elven families. Opposes increasing integration of younger Vaelorians into human commercial society.
- **Tension:** Seralyth is the clear heir, but older traditionalist families distrust her approach and may seek to constrain her authority or promote an alternative within the wider family. No rival claimant is formally established.
- **Agenda:** Maintain financial dominance, preserve trade access, and prevent any rival power from threatening Vaelorian interests.

## House Kharadorn — The Dwarven Empire
- **High King Durnak Kharadorn:** Dwarf, approximately 190; ruled 42 years. Stern, disciplined, traditional, and deeply conscious of his family's history. Values clan unity and the empire's survival over personal glory.
- **Prince Vorgun Kharadorn:** Eldest son and designated successor. Respected military commander, more aggressive than his father, and willing to use military and strategic leverage to secure the empire's interests.
- **Princess Brynja Kharadorn:** Younger daughter, skilled in clan negotiations. Believes unity depends on preserving ancient agreements and clan privileges.
- **Lord Kazrik Kharadorn:** Durnak's younger brother, respected by older clans; believes the main branch has become too accommodating toward surface powers.
- **Tension:** Vorgun's militarism concerns those favoring clan consensus and careful diplomacy. Brynja may become important if clans resist his approach.
- **Agenda:** Preserve dwarven independence, control of the inland sea and Dragonstone, and freedom from surface interference in clan affairs.
- **Historical distinction:** Durnak is a later ruler, not the dwarven leader who directed the immediate post-Rebellion settlement.

## House Edarth — The Western Heartlands
- **Lord Garrick Edarth:** Human, approximately 62; ruled 26 years. Traditional martial lord who values discipline, loyalty, and reputation. Respected among sworn families, though not universally liked.
- **Ser Rowan Edarth:** Eldest son and recognized heir. Capable military commander; believes the house must modernize its political strategy and manage lesser families carefully.
- **Lady Elayne Edarth:** Younger daughter, politically perceptive and connected to lesser noble families. Not the designated heir, but influential.
- **Ser Tomas Edarth:** Garrick's nephew, celebrated knight whose popularity among household soldiers concerns Rowan.
- **Tension:** Rowan is recognized heir, but Elayne's political skill and Tomas's military popularity create other centers of influence. Edarth's decentralized structure may make succession difficult.
- **Agenda:** Preserve traditional noble rights, resist royal centralization, and maintain Edarth's military and agricultural strength.

## House Veyr — The Northern Marches
- **Lord Edran Veyr:** Human, approximately 51; ruled 17 years. Practical, reserved, and accustomed to difficult decisions with limited resources. Regards southern court politics as a distraction from frontier dangers.
- **Lady Anika Veyr:** Eldest daughter and designated heir. Experienced in administration and northern logistics; favors stronger political relationships with the Crown and other Great Houses to secure frontier support.
- **Ser Halvar Veyr:** Younger son, Northern Watch officer, popular among frontier soldiers and less interested in political compromise.
- **Lord Stellan Veyr:** Edran's uncle, veteran commander; believes Veyr has become too dependent on southern supplies and promises.
- **Tension:** Anika is recognized heir, but Halvar's popularity gives him influence beyond his formal position. Edran must preserve the distinction between military respect and hereditary succession.
- **Agenda:** Secure resources for the Northern Watch, preserve frontier control, and prevent the south from neglecting the Ancient Barrier.

## House Merrowen — The Eastern Coast
- **Lady Maris Merrowen:** Human, approximately 48; ruled 14 years. Accomplished political operator with extensive commercial and diplomatic relationships. Pragmatic, confident, and comfortable with foreign merchants and cultures.
- **Lord Corvin Merrowen:** Eldest son and designated heir. Naval commander who believes Merrowen's future depends on a powerful fleet and secure maritime routes.
- **Lady Selene Merrowen:** Younger daughter, involved in trade negotiations and merchant interests. Believes commercial power matters more than military display.
- **Lord Aeric Merrowen:** Maris's brother, traditional coastal lord who believes merchant families have gained too much influence.
- **Tension:** Corvin is recognized heir. The central struggle is between traditional maritime nobility and increasingly wealthy merchant families. Merchants can challenge policy and constrain the ruling family's choices, but do not presently threaten to replace the dynasty.
- **Agenda:** Protect eastern ports and shipping, preserve commercial independence, and prevent Kharadorn or the Crown from dominating maritime trade.

## Current Ruler-to-Ruler Dynamics
- **Aldric Calderan and Theren Vaelorian:** The Crown needs financial support; Theren expects influence in return.
- **Aldric Calderan and Durnak Kharadorn:** Durnak respects the settlement but does not consider himself subject to the Crown.
- **Aldric Calderan and Garrick Edarth:** Garrick suspects the king is gradually weakening the Great Houses.
- **Aldric Calderan and Edran Veyr:** Edran wants practical resources and attention for the north, not ceremonial promises.
- **Aldric Calderan and Maris Merrowen:** Maris supports Crown policy when it benefits maritime commerce.
- **Theren Vaelorian and Maris Merrowen:** Commercial cooperation alongside competition for trade influence.
- **Durnak Kharadorn and Garrick Edarth:** Shared respect for military tradition and independence.
- **Durnak Kharadorn and Edran Veyr:** Mutual respect, limited direct political interaction.
- **Garrick Edarth and Edran Veyr:** Natural military and political understanding.
- **Theren Vaelorian and Garrick Edarth:** Theren considers Garrick predictable; Garrick considers Theren dangerous.
- **Maris Merrowen and Durnak Kharadorn:** Maris wants freer navigation; Durnak preserves dwarven control of the inland sea.
