# Minor Houses and Regional Powers

**Status:** GM-ONLY ACTIVE REFERENCE  
**Baseline:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC  
**Authority:** Consolidated from the established continental minor-house register, Tallowmere regional holdings, feudal framework, and Great House canon.

This is the active political reference for lesser noble houses and regional powers. The legacy `campaign/world_engine/` records remain useful source-review material, but their simulation procedures are retired and they are not required to run the campaign.

## 1. Rules for using lesser powers

Minor houses are important because most political life happens below the six Great Houses. They manage estates, villages, roads, markets, harbors, woodland, farms, crossings, and local obligations.

They do not automatically rival a Great House.

A minor house may possess:
- a hereditary or chartered holding;
- local courts or customary authority;
- tenants, freeholders, workers, or dependent communities;
- household retainers;
- economic rights;
- military obligations;
- political relationships with a liege and neighbors.

A **holding**, **liege relationship**, **strategic interest**, **charter right**, and **political influence** are separate facts. Never turn them into one assumed border polygon.

No exact border, coordinate, surveyed route, army size, treasury, or territorial area is established unless another authoritative record explicitly provides it.

## 2. Northern Marches — House Veyr

### House Coldmere

**Type:** Minor noble banner house  
**Liege:** House Veyr  
**Holding:** Small estate and grazing lands on the settled southern approaches to the Northern Holds, associated with the supply road serving Wintermere.  
**Seat:** Coldmere Hall

Coldmere's traditional role is maintaining local supply and pasture while meeting Veyr obligations. The house has modest pasture, livestock, stores, a small household guard, and practical knowledge of winter supply.

Its principal tension is with Watch quartermasters over requisition and repayment. It is loyal to Veyr in principle and has no established feud with another banner house.

**Limits:** Coldmere cannot independently defend a frontier district and cannot simply refuse a lawful Veyr summons without consequences.

### House Pineward

**Type:** Minor noble banner house  
**Liege:** House Veyr  
**Holding:** Woodland and small agricultural holdings in the Pine Marches, with commercial ties to Pinecross.  
**Seat:** Pineward Lodge

Pineward derives influence from woodland rents, charcoal contracts, and local knowledge. Its central tension is between customary woodland users and the need to satisfy Veyr's supply expectations.

**Limits:** Pineward does not own every woodland resource used by nearby communities and cannot dictate regional timber prices.

## 3. Western Highlands — House Vaelorian

### House Aethwyn

**Type:** Minor elven noble banner house  
**Liege:** House Vaelorian  
**Holding:** Old woodland estate and several small tenant communities in the Green Valleys, away from Vaelorian's major directly controlled mines.  
**Seat:** Aethwyn Court

Aethwyn represents older elven landholding traditions within the Vaelorian sphere. It possesses old land rights, woodland income, family reputation, educated retainers, and scholarly relationships.

The house is formally loyal to Lord Theren Vaelorian. Its traditionalist outlook overlaps with some older elven families, but it is not an established opposition faction.

**Current tension:** Aethwyn views Seralyth Vaelorian's commercial reforms with caution.

**Limits:** Aethwyn has no claim to Vaelorian's major mines and no independent foreign policy.

## 4. Western Heartlands — House Edarth

### House Harrowden

**Type:** Minor landed banner house  
**Liege:** House Edarth  
**Holding:** Grain farms, pasture, and a modest fortified manor in the Western Heartlands, within the agricultural network serving Harveston.  
**Seat:** Harrowden Hall

Harrowden protects grain production, tenant obligations, and local pasture. Its prosperity depends on harvests and transport.

**Resources:** Productive farms, grain stores, draft animals, household retainers, and a small mounted household contingent.

**Current dispute:** Harrowden is the established claimant in the Longmead boundary dispute with House Wrenford.

### House Wrenford

**Type:** Minor landed banner house  
**Liege:** House Edarth  
**Holding:** Mixed farms, pasture, and a small stretch of managed woodland adjoining Harrowden lands.  
**Seat:** Wrenford House

Wrenford's interests center on agricultural rents, woodland products, tenant ties, and maintaining its recognized rights.

**Current dispute:** Wrenford is the opposing claimant in the Longmead boundary dispute.

The dispute concerns an unnamed pasture-and-woodland boundary. A stream and old markers define part of the customary division, but neither house has submitted a complete mutually accepted claim.

Lord Garrick Edarth may arbitrate the matter, but no final ruling is established.

**Map boundary:** Do not draw the disputed area as a political polygon until authoritative regional artwork and a GM-approved boundary decision exist.

## 5. Central Crownlands — House Calderan

### House Rookwell

**Type:** Minor Crown vassal house  
**Liege:** House Calderan / the Crown directly  
**Holding:** Compact estate and road-service rights in the central Crownlands, associated with the inland approaches to Kingsbridge.  
**Seat:** Rookwell Keep

Rookwell's principal political interest is preserving direct Crown-vassal status rather than becoming subordinate to a neighboring Great House.

Its role is administrative and territorial rather than continent-shaping.

**Limits:** Rookwell is not a Great House and does not independently control Crownlands policy.

## 6. Kharadorn sphere — Clan Emberdelve

### Clan Emberdelve

**Type:** Dwarven clan within the Kharadorn imperial union  
**Liege:** Kharadorn imperial structure  
**Holding:** Subterranean mining and metalworking complex within the northeastern mountain system and wider Khar-Dum sphere. Exact location and extent remain undisclosed.

Emberdelve possesses skilled miners, smiths, engineers, subterranean holdings, and clan obligations.

It is **not** a separate Great House and does not independently control Dragonstone or the Inland Sea.

Individual tribute, military, labor, and resource obligations remain unresolved.

**Current status:** No rebellion, succession crisis, or refusal of clan obligations is established.

## 7. Eastern Maritime Territory — House Merrowen

### House Ternholt

**Type:** Minor maritime banner house  
**Liege:** House Merrowen  
**Holding:** Modest coastal estate and fishing harbors in the Northern Coast region, with commercial ties to Eastwatch.  
**Seat:** Gullwatch Hall

Ternholt derives influence from fishing, small craft, pilot knowledge, harbor dues, and local maritime custom.

It is loyal to Merrowen but protective of local practices.

**Limits:** Ternholt has no fleet capable of challenging Merrowen's principal naval power.

Commercial cooperation with Vaelorian merchants is established as practical commerce, not an alliance.

## 8. Southern independent powers

The southern political framework remains deliberately fragmented.

### House Serrane of Sablecross

**Type:** Independent southern lordship  
**Holding:** Fortified well-town and surrounding dry-valley estates associated with Sablecross.  
**Seat:** Sablecross Hall

Serrane is an independent local power rather than a banner house of one of the six Great Houses.

Its authority is built around the settlement, surrounding land, water access, and local caravan connections.

Its civic council is described as having seven elected or guild-appointed seats, but the exact selection balance remains locally contested and should not be treated as a modern democratic system.

### House Veylan of Marrowgate

**Type:** Independent southern island lordship  
**Holding:** Small island territory and harbor rights associated with Marrowgate in the Southern Islands.  
**Seat:** Marrowgate House

Veylan's influence comes from harbor access, fishing, pilotage, coastal vessels, and local retainers.

It maintains practical commercial ties with other island ports. It is not established as a major naval power and cannot police the entire island chain.

### The Brightwater Civic Compact

**Type:** Southern civic power  
**Status:** Established regional actor

The Compact is a civic rather than hereditary noble power. Its existence reinforces the established principle that the southern regions are politically diverse rather than uniformly feudal.

Its exact internal institutions, territory, and military capacity remain limited to what the dedicated regional record establishes.

### The Duskport Shipmasters' Compact

**Type:** Southern maritime commercial power  
**Status:** Established regional actor

The Compact represents shipmaster and maritime commercial interests around Duskport. It is an economic and civic power, not a Great House and not automatically a sovereign navy.

Its precise membership, territorial reach, and relationship with southern noble houses remain limited to established records.

## 9. Tallowmere's established lesser houses

These houses belong to the local political framework and should not be confused with the continental network above.

### House Denvor

**Type:** Minor noble house with a hereditary city and surrounding lordship  
**Seat:** Greywatch  
**Authority:** Tallowmere and modest surrounding territory

House Denvor governs Tallowmere under a hereditary charter confirmed by House Kharadorn after the Great Rebellion.

The charter establishes tribute, harbor-defense obligations, and provision of ships, supplies, or transport when lawfully called upon. Kharadorn retains strategic rights concerning the harbor and inland-sea defense.

Denvor retains ordinary civic administration and local authority.

**Current ruler:** Lord Oswin Denvor.  
**Succession:** Ser Edwyn is increasingly involved in defensive and civic affairs; Lady Anwen maintains relationships beyond the household's traditional circle. The existing Denvor dossier remains authoritative for family details.

### House Avenholt

**Type:** Minor house  
**Holding:** Ashcombe Manor  
**Authority:** House Denvor

Avenholt holds Ashcombe Manor under Denvor. It owes customary dues and military service subject to its tenure.

**Established figures:** Lady Ysabet Avenholt and heir Ser Corvin.

Exact estate boundaries, service duration, and detailed tenure instrument remain unresolved.

### House Merewick

**Type:** Minor hereditary house  
**Holding:** Fordmere crossing  
**Authority:** Separate hereditary crossing charter within Kharadorn's wider sphere

Merewick administers the established Fordmere crossing. House Denvor coordinates access and roads but does not own the crossing.

The exact scope of the crossing charter and outer boundary remain unresolved.

### House Velleran

**Type:** Minor house  
**Holding:** Harthbridge and compact surrounding territory  
**Authority:** Charter confirmed by a regional authority within Kharadorn's sphere

Velleran holds Harthbridge's town charter, market and toll privileges, and compact territory.

The house competes commercially with Tallowmere while cooperating on road security, grain movement, and regional disputes.

Exact obligations to its superior authority and the charter's full legal text remain unresolved.

### House Relland

**Type:** Minor house  
**Liege:** House Velleran  
**Holding:** Manor and tenant lands along the South Road within the broader Velleran-chartered territory

Relland's local income includes rents, grazing rights, grain, and road frontage.

The dedicated Relland record contains a prior merchant incident involving player-character material. That material remains **PROVISIONAL / QUARANTINED** until the player concept and incident are confirmed. It must not be treated as a current scandal or established campaign event.

## 10. Supplemental houses not yet promoted to core political canon

Two additional houses exist in legacy supporting material:

- **House Corven**, associated with Greywater Hall and a coastal territory outside the calibrated Tallowmere region.
- **House Calderick**, associated with Calderick Hall in a fragmented southern region and carrying an unresolved succession situation.

Their existence is retained as **supporting/provisional material**, not promoted to core continental political canon in this audit.

Their exact lieges, locations, borders, family claims, and political relationships remain unresolved. Do not place them on the live atlas or introduce their unresolved succession as an active campaign event without a separate canon decision.

The southern independent powers are not assigned Great House allegiance merely because they trade with a Great House.

## 12. What this audit resolves

The current minor-house network is internally consistent on the major questions that matter for play:

- Veyr houses remain northern.
- Vaelorian's lesser house remains elven and within the western highlands.
- Edarth's two established banner houses remain in the Western Heartlands.
- Rookwell is directly Crown-aligned rather than a Great House vassal.
- Emberdelve is a Kharadorn clan, not a Great House.
- Ternholt is Merrowen-aligned.
- The southern powers remain politically fragmented and independent where established.
- Tallowmere's local houses remain distinct from the continental network.
- House Relland's PC-specific material remains quarantined.
- No minor house is granted a new border or coordinate.
- No existing Great House territory is silently expanded or reduced.

## 13. Open questions

Still unresolved and intentionally left unresolved:

- Exact borders and surveyed geometry of every minor holding.
- Exact wording of most charters and grants.
- Exact tribute, tax, military, and labor quantities.
- Full family trees and collateral branches.
- Detailed household officers and retainers.
- Exact military strength and financial reserves.
- Local succession customs where a dedicated record does not establish them.
- Precise relationships among southern independent powers.
- Whether supplemental houses such as Corven or Calderick become important to the campaign.

These should be answered when a character, location, political conflict, or map calibration makes them useful. The world does not need a noble family tree for every hill merely because humans have an incurable urge to put someone in charge of it.
