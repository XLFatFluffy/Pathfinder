# Political Records

Store Great Houses, minor houses, dynasties, ruling families, titles, succession, royal government, court, council, treaties, alliances, rivalries, territorial claims, and political incidents here.

## Authoritative records

- [The Six Great Houses: Current Rulers and Succession](House_Rulers.md) — approved Stage 8 rulers, immediate families, heirs, and succession tensions.
- [The Imperial Council and Royal Court](Court_and_Council.md) — approved Stage 9 Council representatives, Crown officers, court factions, and political relationships.
- [House Relland and the Merchant Incident](House_Relland_and_the_Merchant_Incident.md) — GM-facing minor-house record and the established incident affecting a player character.

The six Great Houses and their core identities are established: Vaelorian (elven), Kharadorn (dwarven), Edarth (primarily human), Calderan (human ruling dynasty), Veyr (human northern dynasty), and Merrowen (primarily human maritime dynasty). Their broad territories and principal seats are established in Stage 5.

These are approved setting identities, not placeholders awaiting replacement. The final original name of the fallen dragon-riding dynasty and the final name of Dragonstone remain open. Exact borders, coordinates, wider family trees, detailed histories, Council procedures, and faction memberships remain open where not otherwise established.

## Record convention

Track canon status, visibility, ruler, seat, territory, ancestry/culture, heraldry, words, resources, military strength, claims, allies, rivals, vassals, succession, history, and sources. Unknown details should remain blank or explicitly unresolved. Link to authoritative records instead of duplicating detailed material.
