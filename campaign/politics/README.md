# Political Records

Store kingdoms, noble houses, dynasties, ruling families, titles, succession, treaties, alliances, rivalries, and territorial claims here.

## Current status — 2026-11-04

The six Great Houses and their core identities are established: Vaelorian (elven), Kharadorn (dwarven), Edarth (primarily human), Calderan (human ruling dynasty), Veyr (human northern dynasty), and Merrowen (primarily human maritime dynasty). Their broad territories and principal seats are established in Stage 5. Current rulers, immediate families, designated heirs, and succession tensions are recorded in `../House_Rulers.md` (Stage 8). Imperial Council representatives, Crown officers, court-faction leaders, and key associates are recorded in `../Court_and_Council.md` (Stage 9).

These are approved setting identities, not placeholders awaiting replacement. The final original name of the fallen dragon-riding dynasty and the final name of Dragonstone remain open. Exact borders, coordinates, wider family trees, detailed histories, Council procedures, and faction memberships remain open where not otherwise established.

## Record convention

Track canon status, visibility, ruler, seat, territory, ancestry/culture, heraldry, words, resources, military strength, claims, allies, rivals, vassals, succession, history, and sources. Unknown details should remain blank or explicitly unresolved. Do not duplicate detailed records when a dedicated reference exists; link to the authoritative record instead.
