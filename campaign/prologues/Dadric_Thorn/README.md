# Dadric Thorn — Prologue Campaign Record

**Visibility:** GM-ONLY  
**Campaign system:** Pathfinder First Edition  
**Continuity point:** This is a character-focused prologue within the main campaign setting. Its events, relationships, resources, reputation, and world consequences carry into the main campaign.  
**Prologue endpoint:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC, the opening date of the main campaign.

## Purpose

This directory is the persistent record for the Dadric Thorn prologue. It is separate from the main campaign session archive so that the prologue can be played and documented independently without losing continuity with the shared world.

The main campaign's established canon remains authoritative. New details created during play should be recorded here first, then promoted into the appropriate campaign source records only when they establish lasting world facts.

## Records

- [Character Record](Character_Record.md) — current approved character information, mechanical audit, equipment, and advancement.
- [Campaign State](Campaign_State.md) — current in-world position, open threads, known relationships, and continuity state. Update after each session.
- [Session Journal](Session_Journal.md) — chronological record of played events. Preserve prior entries; append new sessions rather than rewriting history.
- [World Consequence Ledger](World_Consequence_Ledger.md) — events and changes that may affect the main campaign, with status and links to the appropriate canon records.

## Recordkeeping standards

- Keep player-known information distinct from GM-only information.
- Do not reveal future plot, hidden motives, secret NPC information, or unrevealed outcomes in player-facing material.
- Record only events that have actually occurred and facts that have been established. Label proposals and unresolved details clearly.
- Track the in-world date and location for each scene or session.
- Record mechanical changes when they occur, including damage, conditions, resources, wealth, inventory, experience, level, feats, skills, and ability scores.
- Preserve player agency. The journal records choices and consequences; it is not a script or a predetermined outcome.
- At the prologue endpoint, prepare a continuity handoff for the main campaign without exposing GM-only material to the other players.
