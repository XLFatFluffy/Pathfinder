### Prologue opening — Settling into Tallowmere

- **In-world date and time:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC; daytime through the following morning. Exact hours not established.
- **Location:** Tallowmere; local lodging, shrine, and stables.
- **Character level / XP:** Level 1 paladin. No XP award or advancement recorded.
- **Opening state:** Dadric arrived in Tallowmere as a newcomer, having spent most of his life in a temple. He is away from the temple for the first time and is beginning to establish himself locally.
- **Events:**
  - Dadric spoke with Edric about his upbringing and religious formation. He explained that he was raised in a loving but rigid temple environment, trained for paladinhood, and taught that light destroys darkness and good overcomes evil. He described his calling as becoming an instrument of light, destroying darkness, and leaving things better than he found them.
  - Dadric asked whether Tallowmere has a temple of Sarenrae. No answer is recorded in this entry.
  - Dadric settled into local lodging and paid an earlier meal and room bill. A later clarification established that this bill had already been paid; it must not be charged again.
  - Dadric arranged to remain longer and changed his lodging to a weekly rate, paying the amount due at that time after accounting for lodging already paid. The exact weekly rate and payment breakdown are not preserved in this journal entry; do not invent them.
  - Dadric bathed, changed into his traveler's clothes, kept his dagger on him, and relaxed before going to meet Harlan in the evening.
  - Dadric became suspicious of a person connected to a letter. He chose to take the letter back and report what had happened, while deliberately acting as though he did not suspect the person, in order to observe the reaction. The precise letter contents and details of the incident should be recovered from the prior play conversation before being treated as established facts.
  - Dadric later shared a meal with his hosts, made ordinary conversation, and spoke positively about Tallowmere and its people. He mentioned visiting the shrine and meeting the kind Mrs. Anwen.
  - The following morning, Dadric ate breakfast and thanked Mrs. Mara for her generosity and hospitality.
  - Dadric went to the stables to check on Spark and speak with Merek. Dadric told Merek he intended to stay in Tallowmere for a week, but that he needed Spark that day because he had business to attend to.
  - Merek agreed to prepare Spark for him. Spark is a mare. An earlier in-play description incorrectly referred to Spark as male; the player corrected this out of character. Use female pronouns for Spark consistently.
- **Dadric's decisions and actions:** Chose to extend his stay in Tallowmere; paid the weekly lodging amount; maintained a friendly demeanor with his hosts; chose a cautious, non-confrontational approach regarding the letter; checked on Spark and requested her for the day's business.
- **NPCs encountered:** Edric; Mrs. Anwen; Harlan; Mrs. Mara; Merek. Spark is Dadric's mare.
- **Relationship changes:** Dadric has begun building friendly familiarity with the people providing his lodging and with stablehand Merek. No numerical relationship changes are established.
- **Information learned by Dadric:** He has met Mrs. Anwen at the shrine and regards her as kind. He is finding Tallowmere and its people agreeable. Further details of the letter incident remain to be recovered from the prior conversation.
- **Rumors and uncertain claims:** None newly established in this entry.
- **GM-only developments:** Preserve unresolved details rather than filling gaps with invented explanations. The letter incident requires continuity review before further developments are adjudicated.
- **Combat and mechanical changes:** None recorded.
- **Inventory, wealth, and resource changes:** Weekly lodging was paid after crediting the previously paid room/meal bill. Exact transaction amount is not available in this record. Spark was prepared for Dadric to take out; no stable charge or other transaction has been established.
- **Consequences and unresolved threads:** Dadric has business to attend to with Spark this morning. The letter incident remains an active thread. Dadric intends to stay in Tallowmere for approximately one week.
- **Potential main-campaign impact:** None established yet.
- **Canon updates required:** Correct Spark's sex to female in any campaign records where it is specified. No broader world-canon changes arise from these events.


### Continuation — A donation to the shrine

- **In-world date and time:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC; morning. Exact hour not established.
- **Location:** Mrs. Anwen's shrine in Tallowmere.
- **Events:** After completing his morning prayer, Dadric told Mrs. Anwen that important business would keep him from helping with shrine repairs for a few days. ### Continuity recovery — Roadside ambush and the Vane letter

- **In-world date and time:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC; morning, before Dadric entered Tallowmere. Exact hour not established.
- **Location:** Road approximately two miles from Tallowmere; the coffer had been collected at Greyfield, south of Tallowmere.
- **Events:** Dadric encountered concealed mounted travelers in the morning mist and announced himself rather than approaching unseen. The travelers proved to be Edric and his injured brother Tomas, both merchants. They explained that three attackers had ambushed them the previous day. Tomas had been cut during the attack. The attackers displayed or wore burgundy-and-gold stag imagery and demanded the sender or cargo; they took a small, locked, iron-bound oak coffer but did not find or take the sealed letter Edric retained. The attackers reportedly claimed the coffer belonged to the Stormlands. No payment was demanded.
- **The delivery:** Edric and Tomas had been hired in Tallowmere three days earlier by Ser Alester Vane, a landed knight with modest holdings north of Tallowmere. At Greyfield, Vane's steward gave Edric the coffer. Edric kept it locked and did not know or disclose its contents. A separate unopened parchment remained with Edric; it was sealed in dark red wax bearing a tower beneath a crescent moon. This seal is distinct from the burgundy-and-gold stag imagery associated with the attackers.
- **Route and exposure:** Edric chose the western road toward Tallowmere. The coffer's loading was witnessed by Edric, Tomas, the steward, a stablehand, and possibly others. The attackers knew which pack to search, but how they learned the route or identified the coffer remains unresolved. Possibilities discussed in play included information learned at loading, the party being followed, or a correct guess; none is established as fact.
- **Dadric's actions:** Dadric helped stabilize Tomas with his healer's kit. He used Detect Evil on the brothers and detected no evil aura. He did not open the sealed parchment. An attempt to identify the heraldic imagery did not establish its identity. He then continued toward Tallowmere.
- **Letter decision:** In later discussion with Harlan, Dadric chose to speak with Edric first, retrieve the still-unopened letter from him, and then report the ambush to Ser Alester Vane while behaving as though he did not suspect Vane. Dadric's purpose is to observe Vane's reaction without revealing his suspicions. Harlan cautioned that a reaction alone would not prove guilt. The letter's contents remain unknown; it has not been opened.
- **NPCs encountered:** Edric; Tomas; Ser Alester Vane (not met); Vane's steward (not named); three unidentified attackers.
- **Information Dadric knows:** The brothers were hired by Vane; the coffer was handed over at Greyfield; the attackers specifically targeted it; Edric still has the unopened letter; the letter's seal depicts a tower beneath a crescent moon. Vane's actual involvement, the coffer's contents, the attackers' identities, and how the attackers learned about the coffer remain unknown.
- **Consequences and unresolved threads:** Dadric intends to retrieve the letter from Edric and report the ambush to Vane under a deliberately neutral pretense. Edric's exact current whereabouts are uncertain. In the present conversation, Harlan suggested checking the cooper's yard near the eastern end of town; earlier information placed Edric overnight at a merchant friend's house near the western street/watchhouse. Treat these as leads, not a confirmed location.
- **Continuity correction:** This entry supplies details that were previously left vague in the opening journal entry. It does not establish the letter's contents or prove any person's complicity.


### Continuation — Vanehold and employment under Ser Alester Vane

- **In-world date and time:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC; morning, exact hour not established.
- **Location:** Vanehold, Ser Alester Vane's manor north of Tallowmere.
- **Opening state:** Dadric and Edric left the merchant friend's home in western Tallowmere with Edric's horse and Spark, carrying the still-sealed letter entrusted to Edric by Vane's steward. Tomas remained behind recovering from his wound.
- **Events:**
  - Dadric and Edric rode north to Vanehold. Edric explained that Ser Alester was a proud man accustomed to being obeyed and suggested that he would handle introductions.
  - At the gate, Dadric introduced himself as Edric's friend and explained that he had offered protection because bandits seemed to be active on the roads, as well as companionship for the journey.
  - Before handing Spark to the stablehand, Dadric quietly told her to be on guard and warned the stablehand that she was feisty. The stablehand took her carefully and offered a stall away from the other horses. Edric's horse was also stabled.
  - Ser Alester received them in his solar. He demanded to know where the coffer was. Edric explained that three men had ambushed them, wounded Tomas, and taken it. Edric then produced the separate sealed letter.
  - Dadric sat quietly and used Detect Evil on Ser Alester. No evil aura was detected. He listened to Edric recount the ambush and the attackers' burgundy-and-gold stag imagery and claim that the coffer belonged to the Stormlands.
  - Ser Alester asked whether the attackers had tried to take the letter. Edric said they had not appeared interested in it. Ser Alester asked who knew about the coffer; Edric identified himself, Tomas, Vane's steward, the stablehand who helped load it at Greyfield, and possibly others.
  - Dadric identified the stag as Baratheon from what he had gathered, while stating he knew nothing else and hoped Ser Alester could help. Ser Alester agreed House Baratheon bears a crowned stag but said burgundy and gold were not colours he associated with their banners. He denied having business with House Baratheon or authorizing anyone to collect property in their name.
  - Ser Alester opened the letter in their presence. Its contents: it concerned a meeting that was supposed to take place at Vanehold; someone was expected to arrive carrying information about activity along the Stormlands border. The writer warned that someone was paying men to disrupt certain shipments and intimidate those involved, and believed the activity was being organized by someone with access to information from within the region.
  - Ser Alester stated that the stolen coffer contained records: names, payments, and correspondence concerning merchants and minor lords operating between the Stormlands and the Crownlands. He said the records could expose people who would prefer to remain hidden, some powerful and others potentially innocent. The letter's writer and intended visitor have not been identified in Dadric's knowledge.
  - Ser Alester asked whether the letter had been discussed with anyone in Tallowmere. Dadric answered that only the healer had been spoken to; neither brother had opened or read it. Edric clarified they had not shown Harlon the letter or told him its contents, only that they intended to return it to Ser Alester.
  - Ser Alester asked Edric whether the attackers intended to kill them or simply take the coffer. Edric said they were willing to hurt them, one struck Tomas when he resisted, but they did not pursue Edric after he fled and left once they had the coffer.
  - Ser Alester offered Edric separate compensation for the lost cargo and trouble, and asked him to remain available for further testimony if needed.
  - Ser Alester offered Dadric work investigating the theft, recovering the coffer if possible, and learning who arranged the ambush and how they knew about the shipment. He offered 2 gp per day, reasonable travel expenses, and an additional reward if the coffer is recovered. Dadric stated that he would do no dishonorable work, draws steel only when there is no other choice, and needs coin to help others. He accepted.
  - Ser Alester agreed to those boundaries, stating he wanted someone who could uncover the truth rather than create a bloodbath. The agreement is for direct service to Ser Alester, with no loyalty oath to House Vane. Ser Alester said wages begin today, reasonable expenses on his business will be covered, and the additional recovery reward is not yet quantified. He said he would have the agreement written up. No wages or expenses have yet been paid.
  - Ser Alester stated his terms included no dishonest dealings, harming innocents, or needless violence. Dadric is not to be presumed to have accepted any task that violates his stated principles.
- **Dadric's decisions and actions:** Rode to Vanehold with Edric; warned the stablehand about Spark and quietly told Spark to be on guard; introduced himself as Edric's friend and protective traveling companion; listened without interrupting; used Detect Evil on Ser Alester; identified the stag as Baratheon only to the extent of his gathered knowledge; accepted Ser Alester's investigation contract with explicit moral boundaries.
- **NPCs encountered:** Ser Alester Vane; Edric; two gate guards; a stablehand; a manor servant. Tomas remains at the merchant friend's home in Tallowmere. The Vane steward remains unnamed and has not yet been questioned by Dadric.
- **Relationship changes:** Ser Alester has hired Dadric directly and appears to regard him as potentially useful. Edric is relieved and will be separately compensated. No numerical relationship values are established.
- **Information learned by Dadric:** Ser Alester denies business with House Baratheon and denies authorizing anyone to collect the coffer. The letter describes a planned meeting at Vanehold and warns of paid disruption/intimidation of shipments, possibly organized by someone with regional access to information. The coffer contains records of names, payments, and correspondence involving merchants and minor lords between the Stormlands and Crownlands. The attackers targeted the coffer and did not appear interested in the letter. Ser Alester's Detect Evil result was negative; this does not establish his innocence or guilt.
- **Rumors and uncertain claims:** The letter writer believes someone with access to information from within the region is organizing the activity; this is a warning or assessment, not proven fact. Dadric's identification of the stag as Baratheon is based on his gathered knowledge; Ser Alester notes the colours do not match those he associates with House Baratheon. The attackers' identity, allegiance, and source of information remain unknown.
- **GM-only developments:** Do not treat Ser Alester's negative Detect Evil result as proof of innocence. The letter's contents are now known to Dadric and Edric because Ser Alester read them aloud / discussed them in their presence. Do not invent the identity of the letter's writer or intended visitor.
- **Combat and mechanical changes:** No combat. Detect Evil used on Ser Alester; no evil aura detected. No XP award or level change recorded.
- **Inventory, wealth, and resource changes:** Dadric's 1 gp shrine donation remains the only confirmed recent wealth change. The new contract pays 2 gp/day beginning today, but no wages have yet been handed over. Reasonable business travel expenses are covered. No additional recovery reward has been quantified. Spark and Edric's horse are stabled at Vanehold.
- **Consequences and unresolved threads:** Dadric is now employed by Ser Alester to investigate the theft and seek recovery of the coffer under strict moral boundaries. Immediate investigative leads include the Greyfield loading, the steward and stablehand, the route and ambush site, and the unknown letter writer / expected visitor. Ser Alester intends to question his steward. Edric is to remain available for testimony and receive separate compensation. Tomas is still recovering in Tallowmere.
- **Potential main-campaign impact:** Dadric has accepted a paid investigative role connected to regional political and commercial activity between the Stormlands and Crownlands.
- **Canon updates required:** No wider world-canon changes established. Treat all claims about the attackers and their affiliations as unverified.


### Session continuation — The Greyfield trail and the hunting lodge

- **In-world date and time:** 2 Harvestfall, 243 AC through the night; the party made camp and awoke at first light on 3 Harvestfall, 243 AC.
- **Location:** Greyfield countryside, creek crossing, ruined mill, woodland route, abandoned hunting lodge, and concealed woodland camp north of the lodge.
- **Character level / XP:** Level 1 paladin. No XP award or advancement recorded.
- **Opening state:** Dadric and Edric had camped in the woods near Greyfield after hiring Rowan, a local hunter/tracker. Dadric had 7 trail rations and 7 days of horse feed originally. One trail ration had been consumed at the first camp; six trail rations and six feed rations were later purchased on Ser Alester's account.
- **Events:**
  - Rowan tracked the brown-cloaked stranger north from Greyfield. He found a second set of footprints near a creek, suggesting the stranger had met another person.
  - Dadric called openly to two men concealed across the creek. Garrick admitted seeing the brown-cloaked stranger three mornings earlier. Dadric paid Garrick 2 gp from personal funds for information and agreed not to identify him.
  - Garrick said the stranger had been expected at the ruined mill, met a tall dark-haired rider in a grey cloak with a scar from left ear toward jaw, and argued with him about the failed ambush and the coffer not being where expected. The pair then rode north. Garrick described an old charcoal-burner's track beyond the mill leading toward a disused hunting lodge.
  - At the ruined mill Dadric met the scarred rider, who identified himself as Corren. Corren said the brown-cloaked man was called Merek, but was not the same Merek as the stablekeeper in Tallowmere. Corren claimed Merek had the coffer and had gone north toward an abandoned watchtower beyond the hunting lodge. Corren named Varrick as the person who arranged and paid for the ambush. These statements remain NPC claims, not verified facts.
  - Corren offered to guide the party and help recover the coffer in exchange for being protected from Varrick. Dadric accepted. A younger crossbowman named Hal was at the mill; his later whereabouts are unknown.
  - Corren led the party to an overlook of the hunting lodge. Rowan scouted and reported at least three men outside, with the number inside unknown. He identified a partly shuttered eastern window. Corren said a dog was sometimes kept at the rear entrance.
  - Dadric chose to approach at night to avoid bloodshed. Rowan opened the eastern shutters; Dadric climbed in unnoticed, followed by Rowan, Edric, and Corren. Rowan's dogs stayed outside.
  - Inside, two men were at a table, another man moved in an adjoining room, and footsteps were heard overhead. Dadric attempted to search for the coffer quietly. A floorboard creaked and one man turned toward the storage area.
  - Dadric revealed himself and said he was there for the coffer on Ser Alester Vane's behalf. He explicitly stated that he did not want to kill or harm anyone. The broad-shouldered man, called Harl during the scene, challenged Dadric and said Varrick would not let anyone walk away with the chest.
  - Dadric threatened the men, saying they should consider what he could do if only one man had been sent, and that if they refused to cooperate they might not live to enjoy another drunken night. Harl backed down. Dadric asked which way Merek had gone.
  - Harl said Merek had taken the coffer north along the old charcoal-burner's track toward the watchtower, and that others were expected to meet him farther along. A second man said the tower was about half a day's travel away.
  - Dadric challenged the apparent timing contradiction: the men had said the chest was to be moved before dawn, but Harl said Merek had left the previous day. Harl clarified that Merek had gone ahead with the coffer, while the lodge men were to hold the lodge until others arrived before dawn and then follow. This is their explanation, not independently verified.
  - Dadric warned the men that if he saw them involved in criminal activity again he would make sure they faced prison, then left the lodge. The occupants did not attack or pursue the party.
  - The party moved away from the lodge and rested in a concealed woodland camp. The night passed without incident. At first light on 3 Harvestfall, Rowan prepared to resume tracking north.
- **Dadric's decisions and actions:** Paid Garrick 2 gp personally; agreed to protect Garrick's identity; accepted Corren's offer to guide the party; chose a night infiltration to reduce the risk of violence; entered the lodge quietly; disclosed his purpose rather than attacking; repeatedly stated he wanted no one killed or harmed; used a threat when the guards resisted; warned them to abandon criminal activity; withdrew and chose to rest rather than travel blindly at night.
- **NPCs encountered:** Garrick and his companion; Corren; Hal; Harl; other unnamed lodge occupants; Rowan; Edric. Rowan's two dogs remained outside the lodge. Dadric's horse Spark and Edric's horse were also outside.
- **Relationship changes:** Garrick accepted payment and asked not to be named. Corren is now accompanying the party as a guide, in exchange for help escaping Varrick's reach. Harl yielded information under threat but did not become an ally.
- **Information learned by Dadric:** The brown-cloaked man is called Merek; he reportedly has the coffer and has gone north toward the watchtower. The watchtower is described as about half a day's travel beyond the lodge. The lodge men expected others to arrive before dawn. Corren and Harl both named Varrick in connection with the operation, but their claims are not independently verified.
- **Rumors and uncertain claims:** Garrick's account of the meeting at the mill; Corren's account of Varrick, Merek, and the ambush; Harl's account of Merek's departure and the planned movement before dawn. The lodge's timing explanation reconciles the statements as reported by the NPCs but is not confirmed independently.
- **GM-only developments:** The number and identity of all lodge occupants remain uncertain. Footsteps were heard overhead. A third man was moving in an adjoining room. Rowan had earlier seen at least three men outside. Do not assume the party identified or accounted for every person at the lodge.
