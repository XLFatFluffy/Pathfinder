# Religion Records

The detailed campaign reference is [Religion, Faith, and Religious Institutions](Religion_and_Faith.md).

Use Pathfinder First Edition's established deities, domains, portfolios, and traditions as the mechanical and cultural foundation unless a later decision changes this.

**Core established mystery:** the gods' actual existence is genuinely uncertain in-world. Divine magic exists mechanically but does not settle the question.

Track institutions, doctrines, orders, holy sites, texts, schisms, public practices, and political influence. Do not resolve the source of divine magic or the existence of gods without explicit GM approval. Distinguish religious claims, institutional doctrine, popular belief, and objective GM knowledge.
