# Hideout Functional Mechanics and Advancement

**Status:** APPROVED GM MECHANICS  
**Visibility:** GM ONLY  
**Applies to:** Current campaign  
**Ruleset:** Pathfinder First Edition

## Purpose

This document converts the approved Hideout and Base Development System from a construction framework into a functioning campaign subsystem.

The player-facing document intentionally does not reveal these mechanical benefits. Players know what a facility is intended to do, but the exact advantages, unlocks, and upgrade paths remain GM-facing.

The design is inspired by Pathfinder 1e's existing downtime-building philosophy, where specialized rooms can provide narrow skill benefits and practical capabilities rather than merely existing as scenery. The published system, for example, gives a Magical Repository bonuses to Knowledge (arcana) and Spellcraft research and an Alchemy Lab the equivalent of specialized tools. citeturn581164view0

## Core Rules

### 1. Development Points are not gold

Base Development Points represent organizational development capacity.

Gold represents the actual economic cost of making that development real.

A Development Point unlocks a facility or improvement. The PCs still pay reasonable costs for construction, materials, labor, equipment, staffing, and acquisition.

### 2. Development Points are never purchased directly with gold

Gold can buy an existing facility, property, equipment, or construction work, but it does not create a new Development Point.

This keeps the two progression tracks meaningful:

- **Development Points:** what the organization is capable of becoming.
- **Gold:** what the organization can currently afford to build, maintain, equip, and operate.

### 3. Standard facility bonuses

Unless a facility has a special rule below:

- A standard 1-point facility gives a narrow **+2 circumstance bonus** to one relevant activity when used for that activity.
- A standard 2-point facility gives either a broader +2 bonus or a narrow capability/unlock in addition to a practical benefit.
- A 3-point facility generally provides a stronger capability, a +2 bonus in multiple closely related activities, or access to an activity that cannot reasonably be performed without it.
- A 4-point facility is a major strategic asset and should provide a significant capability rather than simply a larger skill bonus.

Base facilities do not grant blanket bonuses to every use of a skill.

### 4. Bonuses do not stack with themselves

Multiple copies of the same facility do not automatically create unlimited stacking bonuses.

Where several facilities support the same activity, use the best facility bonus and apply any explicitly stated synergy benefit.

This prevents the PCs from building twelve libraries and becoming omniscient through architecture.

### 5. Facilities work when actually usable

A room must be:

- intact;
- appropriately equipped;
- accessible;
- supplied when supplies are required;
- staffed when staffing is required.

A destroyed, abandoned, inaccessible, or stripped facility does not provide its normal benefit.

### 6. Facilities can unlock better goods

Some facilities allow the PCs to purchase, craft, research, or commission things that are normally difficult or impossible to obtain.

The facility does not create the item for free.

Availability still depends on:

- money;
- materials;
- relevant skill or spellcasting;
- settlement;
- contacts;
- time;
- rarity;
- GM approval.

## Development Point Advancement

### Level advancement

The PCs gain **1 Base Development Point whenever the party gains a character level**.

This is the baseline advancement.

The point represents the party having become more capable of organizing, financing, protecting, and expanding its base.

### Major campaign accomplishments

The party may also gain **1–3 additional Development Points** for major accomplishments that materially increase the organization's long-term capacity.

Examples:

- acquiring a substantial property;
- securing a new source of valuable materials;
- establishing a meaningful trade route;
- recruiting an exceptional specialist;
- establishing a durable intelligence network;
- securing territory;
- gaining a powerful patron or political ally;
- recovering a significant magical resource;
- completing a major construction project;
- defeating a threat that materially endangered the organization;
- completing a major campaign objective.

The award should reflect the **lasting organizational value** of the accomplishment, not merely how difficult the encounter was.

### No routine XP-to-points conversion

Experience points are not separately converted into Development Points.

The normal rule is one point per character level.

### Gold cannot buy Development Points

A player cannot say, "We have 20,000 gp, so we buy two more points."

Instead, they can spend that gold to:

- build something;
- upgrade something;
- buy an existing facility;
- hire workers;
- hire specialists;
- acquire equipment;
- acquire property;
- repair damage;
- establish supplies;
- pay for information or contacts.

### Acquired property

When the PCs acquire an existing building or property, the GM may assign it Development Point value.

Examples:

- an abandoned tower might contain a Tower facility;
- an inherited estate might contain several Living and Property facilities;
- a purchased workshop might already contain a Workshop;
- a captured fortified structure might contain Security facilities.

The PCs do not automatically receive the full theoretical value of the property as free Development Points. The GM evaluates the usable facilities actually present.

### Development Point reserve

Unused Development Points may be saved indefinitely.

They are not lost because the PCs do not immediately spend them.

# Facility Mechanics

## Living and Household

### Private Quarters — 1 point

Functional benefit:

- Provides genuinely private living space.
- When used for normal rest, the occupants gain a +2 circumstance bonus on saves against environmental discomfort caused by poor sleeping conditions.
- Counts as suitable private quarters when an NPC, guest, or specialist requires them.

Campaign function:

- Helps establish the base as a legitimate residence rather than a temporary camp.
- Can be upgraded into more secure or luxurious quarters later.

### Guest Quarters — 1 point

Functional benefit:

- Allows one or more guests to stay without sharing PC sleeping space.
- Provides a +2 circumstance bonus to Diplomacy checks made specifically to host and accommodate a guest who is already friendly or neutral.

Campaign function:

- Makes recruitment, diplomacy, and temporary alliances easier to support.

### Servant/Retainer Quarters — 1 point

Functional benefit:

- Provides appropriate accommodation for up to four ordinary retainers.
- Retainers housed here can remain at the base without displacing the PCs.

Campaign function:

- Supports future staffing.

### Expanded Living Space — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- Increases the base's comfortable residential capacity.
- Supports up to ten ordinary residents beyond the PCs without treating the base as overcrowded.

Campaign function:

- Required before the base can comfortably become a small household or organization.

# Security

### Reinforced Doors — 1 point

Functional benefit:

- Exterior doors are reinforced.
- Defenders receive +2 on checks made to resist ordinary forced entry through those doors.

Does not alter the normal statistics of a door when Pathfinder's object rules specifically apply.

### Reinforced Structure — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- The building is substantially harder to compromise.
- +2 circumstance bonus to relevant checks made to detect or exploit structural weaknesses.

Campaign function:

- Reduces the consequences of ordinary break-ins and environmental damage.

### Defensive Position — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- Choose the dominant defensive feature when the location is established.
- Defenders gain +2 circumstance bonus on initiative checks during an attack that begins within the base or its immediate approaches.

Campaign function:

- The location itself favors the defenders.

### Hidden Entrance — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- Creates a concealed normal route into the base.
- +4 circumstance bonus to the Stealth or Perception DC involved in discovering the entrance, as appropriate.

Campaign function:

- Provides an alternate approach that does not advertise the base.

### Secret Escape Route — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- Provides one concealed emergency exit.
- The route can be used to escape the base without using the primary entrance.

Campaign function:

- Its value is primarily positional rather than numerical.

### Multiple Escape Routes — 3 points

Functional benefit:

- Provides at least two independent emergency exits.
- During a base emergency, the PCs can choose a route that avoids a known obstruction or threat.

Campaign function:

- Makes complete containment substantially harder.

### Secure Vault — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- Provides a protected room for valuables and sensitive objects.
- +4 circumstance bonus to checks made by outsiders to locate concealed valuables inside the vault.
- The vault can be fitted with mundane or magical locks purchased separately.

### Hidden Cache — 1 point

Functional benefit:

- Creates a concealed storage location separate from the main living area.
- +2 circumstance bonus to conceal or protect a small stored cache.

### Watch Post — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- Provides a controlled observation position.
- Defenders gain +2 circumstance bonus on Perception checks to notice approaching creatures or activity in the post's assigned field of view.

### Defensive Emplacements — 3 points

Functional benefit:

- Provides prepared positions for ranged weapons, defenders, or other suitable defensive equipment.
- Defenders gain +2 circumstance bonus on attacks made from prepared positions during an attack on the base.

Equipment itself must still be purchased.

---

# Secrecy and Concealment

### Concealed Location — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- The base is difficult to find by ordinary travel and observation.
- Creatures actively searching for the base take a +4 circumstance increase to the relevant DC to locate it unless they possess specific information pointing them toward it.

### False Identity / Cover — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- The property has a believable public identity.
- +2 circumstance bonus on Bluff or Disguise checks directly connected to maintaining the property's cover.

### Concealed Interior — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- Visitors can enter the public-facing portion without automatically seeing the organization's private facilities.
- +4 circumstance bonus to checks to notice the concealed interior arrangement.

### Magical Concealment — 3 points

Functional benefit:

- The base contains magical measures intended to resist magical observation.
- The PCs gain a +2 circumstance bonus on relevant checks made to resist or identify magical attempts to locate or observe the base.

This does not defeat divination automatically and does not replace a spell's normal rules.

### Misdirection — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- Evidence around the base can support a deliberately misleading explanation.
- +2 circumstance bonus on Bluff checks to maintain the established false explanation.

# Arcane Facilities

### Arcane Study — 1 point

Functional benefit:

- Counts as an appropriate workspace for arcane study.
- +2 circumstance bonus on Knowledge (arcana) and Spellcraft checks made while conducting ordinary research in the room.

### Spell Laboratory — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- Provides specialized equipment for spell analysis and experimentation.
- +2 circumstance bonus on Spellcraft checks involving spell research, identification, or controlled experimentation performed in the laboratory.
- Unlocks advanced spell experimentation that would be impractical in an ordinary bedroom or common room.

### Magical Library — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- Provides a curated collection of magical texts.
- +2 circumstance bonus on Knowledge (arcana) checks involving subjects represented in the library.
- Allows the GM to introduce access to uncommon but non-unique spells, techniques, and references through research.

It does not automatically contain any particular spell.

### Restricted Library — 3 points

Functional benefit:

- Contains material too dangerous, rare, or specialized for an ordinary magical library.
- +2 circumstance bonus on research involving obscure arcane or forbidden subjects.
- Provides a pathway to discovering uncommon magical knowledge.

The actual contents remain campaign discoveries.

### Magical Workshop — 3 points

Functional benefit:

- Counts as appropriate specialized equipment for magical item work.
- +2 circumstance bonus on relevant Spellcraft or Craft checks made for magical item creation or modification when the character already qualifies to perform that work.

The normal Pathfinder prerequisites and costs still apply.

### Ritual Chamber — 3 points

Functional benefit:

- Provides a controlled space for extended magical rituals.
- +2 circumstance bonus on checks associated with noncombat magical rituals conducted in the chamber.
- Reduces the risk of ordinary environmental interruption during a ritual.

It does not create new rituals by itself.

### Magical Observatory — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- Provides instruments for studying celestial, magical, and planar phenomena.
- +2 circumstance bonus on Knowledge (arcana), Knowledge (planes), or Spellcraft checks when the observation is relevant to the equipment available.

### Permanent Magical Feature — 4 points

Functional benefit:

- Establishes one significant magical feature.
- Its exact power is determined by the GM and becomes part of the campaign's discoverable magic.

It may provide a persistent benefit, a source of research, a resource, a mystery, or a risk.

# Necromantic Facilities

### Necromantic Laboratory — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- +2 circumstance bonus on Knowledge (religion) and Spellcraft checks specifically involving necromancy.
- Provides appropriate equipment for necromantic experimentation.

### Corpse Storage — 1 point

Functional benefit:

- Provides secure, sanitary, concealed storage for corpses.
- Properly stored corpses remain usable for necromantic purposes for twice the normal period before ordinary decomposition becomes a significant problem.

It does not preserve corpses indefinitely.

### Anatomical Laboratory — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- +2 circumstance bonus on Heal checks and relevant Knowledge (nature), Knowledge (religion), or Heal checks involving anatomy, death, or bodily investigation.
- Provides tools for anatomical research.

### Undead Containment Room — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- Provides controlled containment for undead creatures.
- +2 circumstance bonus to checks made to prevent an undead creature housed there from escaping through ordinary physical means.
- The room can be fitted with additional restraints, wards, and locks.

### Necromantic Ritual Chamber — 3 points

Functional benefit:

- +2 circumstance bonus on necromancy-related ritual checks.
- Reduces interference from ordinary environmental factors during controlled necromantic work.

### Soul/Spirit Research Chamber — 3 points

Functional benefit:

- +2 circumstance bonus on Knowledge (religion), Knowledge (planes), and Spellcraft checks involving souls, spirits, death, or related supernatural phenomena.
- Enables deeper investigation into subjects that cannot be adequately studied in a normal laboratory.

This facility does not establish the setting's ultimate metaphysics.

# Stealth, Ninja, and Intelligence Facilities

### Training Room — 1 point

Functional benefit:

- +2 circumstance bonus on one chosen physical skill while actively practicing it in the room.
- The chosen skill can be changed after a week of retraining.

### Weapons Practice Area — 1 point

Functional benefit:

- Provides a safe environment for weapons practice.
- After at least 1 hour of practice, the character gains +1 circumstance bonus on the next attack roll made with a practiced weapon during a training exercise, not a normal combat.

Campaign function:

- Primarily supports training and NPC development rather than granting combat power for free.

### Infiltration Training Area — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- +2 circumstance bonus on Stealth, Disable Device, or Acrobatics checks made as part of controlled infiltration practice.
- Allows the PCs to train others in basic infiltration techniques.

### Hidden Training Room — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- Concealed training space.
- +4 circumstance bonus to attempts to notice the room's existence from outside.

### Equipment Workshop — 1 point

Functional benefit:

- Counts as appropriate mundane tools for maintaining specialized infiltration equipment.
- +2 circumstance bonus on relevant Craft or Disable Device checks involving the equipment being maintained.

### Disguise Room — 1 point

Functional benefit:

- Provides mirrors, lighting, clothing storage, cosmetics, prosthetics, and similar mundane resources.
- +2 circumstance bonus on Disguise checks when the character has sufficient time to prepare in the room.

### Intelligence Room — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- Organized space for maps, reports, observations, and planning.
- +2 circumstance bonus on Knowledge (local) and relevant Intelligence checks when using information stored in the room.

### Shadow-Touched Chamber — 3 points

Functional benefit:

- Creates a stable location for investigation of shadow-related phenomena.
- +2 circumstance bonus on relevant Knowledge (planes), Spellcraft, and Perception checks involving an identifiable shadow phenomenon in the chamber.
- May unlock campaign-specific discoveries.

It does not automatically provide planar travel, spells, powers, or a connection to the Shadow Plane.

# Crafting and Production

### General Workshop — 1 point

Functional benefit:

- Counts as masterwork artisan's tools for one selected Craft skill.
- The selected Craft can be changed after a week of retraining.

### Smithy — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- Counts as a properly equipped smithy for metalworking.
- +2 circumstance bonus on Craft checks involving metal weapons, armor, tools, or similar goods.

### Alchemy Laboratory — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- Counts as an alchemist's lab.
- +2 circumstance bonus on Craft (alchemy) and related alchemical research checks.
- Provides access to better-quality mundane alchemical production than an improvised workspace.

### Herbalist/Garden — 1 point

Functional benefit:

- Provides a reliable controlled source of common medicinal and alchemical plants appropriate to the local environment.
- +2 circumstance bonus on relevant Profession (herbalist), Survival, or Craft (alchemy) checks when using plants grown there.

Rare plants still require separate acquisition.

### Advanced Workshop — 3 points

Functional benefit:

- Counts as high-quality tools for one selected Craft or Profession.
- +2 circumstance bonus on the selected skill.
- Can support more complex work that an ordinary workshop cannot reasonably perform.

### Specialized Workshop — 3 points

Functional benefit:

- Choose a specialty when constructed.
- Counts as specialized high-quality tools for that specialty.
- +2 circumstance bonus on relevant checks.
- May unlock access to uncommon mundane equipment associated with the specialty.

# Storage

### General Storage — 1 point

Functional benefit:

- Increases organized storage capacity.
- Reduces the likelihood of ordinary spoilage, loss, or misplacement.

### Armory — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- Securely stores common weapons and armor.
- Provides enough basic common equipment to outfit a small group of ordinary guards or retainers if the PCs have actually acquired those people.

The equipment is not free.

### Treasury — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- Securely stores coin, valuables, records, and portable wealth.
- +2 circumstance bonus to checks made to detect tampering with stored valuables.

### Evidence/Document Archive — 1 point

Functional benefit:

- +2 circumstance bonus on Knowledge (local), Knowledge (history), or related checks made using documents stored in the archive.
- Makes organized long-term recordkeeping practical.

### Contraband Vault — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- Provides unusually secure storage for illegal, dangerous, or socially sensitive objects.
- +4 circumstance bonus to attempts by outsiders to locate objects concealed in the vault.

# Medical

### Basic Infirmary — 1 point

Functional benefit:

- Counts as an appropriate medical workspace.
- +2 circumstance bonus on Heal checks made while treating patients in the infirmary.

### Advanced Infirmary — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- +2 circumstance bonus on Heal checks and disease or injury treatment performed in the infirmary.
- Supports treatment of more serious injuries than a basic field station.

### Surgical Room — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- +2 circumstance bonus on Heal checks involving surgery or complex physical treatment.
- Reduces the risk of interruption during procedures.

### Magical Healing Facility — 3 points

Functional benefit:

- Provides a dedicated environment for divine or magical healing.
- +2 circumstance bonus on relevant Heal checks when magical healing is being administered.
- Can safely support repeated magical treatment sessions.

It does not generate spells, healing, or spell slots.

# Personnel and Organization

### Guard Quarters — 1 point

Functional benefit:

- Houses a small number of guards or retainers.
- Properly housed guards are less likely to suffer fatigue or morale problems caused by inadequate living conditions.

### Stable — 1 point

Functional benefit:

- Houses up to four ordinary mounts with appropriate care.
- Provides normal stable services and storage.

### Kennels — 1 point

Functional benefit:

- Houses trained animals.
- +2 circumstance bonus on Handle Animal checks involving animals housed and trained there.

### Servant Facilities — 1 point

Functional benefit:

- Supports cooking, cleaning, laundry, and ordinary household maintenance.
- Reduces routine labor demands on the PCs.

### Barracks — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- Provides organized housing for a small guard or worker force.
- Supports up to ten ordinary personnel.

### Training Yard — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- Allows organized physical and combat training.
- +2 circumstance bonus on relevant training checks made by NPCs or PCs during structured practice.

### Command Room — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- +2 circumstance bonus on Knowledge (local), Profession (soldier), or relevant organizational planning checks when the room's maps, records, and personnel are used.
- Required for the base to efficiently coordinate larger operations.

# Intelligence Network

### Listening Post — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- Establishes a local observation position.
- When staffed, provides one regular stream of local information appropriate to the location.

It does not provide automatic truth or omniscience.

### Contact Network — 3 points

Functional benefit:

- Establishes several reliable social contacts.
- Once per settlement, the PCs may gain a +2 circumstance bonus on one Diplomacy or Gather Information effort where the network plausibly applies.

The contacts have motives and limits.

### Information Archive — 1 point

Functional benefit:

- +2 circumstance bonus on checks made to retrieve or cross-reference information the organization already possesses.

### Spy Operations Room — 3 points

Functional benefit:

- Allows organized intelligence operations to be planned and tracked.
- +2 circumstance bonus on relevant checks to coordinate surveillance, infiltration, or information gathering.

It does not create spies.

### Communications Room — 2 points

Functional benefit:

- Provides secure mundane communication infrastructure appropriate to the era and location.
- Reduces normal communication delays for messages handled through the organization's network.
- Can support magical communication if the PCs separately acquire the required magic.

- Can support small-scale gatherings and ordinary work.

Functional benefit:

Functional benefit:

Functional benefit:

- +4 circumstance bonus on Perception checks made from the tower's observation position.

# Special Features

### Ancient Ruin — 2 points

Mechanical benefit:

- None guaranteed at creation.
- The structure may contain usable ancient construction, hidden spaces, inscriptions, or magical evidence.

The GM determines what survives.

### Strange Well / Spring — 2 points

Mechanical benefit:

- Provides a reliable local water source if physically possible.
- May possess unusual properties discovered through play.

No magical effect is guaranteed.

### Ancient Ward — 3 points

Mechanical benefit:

- Provides a persistent protective feature.
- The exact protection is discovered through investigation.

### Hidden Chamber — 2 points

Mechanical benefit:

- Provides a concealed chamber.
- +4 circumstance bonus to checks to discover the chamber from outside its entrance.

### Unexplained Magical Feature — 3 points

Mechanical benefit:

- Creates one persistent magical mystery.
- The effect may eventually provide a benefit, resource, danger, or discovery.

# Facility Synergies

Synergies improve the facility rather than granting another separate room.

## Arcane Research Center

Requires:

- Arcane Study
- Magical Library
- Spell Laboratory

Benefits:

- The normal Arcane Study and Magical Library bonuses increase by +1 when used together.
- Research involving spells gains a further +1 circumstance bonus.
- The center can serve as a legitimate base for advanced magical research.

## Necromancer's Sanctum

Requires:

- Arcane Study
- Necromantic Laboratory
- Corpse Storage
- Ritual Chamber

Benefits:

- Necromancy research gains +1 circumstance bonus.
- Necromantic ritual checks gain +1.
- The sanctum can safely support extended necromantic experimentation.

## Ninja Headquarters

Requires:

- Training Room
- Intelligence Room
- Hidden Entrance
- Disguise Room

Benefits:

- Stealth, disguise, and intelligence activities performed as part of a coordinated operation gain +1 circumstance bonus.
- The base can support training for infiltrators.

## Secure Headquarters

Requires:

- Reinforced Structure
- Hidden Entrance
- Secret Escape Route
- Secure Vault

Benefits:

- Security-related bonuses from these facilities each increase by +1 where applicable.
- The base becomes substantially harder to compromise through a single point of failure.

## Growing Stronghold

Requires:

- Barracks
- Training Yard
- Command Room
- Defensive Wall

Benefits:

- Organized defenders operating from the stronghold gain +1 on relevant defensive checks.
- The base can support a larger organized force without the PCs personally supervising every routine activity.

# Facility Upgrades

Facilities can be improved later.

A facility may have:

- **Basic**
- **Advanced**
- **Exceptional**

development tiers.

An upgrade normally costs:

- **1 additional Development Point** for Basic → Advanced;
- **2 additional Development Points** for Advanced → Exceptional;
- plus appropriate gold, materials, labor, time, and access.

The GM decides whether a particular facility has all three tiers.

Advanced facilities should generally improve an existing benefit, unlock a new capability, or allow access to better equipment.

Exceptional facilities should represent a major campaign accomplishment and should rarely be available merely because the PCs have gold.

---

# Base Damage and Recovery

Facilities can be damaged, disabled, occupied, contaminated, or destroyed through play.

A damaged facility does not provide its normal benefit until repaired.

Repair costs depend on the damage.

As a baseline:

- minor damage: 10% of the facility's construction cost;
- serious damage: 25%;
- major damage: 50%;
- destroyed: 100%.

The GM may substitute labor, salvaged materials, favors, or other resources when appropriate.

---

# Maintenance

The base does not require a universal monthly gold tax.

Instead, maintenance is handled through the actual campaign:

- food and supplies are consumed;
- guards and servants require wages;
- animals require feed and care;
- laboratories require materials;
- magical facilities may require components;
- damaged structures require repair;
- larger organizations create larger recurring expenses.

A small empty hideout can therefore remain cheap.

A heavily staffed fortress with undead laboratories, guards, horses, workshops, spies, and political operations should become expensive.

This follows the useful principle in Pathfinder's downtime rules that buildings and organizations become meaningful economic structures rather than merely decorative maps. citeturn581164view0

# Recommended Development Pace

Starting at the campaign's intended low organizational power:

- **Starting:** 20 Development Points
- **Each character level gained:** +1 point
- **Major campaign accomplishment:** +1 to +3 points
- **Major territorial or organizational expansion:** +1 to +3 points
- **Ordinary quest completion:** normally 0 points unless it materially changes the organization's capabilities

This should produce a slow but visible progression.

The PCs should normally feel that a new point is valuable.

---

# GM Design Principle

A facility should answer at least one of these questions:

1. What can the PCs do here that they could not reasonably do elsewhere?
2. What can they do here better?
3. What can they acquire here that would otherwise be difficult to obtain?
4. What campaign information can they uncover here?
5. What kinds of people can this facility support?
6. What future development does this facility make possible?

If the answer to all six is "it is a room with furniture," the facility does not need to cost a Development Point.

**APPROVED:** The PCs gain 1 Development Point per character level gained.

**APPROVED:** Major accomplishments can award 1–3 additional Development Points when they create lasting organizational value.

**APPROVED:** Gold cannot directly purchase Development Points.

**APPROVED:** Facility bonuses are narrow, contextual, and subject to normal Pathfinder prerequisites and rules.
