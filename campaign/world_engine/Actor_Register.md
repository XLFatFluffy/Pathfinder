# Living World — Actor Register

**Status:** Index and recording standard  
**Campaign start:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC

The engine tracks people and institutions as actors. This register is an index, not permission to invent motives or resources.

## Actor record minimum fields

- **Actor ID and name**
- **Type and canon status**
- **Leader / decision process**
- **Public goals**
- **Private goals:** GM-only, only if established
- **Interests and red lines**
- **Resources and constraints:** qualitative unless supported by evidence
- **Territory, holdings, jurisdiction, and rights**
- **Obligations, lieges, vassals, clients, and dependents**
- **Allies, rivals, and current relationships**
- **Current issues and projects**
- **Knowledge, beliefs, suspicions, and misinformation**
- **Likely actions and conditions that would change them**
- **Public visibility and PC knowledge**
- **Source records and last reviewed**

## Important distinctions

- A named house is not automatically a fully simulated actor.
- A title does not establish effective control; control does not automatically establish lawful legitimacy.
- A commercial rival is not automatically a political enemy.
- A charter relationship is not automatically feudal vassalage.
- A local customary right is not automatically territorial sovereignty.
- Unknown values remain UNKNOWN. Do not invent numerical strength ratings.
