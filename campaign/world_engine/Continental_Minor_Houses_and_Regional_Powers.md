# Continental Minor Houses and Regional Powers

**Snapshot:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC  
**Status:** Newly established campaign canon, created under the GM's explicit delegation to develop minor houses, NPCs, and factions.  
**Scope:** First continental layer beyond the six Great Houses and the established Tallowmere network.  
**Map status:** All holdings are regional descriptions only. No exact borders, coordinates, road traces, or political polygons are established here.

This document creates a manageable first network of lesser powers. These actors are locally consequential but do not rival the Great Houses. They have interests, obligations, and limits; they are not a continent-wide alliance.

## 1. Design rules

- Minor houses are wealthy local lords with modest territories, household retainers, and influence over nearby settlements or resources.
- Banner houses owe allegiance to a Great House. Their local autonomy varies by charter and custom.
- Crown vassals owe direct allegiance to the Crown, not automatically to the nearest Great House.
- Independent lords and southern powers are not assumed to owe fealty to a Great House.
- A house's leader, heir, and internal policy differences are established below. Other family members, troop counts, exact income, and private secrets remain unassigned until needed.
- These houses may be replaced, merged, or revised by the GM without disturbing the six Great Houses' established identities.
- No actor receives a precise army size, treasury, or territorial border from this document.

## 2. Banner Houses of the Northern Marches

### House Coldmere — TLM? / VYR-ACT-001

**Type:** Minor noble banner house  
**Liege:** House Veyr  
**Holding:** A small estate and grazing lands on the settled southern approaches to the Northern Holds; associated with the supply road serving Wintermere.  
**Seat:** Coldmere Hall  
**Head:** Lady Ysra Coldmere  
**Heir:** Ser Toman Coldmere

**Position and interests**
- Maintain the estate's grazing, winter stores, and horse-breeding income.
- Keep the southern supply road passable through the first hard frosts.
- Preserve the family's traditional service obligations without allowing emergency requisitions to become permanent.
- Secure predictable compensation when the Northern Watch requisitions food, animals, or wagons.

**Resources and limits:** Modest pasture, livestock, stores, a small household guard, and practical knowledge of winter supply. The house cannot independently defend a frontier district or refuse a lawful Veyr summons without consequences.

**Relationships:** Loyal to Veyr in principle; practical friction with Watch quartermasters over requisition and repayment. No feud with another banner house is established.

**Current status:** WATCH. Seasonal preparation is normal and no supply crisis is established.

### House Pineward — VYR-ACT-002

**Type:** Minor noble banner house  
**Liege:** House Veyr  
**Holding:** Woodland and small agricultural holdings in the Pine Marches, with commercial ties to Pinecross.  
**Seat:** Pineward Lodge  
**Head:** Lord Edric Pineward  
**Heir:** Lady Mara Pineward

**Position and interests**
- Preserve customary timber and charcoal rights.
- Supply timber, pitch, and winter fuel to nearby settlements and frontier posts.
- Resist overcutting that would damage future yields.
- Maintain local authority over woodcutters and charcoal burners.

**Resources and limits:** Woodland rents, charcoal contracts, a modest retinue, and local knowledge. The house depends on buyers and cannot dictate regional prices.

**Relationships:** Veyr expects useful supplies; nearby woodcutters and customary users expect access. Pineward's authority does not erase customary rights.

**Current status:** WATCH. No specific timber-rights lawsuit or illegal cutting operation is established.

## 3. Banner Houses of the Western Highlands

### House Aethwyn — VAE-ACT-001

**Type:** Minor elven noble banner house  
**Liege:** House Vaelorian  
**Holding:** An old woodland estate and several small tenant communities in the Green Valleys, away from Vaelorian's most valuable directly controlled mines.  
**Seat:** Aethwyn Court  
**Head:** Lady Thalira Aethwyn  
**Heir:** Lord Eryndor Aethwyn

**Position and interests**
- Preserve ancestral estate privileges and customary woodland management.
- Protect the family's standing among older elven lineages.
- Resist commercial consolidation that would turn customary use into exclusive leases.
- Maintain influence through scholarship, patronage, and local arbitration rather than military force.

**Resources and limits:** Old land rights, woodland income, family reputation, educated retainers, and ties to local scholars. The house has no claim to Vaelorian's major mines or independent foreign policy.

**Relationships:** Formally loyal to Theren Vaelorian. Its traditionalist outlook overlaps with some older elven families but does not make it a formal opposition faction. Seralyth Vaelorian's commercial reforms are viewed with caution.

**Current status:** WATCH. No organized revolt, legal petition, or secession movement exists.

## 4. Banner Houses of the Western Heartlands

### House Harrowden — EDA-ACT-001

**Type:** Minor landed banner house  
**Liege:** House Edarth  
**Holding:** Grain farms, pasture, and a modest fortified manor in the Western Heartlands, within the wider agricultural network serving Harveston.  
**Seat:** Harrowden Hall  
**Head:** Lord Berold Harrowden  
**Heir:** Ser Alwyn Harrowden

**Position and interests**
- Protect grain production, tenant obligations, and access to local pasture.
- Preserve the family's traditional military service while avoiding unnecessary levies.
- Secure reliable road maintenance for grain carts.
- Maintain a reputation for dependable harvest deliveries.

**Resources and limits:** Productive farms, grain stores, draft animals, household retainers, and a small mounted household contingent. The house's prosperity depends on harvests and transport; it cannot act independently of Edarth in a major conflict.

**Relationships:** Edarth is liege. House Wrenford is a neighboring banner house and the opposing party in the Longmead boundary dispute below.

### House Wrenford — EDA-ACT-002

**Type:** Minor landed banner house  
**Liege:** House Edarth  
**Holding:** Mixed farms, pasture, and a small stretch of managed woodland adjoining Harrowden lands in the Western Heartlands.  
**Seat:** Wrenford House  
**Head:** Lady Cerys Wrenford  
**Heir:** Ser Merek Wrenford

**Position and interests**
- Preserve access to summer grazing and woodland resources.
- Maintain the family's customary rights along its eastern estate boundary.
- Avoid losing land or standing through an unfavorable arbitration.
- Keep the dispute with Harrowden from disrupting harvest labor and local trade.

**Resources and limits:** Mixed agricultural rents, woodland products, household retainers, and local tenant ties. The house is not strong enough to enforce a contested claim by force against Edarth's wishes.

**Relationships:** Edarth is liege. House Harrowden is a neighbor and current legal rival, not an inherited enemy.

### EDA-ISS-001 — The Longmead Boundary Dispute

**Status:** ACTIVE, LOW-INTENSITY JURISDICTIONAL DISPUTE  
**Location:** An unnamed pasture-and-woodland boundary between Harrowden and Wrenford holdings in the Western Heartlands. The precise location is deliberately not mapped.  
**Parties:** Lord Berold Harrowden and Lady Cerys Wrenford.  
**Liege authority:** Lord Garrick Edarth, whose arbitration has been requested informally but not yet formally heard.

**Established facts**
- A stream and old boundary markers define part of the customary division between the two holdings.
- Seasonal flooding and years of inconsistent maintenance have made the markers difficult to interpret.
- Both houses claim customary rights to a narrow strip of summer pasture and adjoining managed woodland.
- Each side has tenants who have used the area under different interpretations of the boundary.
- No armed clash, killing, raid, or formal seizure has occurred.
- Neither house has yet submitted a complete formal claim with mutually accepted evidence.

**Positions**
- Harrowden argues that its tenants have historically maintained and grazed the pasture.
- Wrenford argues that its customary woodland and grazing rights extend farther west than Harrowden acknowledges.
- Both houses want a favorable ruling but would prefer not to lose labor, harvest, or favor with Edarth.

**Unknown**
- The original charter wording, age and provenance of the markers, precise extent of the disputed strip, and whether earlier stewards knowingly altered or neglected the markers.
- Whether any tenant has deliberately moved a marker.
- Whether Edarth will hear the case directly or appoint an intermediary.

**Plausible paths**
- Joint survey and restoration of boundary markers.
- Temporary shared-use agreement for the current season.
- Formal arbitration by Edarth.
- A negotiated exchange of grazing or woodland rights.
- Continued low-level friction if neither side wants to pay for evidence or accept compromise.

**Escalation threshold:** Deliberate destruction of markers, eviction of tenants, seizure of livestock, or violence by retainers could turn the dispute into a serious feudal matter. None has happened at campaign start.

**Map rule:** This is a disputed customary boundary, not permission to draw overlapping territory polygons. Keep the area uncalibrated until authoritative regional artwork and a GM-approved boundary decision exist.

## 5. Crown Vassal of the Central Crownlands

### House Rookwell — CAL-ACT-001

**Type:** Minor Crown vassal house  
**Liege:** House Calderan / the Crown directly  
**Holding:** A compact estate and road-service rights in the central Crownlands, associated with the inland approaches to Kingsbridge.  
**Seat:** Rookwell Keep  
**Head:** Lord Mathen Rookwell  
**Heir:** Lady Elira Rookwell

**Position and interests**
- Preserve direct Crown-vassal status and avoid subordination to a neighboring Great House.
- Maintain the estate road, relay stables, and messenger hospitality expected under its service.
- Secure predictable royal compensation for extraordinary courier and transport duties.
- Gain a stronger voice in Crownland administration.

**Resources and limits:** Estate rents, relay stables, horses, local retainers, and a useful road-service network. Its influence depends on reliable service to the Crown; it has no independent right to direct royal policy.

**Relationships:** Loyal to the Crown, but protective of its direct status. It is not a member of a court faction by default.

**Current status:** WATCH. No current refusal of service or dispute with the Crown is established.

## 6. Dwarven clan actor of the Kharadorn sphere

### Clan Emberdelve — KHA-ACT-001

**Type:** Dwarven clan within the Kharadorn imperial union; not a separate Great House  
**Political relationship:** One of the independent clans united under the main branch of the dwarven empire.  
**Holding:** A subterranean mining and metalworking complex in the northeastern mountain system, within the wider Khar-Dum sphere. Exact location and extent remain undisclosed.  
**Clan Speaker:** Thane Orsik Emberdelve  
**Designated successor:** Halla Emberdelve

**Position and interests**
- Preserve clan autonomy in internal affairs.
- Maintain profitable mining and specialist metalworking contracts.
- Protect the clan's technical reputation and control over its own skilled labor.
- Resist outside demands that would expose restricted workings or methods.

**Resources and limits:** Skilled miners, smiths, engineers, subterranean holdings, and clan obligations within the imperial union. The clan is not a rival empire and does not independently control Dragonstone or the Inland Sea.

**Relationships:** Owes the established duties of a member clan to the Kharadorn imperial structure. The exact tribute, military, and labor obligations of individual clans remain unrecorded.

**Current status:** WATCH. No rebellion, succession crisis, or refusal of clan obligations is established.

## 7. Banner House of the Eastern Maritime Territory

### House Ternholt — MER-ACT-001

**Type:** Minor maritime banner house  
**Liege:** House Merrowen  
**Holding:** A modest coastal estate and fishing harbors in the Northern Coast region, with commercial ties to Eastwatch.  
**Seat:** Gullwatch Hall  
**Head:** Lady Sabine Ternholt  
**Heir:** Ser Owyn Ternholt

**Position and interests**
- Preserve local fishing rights and small-harbor privileges.
- Supply pilots, coastal craft, and provisions to larger shipping interests.
- Resist having local harbors treated as mere extensions of the great ports.
- Keep coastal defenses adequate without diverting too much income from trade.

**Resources and limits:** Fishing income, small craft, pilot knowledge, harbor dues, and a modest household guard. It has no fleet capable of challenging Merrowen's main naval power.

**Relationships:** Loyal to Merrowen, but protective of local maritime custom. Commercial cooperation with Vaelorian merchants is practical, not an alliance.

**Current status:** WATCH. No specific conflict with Merrowen or neighboring ports is established.

## 8. Southern independent powers

The following actors are deliberately independent of the six Great Houses. Their existence reflects the established political fragmentation of the Southern Reaches.

### House Serrane of Sablecross — STH-ACT-001

**Type:** Independent minor lordship  
**Holding:** A fortified well-town and surrounding dry-valley estates in the Southwestern Marches, associated with Sablecross.  
**Seat:** Sablecross Hall  
**Head:** Lady Amara Serrane  
**Heir:** Ser Dario Serrane

**Position and interests**
- Protect wells, cisterns, and caravan access.
- Maintain neutrality between larger southern powers when possible.
- Collect customary market and water dues without provoking neighboring communities.
- Preserve local autonomy and secure reliable trade in grain, salt, wool, and metal goods.

**Resources and limits:** Water rights, caravan services, modest defensive works, local retainers, and control of a useful stopping place. Its wealth is meaningful locally but does not make it a continental power.

**Relationships:** Independent. It has commercial dealings with multiple southern settlements; no formal treaty or feud is established.

**Current status:** WATCH. Water access is a recurring source of negotiation, not a declared conflict.

### The Brightwater Civic Compact — STH-ACT-002

**Type:** Independent city government / merchant and civic council  
**Location:** Brightwater, in the Southern Principalities.  
**Presiding First Speaker:** Ilyan Merrow  
**Council:** Seven elected or guild-appointed civic seats; the precise selection balance is locally contested and not treated as a modern democracy.

**Position and interests**
- Preserve Brightwater's chartered independence.
- Protect river and market commerce.
- Prevent any neighboring prince from turning temporary protection into permanent control.
- Maintain public works, market law, and a workable relationship with nearby rural lords.

**Resources and limits:** Market dues, river trade, civic offices, local watch, and merchant credit. The Compact can negotiate, hire limited protection, and impose city rules; it cannot field a major army or command the Southern Principalities.

**Relationships:** Independent. It trades with neighboring powers and is wary of dynastic claims that threaten its charter.

**Current status:** WATCH. No current siege, succession crisis, or external occupation is established.

### House Veylan of Marrowgate — STH-ACT-003

**Type:** Independent island lordship  
**Holding:** A small island territory and harbor rights associated with Marrowgate in the Southern Islands.  
**Seat:** Marrowgate House  
**Head:** Lord Cassian Veylan  
**Heir:** Lady Nerissa Veylan

**Position and interests**
- Protect harbor dues and local fishing rights.
- Keep island shipping open and avoid dependence on any single mainland patron.
- Balance local shipmasters against the hereditary rights of the house.
- Prevent piracy and private violence from making the harbor unsafe.

**Resources and limits:** Harbor access, fishing, pilotage, a small number of coastal vessels, and local retainers. The house cannot police the entire island chain.

**Relationships:** Independent. Maintains commercial ties with other island ports, including Duskport, but no formal alliance is established.

**Current status:** WATCH. Piracy and smuggling are regional possibilities, not confirmed current operations.

### The Duskport Shipmasters' Compact — STH-ACT-004

**Type:** Independent port association / civic faction  
**Location:** Duskport, Southern Islands.  
**Principal Speaker:** Captain Ysabet Korr  
**Membership:** Shipmasters, chandlers, harbor factors, and recognized port households.

**Position and interests**
- Preserve Duskport's open-port privileges.
- Keep harbor dues predictable and resist monopolies.
- Protect local shipmasters from outside seizure or arbitrary levies.
- Maintain enough common defense to discourage raiders without surrendering civic authority to a single lord.

**Resources and limits:** Commercial credit, shipping information, local vessels, and influence over port labor. It is not a unified navy or a sovereign power over neighboring islands.

**Relationships:** Practical commercial ties with Marrowgate and other island ports. Its relationship with House Veylan is cooperative but competitive.

**Current status:** WATCH. No blockade, piracy campaign, or port revolt is established.

## 10. Starting-state guardrails

At 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC:

- The Longmead boundary dispute is the only new named territorial/customary boundary dispute established by this document. It is local, low-intensity, and not an armed conflict.
- No Great House war, continental border war, naval blockade, assassination plot, or southern invasion is established.
- No new alliance among the Southern powers is established.
- No new house is placed on the map with exact borders or coordinates.
- No actor knows the vampire PC's identity or survival unless the player establishes otherwise.
- No actor knows the ratfolk PC survived the Relland incident unless the player reveals it or evidence emerges in play.
- The first world turn must evaluate the new actors' actual opportunities and actions. It must not automatically escalate the Longmead dispute or any WATCH item.
