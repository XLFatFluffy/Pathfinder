## Detailed project records

### CNT-PRJ-001 — Northern Watch autumn provisioning

**Sponsor:** House Veyr  
**Leads:** Lady Anika Veyr coordinates political and administrative arrangements; Lord Stellan Veyr reviews practical frontier needs.  
**Status:** UNDERWAY / seasonal.

**Objective:** Prepare the Northern Watch and associated frontier posts for the coming cold season by reviewing stores, transport, repair materials, and the timing of southern deliveries.

**Established context:** Veyr depends on southern grain and support. The Northern Watch is a permanent frontier force, supported by mobile Marcher Companies and the House Guard. The house's sparse settlement and supply dependence are established constraints.

**Unknown:** Actual reserve levels, quantities required, specific suppliers, contracts, route condition, delivery dates, and any shortfall. No shortage or frontier emergency is established.

**Likely pressures:** Competition for wagons and grain, weather delays, Crown or Edarth priorities, and the need to preserve local reserves.

**No automatic outcome:** The project may conclude with adequate stores, modest delays, negotiated support, or a documented shortfall. Do not invent a crisis before evidence exists.

### CNT-PRJ-002 — Edarth banner rolls and service review

**Sponsor:** House Edarth  
**Lead:** Ser Rowan Edarth, acting under Lord Garrick's authority.  
**Status:** UNDERWAY / administrative.

**Objective:** Consolidate records of sworn banner houses, customary service expectations, local disputes, and outstanding petitions. Rowan believes the decentralized structure of Edarth requires better political coordination.

**Established context:** Edarth relies on lesser landed families, agricultural production, heavy infantry, levies, and household cavalry. Rowan favors a more modern political strategy and careful management of lesser families. The exact number of sworn houses and their service obligations remain unknown.

**Known immediate relevance:** Houses Harrowden and Wrenford are both Edarth banner houses and are involved in the Longmead boundary dispute. Their case may appear in the review, but no formal hearing has been scheduled.

**Unknown:** The complete banner-house roster, actual service terms, record quality, and whether Garrick will authorize changes to custom.

**Likely pressures:** Lesser houses may welcome clearer obligations or fear tighter control. A review of records does not itself increase levies or change tenure.

### CNT-PRJ-003 — Crownland road and courier service review

**Sponsor:** House Calderan / Crown  
**Lead:** Lord Chancellor Othmar Rusk; House Rookwell supplies records concerning its road-service holding.  
**Status:** UNDERWAY / administrative.

**Objective:** Review the reliability of royal messengers, relay stables, road maintenance, and the obligations attached to Crownland service holdings.

**Established context:** The Crown seeks to strengthen administration through appointments and institutional improvements. House Rookwell holds a compact estate and road-service rights near the inland approaches to Kingsbridge.

**Unknown:** Which routes are included, the number and condition of relay stations, actual costs, any missed messages, and whether any service obligations are disputed.

**Likely pressures:** Rookwell may seek compensation for extraordinary duties; the Crown may seek more predictable service. Neither side is currently in breach.

### CNT-PRJ-004 — Western Pass commercial terms review

**Sponsor:** House Vaelorian  
**Lead:** Lady Seralyth Vaelorian  
**Status:** UNDERWAY / internal assessment.

**Objective:** Review trade access, route reliability, commercial guarantees, and the terms under which Vaelorian merchants and partners use the Western Passes.

**Established context:** Vaelorian's influence rests on finance, trade, contracts, mines, and commercial relationships. Seralyth is more adaptive and commercially ambitious than Theren. The Western Passes are an established regional feature.

**Unknown:** Specific contracts, route fees, merchants involved, any disputed guarantee, and whether the review will produce a proposal to another power.

**Likely pressures:** Older families may object if commercial arrangements reduce customary privileges. No specific debt, default, or political ultimatum is established.

### CNT-PRJ-005 — Eastern Coast pilotage and harbor-practice review

**Sponsor:** House Merrowen  
**Lead:** Lady Selene Merrowen  
**Status:** PROPOSED / information gathering.

**Objective:** Collect information from smaller ports and pilot houses about navigation practices, harbor dues, pilotage, and local coastal defense needs.

**Established context:** Merrowen protects eastern ports and shipping. Smaller banner houses such as Ternholt retain local customs and harbor interests. Selene is involved in trade negotiations and merchant interests.

**Unknown:** Whether Maris has formally authorized a policy review, which ports will respond, and whether the information will support central standards or greater local autonomy.

**Boundary:** This is not a naval mobilization or a challenge to Kharadorn's inland-sea control. No port has been ordered to change its dues.

### CNT-PRJ-006 — Brightwater river-market charter review

**Sponsor:** Brightwater Civic Compact  
**Lead:** Ilyan Cress  
**Status:** WATCH / routine review.

**Objective:** Review market and river-use rules to ensure the city's charter remains workable as trade patterns change.

**Established context:** Brightwater is an independent city in the Southern Principalities, governed by a civic and merchant compact. It protects its chartered independence and river commerce.

**Unknown:** Whether any specific rule is under review, the views of rural suppliers, and the positions of neighboring rulers.

**Boundary:** No external claimant, siege, or active charter crisis is established.

## Update rules

- Project progress changes only when time, resources, information, or decisions justify it.
- A review may produce no change.
- A proposal is not a funded or approved undertaking.
- Do not invent project budgets, deadlines, completion percentages, troop movements, or resource shortages.
- Record who knows about a project and whether it is public, private, or routine administration.
- A project may create a new issue, but only if an actual conflict or consequence emerges.
- These descriptions do not cause a project to advance. The GM determines any change from established events, resources, information, and decisions.
