# Diplomatic and Relationship Model

**Status:** Phase 2 World Engine model established
**Baseline:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC
**Execution:** GM-reviewed

## Purpose
Represent relationships as changing political conditions rather than fixed labels.

## Relationship dimensions
A relationship may include:
- trust
- cooperation
- rivalry
- dependency
- leverage
- obligation
- dispute
- treaty status
- marriage connection
- debt
- strategic alignment
- political tension

Not every relationship requires every dimension.

## Relationship state
Each record should identify:
- parties;
- current condition;
- established obligations;
- active pressure;
- recent cause;
- intended or likely response;
- visibility;
- source records.

Qualitative states are preferred unless a source establishes a numerical measure.

## Established relationships
- Calderan and Vaelorian: financial dependence and political leverage.
- Kharadorn and Merrowen: strategic competition.
- Edarth and Calderan: political tension over royal centralization.
- Veyr and Calderan: practical dependence and negotiation over northern support.
- Vaelorian and Merrowen: commercial cooperation and competition.
- Kharadorn and Calderan: uneasy formal relationship.
- Edarth and Veyr: military understanding.
- Edarth and Kharadorn: mutual respect.

These are baseline conditions, not permanent future outcomes.

## Diplomatic evaluation
**interest → obligation → leverage → information → opportunity → action → response**

The engine should distinguish:
- what an actor wants;
- what an actor can credibly demand;
- what an actor believes the other party will do;
- what the other party actually intends.

Unknown motives remain unknown.

## Escalation
Diplomatic tension can produce:
- negotiation;
- concession;
- delay;
- commercial pressure;
- appointment pressure;
- legal action;
- alliance building;
- military preparation;
- or armed conflict.

The engine must not skip intermediate steps without supporting causes.
