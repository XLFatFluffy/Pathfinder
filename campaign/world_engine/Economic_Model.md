# Economic Model

**Status:** Phase 2 World Engine model established
**Baseline:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC
**Execution:** GM-reviewed

## Purpose
Represent economic conditions as structured state without inventing unsupported production totals, prices, tax receipts, treasury values, or market volumes.

## Economic domains
- production
- food supply
- livestock
- labor
- major materials
- trade corridors
- ports and river transport
- taxation and toll pressure
- credit and debt pressure
- shortages and surpluses
- economic dependencies

## State representation
Each economic condition should identify:
- actor or location affected;
- good, service, or resource;
- condition;
- direction of pressure where relevant;
- known cause;
- dependencies;
- alternatives;
- affected projects or issues;
- information visibility;
- source records.

Qualitative states such as **stable**, **pressure**, **shortage**, **surplus**, **disrupted**, and **unknown** are preferred unless canon supplies a numerical value.

## Core rules
1. A poor harvest is not automatically a famine.
2. A shortage in one place does not automatically become a continental shortage.
3. Transport, storage, credit, substitution, and political decisions mediate economic effects.
4. Rights must remain distinct: ownership, tenancy, customary use, chartered market rights, toll rights, harbor rights, mineral rights, woodland rights, tax authority, and military protection.
5. Local prices are contextual, not universal constants.
6. Economic actors are not automatically unified factions.
7. Structural dependency is not the same as current crisis.

## Established baseline dependencies
- Veyr depends on grain, manufactured goods, and Crown support.
- Vaelorian depends on food, labor, and maritime access.
- Edarth depends on metals, manufactured goods, and reliable roads.
- Calderan depends on cooperation, revenue, and noble compliance.
- Kharadorn depends on food and surface markets.
- Merrowen depends on raw materials, food, and inland markets.

Major corridors include the Northern Road, Western Mountain Road, King's Road, Inland Sea Route, Eastern Coastal Road, and Southern Trade Road. Exact courses remain unresolved.

## Economic evaluation
When an economic change is relevant:

**condition → affected actors/locations → available alternatives → political response → consequence**

Do not create an event merely because a dependency exists.

## Unknowns
The engine must preserve:
- exact production quantities;
- exact prices;
- exact tax totals;
- exact treasury totals;
- exact market volumes;
- exact debt balances;
- exact route geometry.

The machine-readable state records economic pressure only when supported by current canon or an approved simulation decision.
