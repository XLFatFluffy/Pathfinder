# Global Transformation Framework

**Status:** Phase 3 framework established
**Baseline:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC
**Execution:** GM-reviewed

## Purpose

Provide a place for the campaign's eventual major transformation without deciding its nature, timing, or outcome in advance.

The campaign establishes that the world may eventually change substantially. The form of that change remains intentionally undetermined.

## What is established

- Dragons are real.
- The old dragon-riding dynasty and the twelve-dragon historical peak are established canon.
- The fate and future relevance of surviving dragons remain unresolved.
- The wider world is not necessarily fully known.
- Dothraki are established as orcs, known to the wider world, and feared.
- Detailed Dothraki lore remains future work.
- A substantial global transformation may eventually occur.

## What is not established

The framework does not define:

- the nature of the transformation;
- a trigger date;
- a predetermined villain or faction;
- a required supernatural explanation;
- a required military conflict;
- a required dragon return;
- a required discovery of new lands;
- a required outcome.

## Evaluation

The engine may monitor:

**accumulated conditions → emerging pressure → plausible triggers → materially different futures → fork**

Only when sufficient evidence exists should the engine create a major fork.

## GM control

The GM may approve, reject, defer, or permit engine resolution of a major fork.

No hidden final answer is stored merely to preserve a surprise.
