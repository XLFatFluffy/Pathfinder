# Great House Strategic and Roleplaying Dossiers — Legacy Descriptive Reference

**Snapshot:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC  
**Status:** Consolidation of established Great House canon. This file does not create new active plots, secret plans, or military ratings.

## House Calderan — Crown / Central Crownlands

**Ruler:** King Aldric Calderan  
**Recognized heir:** Crown Prince Edric Calderan  
**Other immediate political figure:** Princess Maerelle Calderan  
**Seat and capital:** Valedorn  
**Council representative:** Lord Chancellor Othmar Rusk  
**Court connections:** Queen Elowen Calderan leads the Royal Court movement; Maerelle leads the Royal Expansionists.

**Established strategic agenda:** Gradually expand royal authority through appointments, administration, taxation, and strategic alliances without provoking open confrontation or a united Great House opposition.

**Internal tensions:** Aldric wants to strengthen the Crown; Edric prioritizes preserving the post-Rebellion settlement; Maerelle favors a more assertive royal strategy. The Queen and Rusk emphasize stability and institutional legitimacy.

**Leverage and constraints:** Formal royal authority, Crown administration, royal household guard and distinct royal military force, legitimacy of the Imperial Seat, and access to central institutions. The Crown's forces are sufficient for Valedorn and ordinary royal authority but are not assumed capable of defeating all six Great Houses together. The king's practical freedom is constrained by Great House interests and court factions.

**Known relationships:** Needs Vaelorian financial support; maintains an uneasy but formal relationship with Kharadorn; faces Edarth suspicion of centralization; Veyr seeks northern resources; Merrowen cooperates when maritime commerce benefits.

**GM portrayal boundary:** No assassination plot, succession crisis, or hidden controller is established. The king is neither guaranteed safety nor destined death.

## House Vaelorian — Western Highlands

**Ruler:** Lord Theren Vaelorian  
**Designated heir:** Lady Seralyth Vaelorian  
**Council representative:** Lady Ilyra Vaelorian  
**Seat:** Aurelith

**Established strategic agenda:** Maintain financial dominance, preserve trade access, and prevent any rival power from threatening Vaelorian interests.

**Internal tensions:** Seralyth is more openly ambitious and adaptive than Theren. Older traditionalist families distrust her commercial approach; Lady Virella opposes increasing integration with human commercial society. No formal rival heir is established.

**Leverage and constraints:** Financial and diplomatic leverage, commercial relationships, trade agreements, noble debts, and expertise. Exact wealth, lending exposure, troop strength, and dependency figures are not established.

**Known relationships:** The Crown needs financial support and Theren expects influence in return. Vaelorian and Merrowen cooperate commercially while competing for trade influence. Theren regards Garrick Edarth as predictable; Garrick regards Theren as dangerous.

**GM portrayal boundary:** Financial influence is substantial but does not mean Vaelorian controls the Crown, every lender, or all continental commerce.

## House Kharadorn — Dwarven Empire / Dragonstone and Inland Sea

**Ruler:** High King Durnak Kharadorn  
**Designated successor:** Prince Vorgun Kharadorn  
**Other immediate political figures:** Princess Brynja Kharadorn; Lord Kazrik Kharadorn  
**Council representative:** Thane Brokk Ironvein  
**Seats and centers:** Dragonstone and the Inland Sea dominion; separate ancestral homeland centered on Khar-Dum in the northeastern mountains.

**Established strategic agenda:** Preserve dwarven independence, control of Dragonstone and the inland sea, and freedom from surface interference in clan affairs.

**Internal tensions:** Vorgun favors stronger military and strategic leverage. Brynja favors preserving ancient agreements and clan privileges. Kazrik believes the main branch has become too accommodating. Durnak prioritizes clan unity and imperial survival.

**Leverage and constraints:** Dwarven imperial institutions, Dragonstone, control of important inland-sea interests, specialized military tradition, and strategic charter rights. Exact force size, fleet strength, treasury, and readiness are not quantified. The empire is not a collection of collapsed successor states.

**Known relationships:** Durnak respects the post-Rebellion settlement but does not consider himself subject to the Crown. Kharadorn's charter rights in Tallowmere are strategic, not ordinary city government. Maris Merrowen seeks freer navigation; Durnak preserves Kharadorn's dominant strategic position over key inland-sea routes and naval approaches.

**GM portrayal boundary:** Do not turn every local Kharadorn inspection into occupation. The secret underground route to Dragonstone remains GM-only and is not known to ordinary actors.

## House Edarth — Western Heartlands

**Ruler:** Lord Garrick Edarth  
**Recognized heir:** Ser Rowan Edarth  
**Other immediate political figures:** Lady Elayne Edarth; Ser Tomas Edarth  
**Council representative:** Lady Ysabet Edarth  
**Seat:** Stoneguard

**Established strategic agenda:** Preserve traditional noble rights, resist royal centralization, and maintain Edarth's military and agricultural strength.

**Internal tensions:** Rowan wants a more modern political strategy and careful management of lesser families. Elayne is politically capable and connected to lesser nobles. Tomas's popularity among household soldiers concerns Rowan. Edarth's decentralized structure may complicate succession.

**Leverage and constraints:** Landed and agricultural strength, martial tradition, sworn families, and influence among lesser landed houses. Exact military strength, harvest surplus, and number of sworn houses are unknown.

**Known relationships:** Garrick suspects the king is weakening Great House authority. Edarth shares a natural military understanding with Veyr and mutual respect with Kharadorn, while Garrick regards Theren Vaelorian warily.

**GM portrayal boundary:** Traditionalist does not mean automatic rebellion. Edarth may negotiate or cooperate where its rights and interests are protected.

## House Veyr — Northern Marches

**Ruler:** Lord Edran Veyr  
**Designated heir:** Lady Anika Veyr  
**Other immediate political figures:** Ser Halvar Veyr; Lord Stellan Veyr  
**Council representative:** Ser Merek Veyr  
**Seat:** Frosthold

**Established strategic agenda:** Secure resources for the Northern Watch, preserve frontier control, and prevent the south from neglecting the Ancient Barrier.

**Internal tensions:** Anika favors stronger political relationships to secure support. Halvar is popular among frontier soldiers and less interested in compromise. Stellan believes Veyr has become too dependent on southern supplies and promises.

**Leverage and constraints:** Control of most northern frontier territories, frontier experience, Northern Watch, and responsibility for surviving portions of the Ancient Barrier. Sparse settlement and reliance on southern supplies constrain the house. Exact force strength and supply reserves are unknown.

**Known relationships:** Edran regards southern court politics as a distraction. He wants practical support from the Crown. Veyr shares military understanding with Edarth and mutual respect with Kharadorn.

**GM portrayal boundary:** The northern threat and the Ancient Barrier's purpose remain unresolved. Do not invent a new invasion or a collapse of the Barrier without a supported event.

## House Merrowen — Eastern Maritime Territory

**Ruler:** Lady Maris Merrowen  
**Designated heir:** Lord Corvin Merrowen  
**Other immediate political figures:** Lady Selene Merrowen; Lord Aeric Merrowen  
**Council representative:** Lady Cassia Merrowen  
**Seat:** Tidemark

**Established strategic agenda:** Protect eastern ports and shipping, preserve commercial independence, and prevent Kharadorn or the Crown from dominating maritime trade.

**Internal tensions:** Corvin favors a powerful fleet and secure routes. Selene emphasizes commercial influence. Aeric believes merchant families have gained too much influence. Merchants can constrain policy but do not presently threaten to replace the ruling dynasty.

**Leverage and constraints:** Maritime territory, ports, naval tradition, commercial relationships, and access to eastern coastal routes. Exact fleet strength, port capacity, and treasury are unknown.

**Known relationships:** Maris supports Crown policy when it benefits maritime commerce. Merrowen and Vaelorian cooperate and compete in trade. Maris seeks freer navigation than Kharadorn is inclined to allow.

**GM portrayal boundary:** Merrowen does not control every eastern port. Commercial interests are influential but are not a single unified faction.

## Cross-house strategic tensions

These are established interests and relationships, not active wars:
- Crown centralization versus Great House autonomy.
- Crown dependence on Vaelorian finance and the political price of credit.
- Kharadorn's inland-sea control versus Merrowen's desire for freer navigation.
- Edarth's defense of traditional noble rights.
- Veyr's demand for reliable northern resources and attention.
- Vaelorian and Merrowen commercial cooperation and competition.
- Kharadorn's independent status and uneasy peace with the human monarchy.

## GM portrayal and adjudication guidance

- A strategic agenda is not automatically an active project.
- A ruler's public position, private belief, and actual decision may differ.
- Council representatives are not necessarily the house ruler's mouthpiece.
- Court faction alignment is issue-specific and does not determine house loyalty.
- Great Houses have substantial power, but no unsupported numeric army, wealth, or influence score should be assigned.
- A Great House reacts to local events only when it has a credible interest, information, and a practical means to respond.
- Do not generate a Great House war merely to create activity. Diplomatic pressure, trade leverage, legal claims, appointments, and delayed support are often more plausible first moves.
