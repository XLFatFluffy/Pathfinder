# Living World — Initial Issue Register

**Snapshot date:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC  
**Status:** Initial GM baseline; details intentionally incomplete  
**Authority:** Session One GM Readiness Packet, section 8

These issues are established as current pressures. This register does not invent causes, responsible individuals, secret connections, or predetermined outcomes. Each needs a fuller actor-and-evidence record before the first simulated world turn.

## Operating rules

- Each issue is independent until evidence links it to another.
- ACTIVE means the pressure exists and may prompt action; it does not mean imminent disaster.
- WATCH means monitor for new evidence or seasonal change.
- No issue advances merely because a session ends. Advance it only when in-world time passes and its causes support movement.
- Record actor positions separately. Two parties may describe the same dispute differently.
- Record rumors as rumors and official claims as claims.
- The PCs may never encounter some of these issues. They still develop only if the relevant actors have motive, means, opportunity, and information.
- If PCs intervene, record their actual effect and allow other actors to respond plausibly.
- Do not turn these seven pressures into one conspiracy without evidence.

## Initial world-turn gate

Before advancing the first world turn beyond 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC, establish:
1. Which issues have actors already taking action.
2. What actions are actually underway and when they began.
3. What information each actor possesses.
4. Which issues are waiting on a decision, resource, or external event.
5. Which issues can plausibly change during the elapsed time.

The GM may run a short opening snapshot without fully detailing every actor. Missing information remains UNKNOWN.
