## Detailed issue records

### TLM-ISS-001 — Harbor berth allocation
**Pressures:** Capacity, predictability, established shipowner privileges, smaller-trader access, and the needs of river traffic.  
**Established positions:** Harbor Fellowship wants more predictable access to newer quays and argues established shipowners should receive priority in crowded seasons. Smaller traders and river interests fear that temporary priority becomes permanent privilege. Ysella Vane must apply harbor rules while keeping traffic moving. Tavin Rusk favors predictable allocation and resists special treatment for any single fleet.  
**Unknown:** Actual berth capacity, formal priority rules, current petitions, specific vessels/owners, any formal vote, Kharadorn's position beyond strategic oversight.  
**Possible paths:** negotiated schedule, revised rules, temporary allocation, delayed cargo, Council recommendation, or unresolved seasonal friction. No outcome is predetermined.

### TLM-ISS-002 — Warehouse fire precautions
**Pressures:** Fire safety, insurance/compensation, cost allocation, disruption, and competition between established and smaller warehouse operators.  
**Established positions:** Warehouse Guild argues improvements are overdue. Some owners suspect the Guild is using safety concerns to force smaller competitors out. Meret Sorn favors investment but wants costs shared according to responsibility.  
**Unknown:** Exact fire dates/sites, causes, damage, responsible parties, investigation, individual owners, and whether incidents are connected.  
**Possible paths:** inspection, voluntary repairs, cost-sharing dispute, ordinance proposal, delayed work, or further incidents if an actual cause remains unaddressed. Do not decide arson or conspiracy without evidence.

### TLM-ISS-003 — Northfield harvest
**Pressures:** Uneven yields, farm damage, labor shortages, storage, prices, and transport.  
**Established positions:** Harvest is expected to be adequate but uneven; several farms report damage and labor shortages. Grain merchants are watching closely.  
**Unknown:** Actual yields, affected farms, causes, prices, reserves, contracts, and whether any local household faces hardship.  
**Possible paths:** ordinary adequate harvest with local winners/losers, price movement, delayed deliveries, labor hiring, or localized hardship. No famine is established.

### TLM-ISS-004 — Ratfolk market access
**Pressures:** Formal access to stalls and leases, existing market privileges, safety from retaliation, and political representation.  
**Established positions:** Tikka Vell wants ratfolk workshops to sell openly rather than through intermediaries. Nix-of-the-Cistern worries that greater visibility may expose vulnerable households to retaliation. Miraq Ashwhisk favors incremental leases and dependable customers. Vessa Reedtail insists that household safety and consent remain central. Some merchants support regular access; others resist changing practice. Ratfolk may petition through the Reeve or seek sponsorship; there is no guaranteed Council seat.  
**Unknown:** Named applicants beyond Tikka, exact proposed stalls/leases, individual landlord positions, legal basis for any refusal, current petition text and status, public support.  
**Possible paths:** trial stalls, sponsored leases, guild negotiation, delayed petition, divided community response, or a civic ruling. Ratfolk communities are not a unified bloc.

### TLM-ISS-005 — Denvor household priorities
**Pressures:** Cost of defenses and infrastructure, civic cooperation, commercial relationships, family continuity, and local authority under the Kharadorn charter.  
**Established positions:** Oswin is cautious and values negotiated solutions; Edwyn favors greater defensive and infrastructure investment; Catrin maintains civic and noble relationships; Anwen builds commercial and civic ties.  
**Unknown:** Specific private proposals, budget, timing, and which disagreements will reach formal decision.  
**Boundary:** This is not an open succession crisis. Edwyn is the recognized heir. Do not invent a rival claimant or imminent death.

### TLM-ISS-006 — Kharadorn harbor-defense review
**Pressures:** Denvor autonomy, harbor readiness, cost of repairs, trade growth, and charter compliance.  
**Established positions:** Vardek Flintmark reviews tribute accounts, harbor-defense stores and works, and availability of ships/supplies/transport promised under the charter. He respects Denvor's ordinary authority but will report genuine failures.  
**Unknown:** Inspection date, findings, deficiencies, repair costs, deadlines, and any formal recommendation from Kharadorn.  
**Boundary:** The Charter House is not an occupation force. Vardek cannot command the Watch or rewrite the charter.

### TLM-ISS-007 — Skilled labor, credit, and supply
**Pressures:** Competing repair and construction demands, specialist labor, debt, late deliveries, quality disputes, and commercial risk.  
**Established positions:** Shipwrights and artificers protect standards and skilled labor. Dagna Coppervein resists unrealistic deadlines. Ilyne Marris brokers independent commercial relationships. Existing debts and contracts create fragmented pressure.  
**Unknown:** Named debtors/creditors beyond established contacts, exact sums, wage rates, material shortages, late contracts, and which disputes are current versus general conditions.  
**Boundary:** Do not assign invented debts or shortages to named NPCs. Do not make separate disputes one conspiracy without evidence.

### EDA-ISS-001 — Longmead boundary dispute

**Status:** ACTIVE / LOW-INTENSITY  
**Location:** Unmapped pasture-and-woodland boundary between House Harrowden and House Wrenford in the Western Heartlands.  
**Parties:** Lord Berold Harrowden and Lady Cerys Wrenford.  
**Liege authority:** Lord Garrick Edarth.

A stream and old boundary markers define part of the customary division between the two holdings. Seasonal flooding and inconsistent maintenance have made the markers difficult to interpret. Both houses claim customary rights to a narrow strip of summer pasture and adjoining managed woodland. Tenants on both sides have used the area under different interpretations.

No armed clash, killing, raid, eviction, or formal seizure has occurred. Neither side has submitted a complete formal claim with mutually accepted evidence. Both prefer a favorable ruling but have reasons to avoid disrupting harvest labor or losing Edarth's favor.

**Unknown:** Original charter wording; age and provenance of the markers; exact extent of the disputed strip; whether any tenant deliberately moved a marker; and whether Edarth will hear the case directly or appoint an intermediary.

**Possible paths:** Joint survey and restored markers; temporary shared-use agreement; Edarth arbitration; negotiated exchange of grazing or woodland rights; or continued low-level friction.

**Escalation threshold:** Deliberate marker destruction, eviction, livestock seizure, or violence by retainers could elevate the matter. None has happened at campaign start.

**Map safeguard:** This is a customary boundary dispute, not permission to draw overlapping territory polygons. Keep the exact area uncalibrated pending authoritative artwork and GM approval.

## Operating rules

- Each issue is independent until evidence links it to another.
- ACTIVE means pressure exists and actors may act; it does not mean imminent disaster.
- WATCH means monitor for evidence or seasonal change.
- No issue advances merely because a session ends. Time, cause, opportunity, and actor capability must justify movement.
- Record actor positions separately. Conflicting accounts remain distinct.
- Rumors are not objective facts.
- The PCs may never encounter some issues. They still develop only when actors have motive, means, opportunity, and information.
- Ignoring an issue does not automatically produce its worst possible outcome.
- Player intervention can resolve, worsen, redirect, or transform an issue.
