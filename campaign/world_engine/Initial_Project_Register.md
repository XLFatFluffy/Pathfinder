## Project handling rules

- UNDERWAY means an action is established as in progress.
- PROPOSED means an idea or demand exists; approval and funding are not assumed.
- PETITION / advocacy means someone is actively seeking a decision, not that the institution has accepted it.
- DISPUTED means parties disagree; it does not imply violence.
- MONITORING means observing conditions, not controlling them.
- Do not assign deadlines, progress percentages, budgets, or success probabilities without supporting information.
- A project may stall, change sponsor, lose support, be superseded, or succeed partially.
- A completed project must record its actual result and any new obligations or consequences.
