# Military Model

**Status:** Phase 2 World Engine model established
**Baseline:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC
**Execution:** GM-reviewed

## Purpose
Represent military capacity, readiness, logistics, and political constraints without inventing troop counts or automatically escalating disputes into war.

## Force categories
- household guard
- professional force
- garrison
- vassal contingent
- local levy
- watch or town guard
- clan force
- naval force
- auxiliary support

These categories must remain distinct.

## Military state
Each military record should track, where established:
- actor;
- force category;
- role;
- location or strategic area;
- readiness condition;
- supply condition;
- command authority;
- mobilization status;
- dependencies;
- political constraints;
- known objectives;
- source records.

Readiness and mobilization are separate fields.

## Core rules
1. Do not invent troop totals, fleet sizes, casualty figures, readiness percentages, or campaign budgets.
2. A garrison is not automatically deployable.
3. A vassal obligation is not a universal service quota.
4. Commercial ships are not automatically a war fleet.
5. Logistics constrain operations.
6. Political authorization matters.
7. A dispute, petition, inspection, or insult does not automatically create armed conflict.

## Established strategic profiles
- The Crown maintains a permanent household guard and distinct royal military force.
- Veyr maintains the Northern Watch, Mobile Marcher Companies, and a Household Guard.
- Vaelorian maintains a comparatively compact professional force with infantry, archers, household troops, and mountain garrisons.
- Edarth relies on ruling-house forces, landed nobility, lesser-house relationships, roads, and fortifications.
- Kharadorn maintains specialized heavy infantry, armored troops, engineers, siege capability, clan contingents, underground fortifications, and naval forces associated with the Central Inland Sea.
- Merrowen's military character includes maritime power, ports, naval tradition, and coastal defense.
- Exact force totals and detailed readiness remain unresolved.

## Mobilization
Use qualitative states unless canon establishes more:
- **STANDING**
- **READY**
- **LIMITED MOBILIZATION**
- **MOBILIZED**
- **DEMOBILIZING**
- **UNKNOWN**

A mobilization change requires a cause and should identify logistical and political consequences.

## Military evaluation
**objective → available forces → readiness → logistics → political authorization → action → consequence**

The engine should prefer non-war outcomes when established circumstances do not support escalation.
