## Operating standards

- Unknown remains unknown. Do not invent exact borders, troop strengths, treasury figures, debts, or private plans.
- An established strategic agenda is not automatically an active project.
- A recurring complaint is not automatically an active dispute.
- A rumor is not an objective fact.
- Proposed events remain proposals until the GM approves them.
- Record what each actor knows and believes separately from objective GM truth.
- Do not advance time merely because a real-world conversation or session ends. Use the campaign calendar.
- After each approved world turn, update affected actor, issue, project, event, timeline, and current-world-state records.
