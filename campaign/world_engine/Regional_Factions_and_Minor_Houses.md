# Regional Factions and Minor Houses Register

**Snapshot:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC  
**Status:** GM canon created to support the Living World Engine.  
**Scope:** New actors introduced to give existing locations believable institutional and political depth. No new territorial borders are drawn.

These actors are now part of the campaign's working canon. Their existence, leaders, broad interests, and relationships are established below. Exact wealth, troop strength, boundaries, secret plans, and future actions remain unknown unless explicitly stated.

## Design rule

A new actor exists because an existing location or pressure needs someone capable of making decisions. These actors are not pre-written villains or quest dispensers. They can cooperate, compromise, fail, change leadership, or become irrelevant depending on play.

# I. Tallowmere and Hinterland Factions

## TLM-FAC-001 — Millers' Guild of the River Vale

**Type:** Local economic guild  
**Base:** Millhaven and river-mill communities  
**Public purpose:** Protect mill rights, water access, grain processing, and reliable river maintenance.

**Principal NPC:** Harlan Tebb, senior reeve and respected mediator.

**Interests**
- Reliable water flow and mill maintenance.
- Predictable grain movement toward Tallowmere.
- Protection of established mill rights.
- Avoiding unnecessary interference by competing landholders.

**Internal division**
Older mill families favor customary arrangements. Younger operators want investment in improved equipment and clearer maintenance obligations.

**Political leverage**
Grain processing, local knowledge, and relationships with farmers and river carriers.

**Constraints**
They are economically important but do not control the River Vale or Denvor policy.

**Engine hooks**
Flooding, drought, upstream maintenance, road disruption, grain shortages, or a dispute over water access can activate this faction.

---

## TLM-FAC-002 — Brackenwood Charcoal Fellowship

**Type:** Woodland labor and charcoal producers' association  
**Base:** Brackenholt and surrounding managed woodland.

**Principal NPC:** Mara Bellwood, senior charcoal burner and elected spokesman for several working families.

**Interests**
- Preserve customary access to woodland resources.
- Maintain predictable cutting limits.
- Secure fair charcoal prices.
- Prevent outside buyers from monopolizing local production.

**Internal division**
Established households favor customary rights. Some younger burners favor formal contracts with Tallowmere merchants.

**Political leverage**
Fuel supply for smiths, kilns, kitchens, and workshops.

**Constraints**
The Fellowship does not own the woodland as a unified body and remains subject to Denvor authority and individual customary rights.

**Engine hooks**
A timber shortage, charcoal price spike, woodland boundary dispute, merchant contract, or major construction project could change its position.

---

## TLM-FAC-003 — Stonehollow Quarry Compact

**Type:** Coalition of quarrying families  
**Base:** Stonehollow.

**Principal NPC:** Dagna Vale, senior quarry contractor and principal negotiator among the largest family partnerships.

**Interests**
- Preserve quarry rights and family shares.
- Keep Quarry Road usable.
- Obtain predictable contracts for building stone.
- Prevent outside investors from displacing local families.

**Internal division**
Larger partnerships favor long contracts with Tallowmere. Smaller families fear losing independence.

**Political leverage**
Stone, masons, quarry labor, and knowledge of the Greyback Ridges.

**Constraints**
The Compact has no unified territorial sovereignty and remains subject to the Denvor charter and local law.

**Engine hooks**
Tallowmere construction, road repairs, quarry accidents, competing claims, or a large external buyer could produce political pressure.

---

## TLM-FAC-004 — Saltwick Shore Compact

**Type:** Association of fishing and net-making households  
**Base:** Saltwick and nearby shore camps.

**Principal NPC:** Edda Rusk, senior freeholder and frequent representative in shore-charter negotiations.

**Interests**
- Protect traditional fishing grounds.
- Preserve landing and drying rights.
- Keep net-making profitable.
- Resist arbitrary control by outside officials.
- Maintain enough fish supply for local households and trade.

**Internal division**
Fishing families prioritize access. Net-makers and merchants favor stable contracts and wider trade.

**Political leverage**
Food supply, coastal knowledge, fishing labor, and local boats.

**Constraints**
The Compact lacks a single superior political authority of its own, and its precise charter relationship remains unresolved.

**Engine hooks**
A fishing-ground dispute, storm damage, new harbor regulation, outside crews, or Tallowmere demand for fish can activate competing interests.

---

## TLM-FAC-005 — Tallowmere Warehouse Guild

**Type:** Established commercial guild  
**Base:** New Quays / Warehouse Ward.

**Principal NPC:** Meret Sorn, guild leader.

**Interests**
- Protect stored cargo and warehouse property.
- Reduce fire risk.
- Secure labor and credit.
- Maintain favorable harbor access.
- Prevent arbitrary inspection or seizure.

**Political leverage**
Warehousing, merchant credit networks, cargo information, and cooperation with the harbor.

**Constraints**
The Guild depends on the city's roads, harbor, labor supply, and political stability.

**Engine hooks**
The existing warehouse-fire issue, insurance/credit disputes, labor shortages, or harbor allocation can move the Guild into active negotiation.

# II. Newly Established Minor Houses

These houses are intentionally kept outside exact map geometry until the continental and regional territorial layers are calibrated.

## MIN-HSE-001 — House Harrowen

**Type:** Minor human landed house  
**Seat:** Harrowfield Manor, location within the broader southern political sphere not yet surveyed.  
**Current head:** Lady Elira Harrowen.  
**Heir:** Ser Tomas Harrowen.

**Established interests**
- Preserve hereditary land rights.
- Maintain profitable grain and horse breeding.
- Avoid becoming dependent on any single Great House.
- Seek marriages that improve regional security.

**Political character**
Practical and conservative. Elira prefers treaties and marriages to military confrontation.

**Leverage**
Agricultural production and established marriage connections.

**Unknown**
Exact liege, territory, wealth, military strength, and neighboring houses.

**Engine role**
A useful neutral minor house for future southern diplomacy once its location and superior relationship are established.

---

## MIN-HSE-002 — House Corven

**Type:** Minor human coastal house  
**Seat:** Greywater Hall, on a coastal territory outside the currently surveyed Tallowmere region.  
**Current head:** Lord Rennic Corven.  
**Heir:** Lady Mira Corven.

**Established interests**
- Protect coastal fishing and small-craft trade.
- Preserve local charter privileges.
- Avoid domination by larger maritime powers.
- Increase the value of the family's harbor.

**Political character**
Suspicious of outside interference but willing to trade access for protection.

**Leverage**
A modest harbor, fishing fleet, and coastal contacts.

**Unknown**
Exact position, liege, harbor size, fleet strength, and relationship with House Merrowen.

**Engine role**
Potential future maritime actor. No current conflict with Merrowen is established.

---

## MIN-HSE-003 — House Wrenford

**Type:** Minor human landed house  
**Seat:** Wrenford Keep, near a major southern road outside the Tallowmere survey.  
**Current head:** Lord Alester Wrenford.  
**Heir:** Ser Rowan Wrenford.

**Established interests**
- Maintain road security and toll income.
- Protect local market rights.
- Preserve the family's independence.
- Avoid costly military commitments.

**Political character**
Transactional and locally focused.

**Leverage**
Road access, market privileges, and local retainers.

**Unknown**
Exact superior authority, territory, toll arrangements, and neighboring claims.

**Engine role**
Potential intermediary between southern powers and the larger political system.

---

## MIN-HSE-004 — House Calderick

**Type:** Minor human noble house  
**Seat:** Calderick Hall, within a fragmented southern political region.  
**Current head:** Ser Oren Calderick, acting lord after the death of his elder brother.  
**Heir:** Not yet confirmed.

**Established condition**
The house is experiencing a succession uncertainty because the previous lord's heir has not yet been publicly confirmed.

**Interests**
- Preserve the holding.
- Establish lawful succession.
- Avoid external intervention.

**Political character**
Internal divisions are more important than external ambition.

**Leverage**
Local land, household retainers, and a recognized noble name.

**Unknown**
The identity and legitimacy of possible heirs, the cause and date of the former lord's death, exact liege, territory, and any competing claim.

**Engine role**
This is a deliberately unresolved succession pressure. It is not currently a crisis and has no established connection to the Crown, Great Houses, or any PC.

# III. New Individuals Who Make the Existing World Functional

## NPC-001 — Harlan Tebb

**Role:** Reeve of Millhaven  
**Affiliation:** House Denvor / River Vale communities  
**Character:** Patient, practical, protective of local customs.  
**Goal:** Keep grain production and river commerce stable.  
**Conflict tendency:** Prefers mediation; becomes firm when outside authorities threaten Millhaven's customary rights.

## NPC-002 — Mara Bellwood

**Role:** Charcoal-burner representative at Brackenholt  
**Affiliation:** Brackenwood Charcoal Fellowship  
**Character:** Blunt, observant, politically inexperienced but locally influential.  
**Goal:** Preserve woodland access and fair prices.  
**Conflict tendency:** Will organize workers rather than immediately appeal to nobles.

## NPC-003 — Dagna Vale

**Role:** Quarry contractor and negotiator  
**Affiliation:** Stonehollow Quarry Compact  
**Character:** Hard-working, suspicious of outsiders, highly knowledgeable about stone and quarry operations.  
**Goal:** Protect family quarry shares and secure profitable contracts.  
**Conflict tendency:** Prefers collective bargaining and legal petitions.

## NPC-004 — Edda Rusk

**Role:** Saltwick freeholder and shore-charter representative  
**Affiliation:** Saltwick Shore Compact  
**Character:** Calm, stubborn, experienced in negotiating with merchants.  
**Goal:** Preserve traditional fishing and landing rights.  
**Conflict tendency:** Will resist unilateral outside control but accepts negotiated arrangements.

## NPC-005 — Elira Harrowen

**Role:** Lady of House Harrowen  
**Character:** Measured, politically cautious, highly attentive to family reputation.  
**Goal:** Keep her house independent and solvent.  
**Current action:** Maintaining existing relationships rather than pursuing a new political project.

## NPC-006 — Rennic Corven

**Role:** Lord of House Corven  
**Character:** Suspicious, commercially minded, proud of local autonomy.  
**Goal:** Increase the value of his harbor without surrendering control.  
**Current action:** No active expansion project established.

## NPC-007 — Alester Wrenford

**Role:** Lord of House Wrenford  
**Character:** Practical and transactional.  
**Goal:** Keep roads safe and retain toll income without provoking stronger neighbors.  
**Current action:** Maintaining local arrangements.

## NPC-008 — Ser Oren Calderick

**Role:** Acting lord of House Calderick  
**Character:** Defensive, politically cautious, increasingly concerned about legitimacy.  
**Goal:** Establish a lawful succession before outsiders exploit uncertainty.  
**Current action:** Seeking confirmation of the family's succession position through lawful channels.

# IV. Engine Safeguards for These New Actors

1. These actors do not automatically know about one another.
2. Their existence does not establish communication, alliances, rivalries, or intelligence networks.
3. Their resources remain qualitative unless later established.
4. House Calderick's succession uncertainty is local and does not imply a Crown succession issue.
5. House Corven's coastal position does not create a dispute with Merrowen.
6. House Harrowen's grain production does not establish a trade agreement with Tallowmere.
7. The three woodland/quarry/shore organizations do not constitute independent governments.
8. No new border polygons should be drawn from this register.
9. The Living World Engine may use these actors when a plausible event, request, opportunity, or piece of information connects them to the active world state.
10. Player characters can create entirely new factions or destroy existing ones through play.

# V. Questions Deliberately Deferred

- Exact lieges of Houses Harrowen, Corven, Wrenford, and Calderick.
- Exact locations and borders of their holdings.
- Their Great House relationships.
- Their military and financial capabilities.
- Private family scandals, secrets, and hidden alliances.
- The identity of House Calderick's possible heir.
- Whether any of these houses will matter to the PCs.

These questions should be answered only when the campaign reaches the relevant region or when continental political mapping requires them.
