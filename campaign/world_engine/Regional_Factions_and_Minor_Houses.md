# Regional Factions and Supplemental Minor Actors Register

**Snapshot:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC  
**Status:** GM canon created under explicit delegation to develop NPCs, minor factions, and supporting actors.  
**Scope:** Tallowmere hinterland factions plus a small number of supplemental minor actors not already covered by the continental minor-house register.

The existing continental network remains authoritative in [Continental Minor Houses and Regional Powers](Continental_Minor_Houses_and_Regional_Powers.md). This document does **not** duplicate those houses.

## Design rule

These actors exist because established locations and world pressures need people capable of making decisions. They are not pre-written villains or quest dispensers. They can cooperate, compromise, fail, change leadership, or become irrelevant depending on play.

# I. Tallowmere and Hinterland Factions

## TLM-FAC-001 — Millers' Guild of the River Vale

**Type:** Local economic guild  
**Base:** Millhaven and river-mill communities  
**Public purpose:** Protect mill rights, water access, grain processing, and reliable river maintenance.

**Principal NPC:** Harlan Tebb, senior reeve and respected mediator.

**Interests**
- Reliable water flow and mill maintenance.
- Predictable grain movement toward Tallowmere.
- Protection of established mill rights.
- Avoiding unnecessary interference by competing landholders.

**Internal division:** Older mill families favor customary arrangements. Younger operators want investment in improved equipment and clearer maintenance obligations.

**Political leverage:** Grain processing, local knowledge, and relationships with farmers and river carriers.

**Constraints:** Economically important but does not control the River Vale or Denvor policy.

**Potential relevance in play:** Flooding, drought, upstream maintenance, road disruption, grain shortages, or a dispute over water access.

---

## TLM-FAC-002 — Brackenwood Charcoal Fellowship

**Type:** Woodland labor and charcoal producers' association  
**Base:** Brackenholt and surrounding managed woodland.

**Principal NPC:** Mara Bellwood, senior charcoal burner and elected spokesman for several working families.

**Interests**
- Preserve customary access to woodland resources.
- Maintain predictable cutting limits.
- Secure fair charcoal prices.
- Prevent outside buyers from monopolizing local production.

**Internal division:** Established households favor customary rights. Some younger burners favor formal contracts with Tallowmere merchants.

**Political leverage:** Fuel supply for smiths, kilns, kitchens, and workshops.

**Constraints:** Does not own the woodland as a unified body and remains subject to Denvor authority and individual customary rights.

**Potential relevance in play:** Timber shortage, charcoal price spike, woodland boundary dispute, merchant contract, or major construction project.

---

## TLM-FAC-003 — Stonehollow Quarry Compact

**Type:** Coalition of quarrying families  
**Base:** Stonehollow.

**Principal NPC:** Dagna Vale, senior quarry contractor and principal negotiator among the largest family partnerships.

**Interests**
- Preserve quarry rights and family shares.
- Keep Quarry Road usable.
- Obtain predictable contracts for building stone.
- Prevent outside investors from displacing local families.

**Internal division:** Larger partnerships favor long contracts with Tallowmere. Smaller families fear losing independence.

**Political leverage:** Stone, masons, quarry labor, and knowledge of the Greyback Ridges.

**Constraints:** No unified territorial sovereignty. Subject to the Denvor charter and local law.

**Potential relevance in play:** Tallowmere construction, road repairs, quarry accidents, competing claims, or a large external buyer.

---

## TLM-FAC-004 — Saltwick Shore Compact

**Type:** Association of fishing and net-making households  
**Base:** Saltwick and nearby shore camps.

**Principal NPC:** Edda Rusk, senior freeholder and frequent representative in shore-charter negotiations.

**Interests**
- Protect traditional fishing grounds.
- Preserve landing and drying rights.
- Keep net-making profitable.
- Resist arbitrary control by outside officials.
- Maintain enough fish supply for local households and trade.

**Internal division:** Fishing families prioritize access. Net-makers and merchants favor stable contracts and wider trade.

**Political leverage:** Food supply, coastal knowledge, fishing labor, and local boats.

**Constraints:** The Compact lacks a single superior political authority of its own, and its precise charter relationship remains unresolved.

**Potential relevance in play:** Fishing-ground dispute, storm damage, new harbor regulation, outside crews, or Tallowmere demand for fish.

---

## TLM-FAC-005 — Tallowmere Warehouse Guild

**Type:** Established commercial guild  
**Base:** New Quays / Warehouse Ward.

**Principal NPC:** Meret Sorn, guild leader.

**Interests**
- Protect stored cargo and warehouse property.
- Reduce fire risk.
- Secure labor and credit.
- Maintain favorable harbor access.
- Prevent arbitrary inspection or seizure.

**Political leverage:** Warehousing, merchant credit networks, cargo information, and cooperation with the harbor.

**Constraints:** Depends on the city's roads, harbor, labor supply, and political stability.

**Potential relevance in play:** Existing warehouse-fire issue, insurance/credit disputes, labor shortages, or harbor allocation.

# II. Supplemental Minor Houses

These houses are outside the currently calibrated Tallowmere map. Their existence does not establish exact borders or Great House relationships unless stated.

## MIN-HSE-001 — House Corven

**Type:** Minor human coastal house  
**Seat:** Greywater Hall, on a coastal territory outside the currently surveyed Tallowmere region.  
**Current head:** Lord Rennic Corven.  
**Heir:** Lady Mira Corven.

**Established interests**
- Protect coastal fishing and small-craft trade.
- Preserve local charter privileges.
- Avoid domination by larger maritime powers.
- Increase the value of the family's harbor.

**Political character:** Suspicious of outside interference but willing to trade access for protection.

**Leverage:** Modest harbor, fishing fleet, and coastal contacts.

**Unknown:** Exact position, liege, harbor size, fleet strength, and relationship with House Merrowen.

**Engine role:** Potential future maritime actor. No current conflict with Merrowen is established.

---

## MIN-HSE-002 — House Calderick

**Type:** Minor human noble house  
**Seat:** Calderick Hall, within a fragmented southern political region.  
**Current head:** Ser Oren Calderick, acting lord after the death of his elder brother.  
**Heir:** Not yet confirmed.

**Established condition:** The house is experiencing a succession uncertainty because the previous lord's heir has not been publicly confirmed.

**Interests**
- Preserve the holding.
- Establish lawful succession.
- Avoid external intervention.

**Political character:** Internal divisions matter more than external ambition.

**Leverage:** Local land, household retainers, and a recognized noble name.

**Unknown:** Identity and legitimacy of possible heirs, cause and date of the former lord's death, exact liege, territory, and any competing claim.

**Engine role:** A deliberately unresolved succession pressure. It is not currently a crisis and has no established connection to the Crown, Great Houses, or any PC.

# III. Supporting NPCs

## NPC-001 — Harlan Tebb

**Role:** Reeve of Millhaven  
**Affiliation:** House Denvor / River Vale communities  
**Character:** Patient, practical, protective of local customs.  
**Goal:** Keep grain production and river commerce stable.  
**Conflict tendency:** Prefers mediation; becomes firm when outside authorities threaten Millhaven's customary rights.

## NPC-002 — Mara Bellwood

**Role:** Charcoal-burner representative at Brackenholt  
**Affiliation:** Brackenwood Charcoal Fellowship  
**Character:** Blunt, observant, politically inexperienced but locally influential.  
**Goal:** Preserve woodland access and fair prices.  
**Conflict tendency:** Organizes workers before immediately appealing to nobles.

## NPC-003 — Dagna Vale

**Role:** Quarry contractor and negotiator  
**Affiliation:** Stonehollow Quarry Compact  
**Character:** Hard-working, suspicious of outsiders, highly knowledgeable about stone and quarry operations.  
**Goal:** Protect family quarry shares and secure profitable contracts.  
**Conflict tendency:** Prefers collective bargaining and legal petitions.

## NPC-004 — Edda Rusk

**Role:** Saltwick freeholder and shore-charter representative  
**Affiliation:** Saltwick Shore Compact  
**Character:** Calm, stubborn, experienced in negotiating with merchants.  
**Goal:** Preserve traditional fishing and landing rights.  
**Conflict tendency:** Resists unilateral outside control but accepts negotiated arrangements.

## NPC-005 — Rennic Corven

**Role:** Lord of House Corven  
**Character:** Suspicious, commercially minded, proud of local autonomy.  
**Goal:** Increase the value of his harbor without surrendering control.  
**Current action:** Maintaining existing arrangements; no expansion project established.

## NPC-006 — Mira Corven

**Role:** Heir to House Corven  
**Character:** More commercially curious than Rennic, but loyal to the family's independence.  
**Goal:** Learn how larger maritime powers conduct trade without allowing them to dominate the house.  
**Current action:** No formal project established.

## NPC-007 — Ser Oren Calderick

**Role:** Acting lord of House Calderick  
**Character:** Defensive, politically cautious, increasingly concerned about legitimacy.  
**Goal:** Establish lawful succession before outsiders exploit uncertainty.  
**Current action:** Seeking confirmation of the family's succession position through lawful channels.

# IV. GM Reference Safeguards

1. These actors do not automatically know about one another.
2. Their existence does not establish communication, alliances, rivalries, or intelligence networks.
3. Their resources remain qualitative unless later established.
4. House Calderick's succession uncertainty is local and does not imply a Crown succession issue.
5. House Corven's coastal position does not create a dispute with Merrowen.
6. The Tallowmere factions do not constitute independent governments.
7. No new border polygons should be drawn from this register.
8. The GM may bring these actors into play when a plausible event, request, opportunity, or piece of information connects them to the situation at hand.
9. Player characters can create entirely new factions or destroy existing ones through play.

# V. Deferred Questions

- Exact liege of House Corven.
- Exact location and borders of Houses Corven and Calderick.
- Their military and financial capabilities.
- Private family scandals, secrets, and hidden alliances.
- Identity and legitimacy of House Calderick's possible heir.
- Whether either supplemental house becomes important to the PCs.

These questions should be answered only when the campaign reaches the relevant region or when continental political mapping requires them.
