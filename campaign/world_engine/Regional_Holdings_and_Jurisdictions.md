## 2. Confirmed political relationships

- House Denvor is a minor house with local authority over Tallowmere and a modest surrounding lordship. It owes charter obligations to House Kharadorn but retains ordinary civic administration.
- House Avenholt holds Ashcombe Manor under House Denvor. It owes customary dues and military service, subject to its tenure.
- House Merewick administers Fordmere under a separate hereditary crossing charter. Denvor negotiates access and road coordination but does not own the crossing.
- House Velleran holds Harthbridge and a compact territory. It competes commercially with Tallowmere while cooperating on road security, grain movement, and regional disputes.
- House Relland is subordinate to House Velleran. Its holding lies along the South Road within the broader Velleran-chartered territory.
- Saltwick's shore-charter families exercise local authority. The superior jurisdiction remains unresolved.
- Stonehollow's quarry rights belong to several local families under a Denvor charter, not a newly invented noble house.

## 3. Established obligations and rights

### House Denvor
The hereditary charter requires annual tribute, maintenance of harbor defenses, and provision of ships, supplies, or transport when lawfully called upon. Kharadorn retains strategic rights concerning the harbor and inland-sea defense. Exact tribute amount, charter wording, enforcement process, and historical precedents remain unresolved.

### House Avenholt
Customary dues and military service are owed to House Denvor. Exact quantities, service duration, and the detailed tenure instrument are not established.

### House Merewick
The family administers the Fordmere crossing charter and collects crossing fees. It seeks to preserve the crossing's profitability and opposes a competing bridge nearby. Exact toll schedule and the full extent of its charter are unresolved.

### House Velleran
The house holds Harthbridge's town charter, market and toll privileges, and compact territory. Exact obligations to its superior authority and the charter's full legal text remain unresolved.

### House Relland
The house owes allegiance to House Velleran. Its local income includes rents, grazing rights, grain, and road frontage. Its retainers' past extortion and murder, and the household cover-up, are established in the dedicated GM record.

## 5. Border and map safeguards

- Do not draw exact boundaries from this register.
- Do not assume that a town's charter defines every acre of its surrounding territory.
- Do not convert customary woodland, fishing, grazing, or quarry rights into exclusive territorial sovereignty.
- Do not assume that every jurisdiction is a neat contiguous polygon.
- A future border dispute requires a specific competing claim, evidence or precedent, actors, and an interest worth contesting.
- A disputed toll, access right, or resource use may be a jurisdictional conflict without being a territorial war.
- Map geometry must be calibrated against authoritative artwork before promotion to live atlas synchronization.
