# Seasonal Conditions and Agricultural Cycles — Legacy Source Note

**Status:** Retired operating worksheet; descriptive material migrated  
**Replaced by:** [Agriculture, Food Supply, and Seasonal Work](../world/Agriculture_Food_and_Seasonal_Work.md)  
**Historical baseline:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC

This file previously combined descriptive regional climate and agricultural context with procedures for the former Living World Engine. The automated simulation, world-turn cycle, seasonal tracking fields, and event-propagation procedures have been retired from campaign scope.

The useful descriptive material has been consolidated into the replacement reference:
- Broad regional climate tendencies and geographic sensitivities.
- The general agricultural cycle from land preparation through winter storage.
- Grain, livestock, timber, river, maritime, and arid-region dependencies.
- The distinction between calendar month names and actual seasonal conditions.
- The opening-date safeguard: 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC does not by itself establish a particular temperature, rainfall pattern, storm, frost, or other weather event.
- The Northfield harvest-monitoring note and Northern Watch autumn-provisioning context.

This legacy file is retained to make the repository's history and migration clear. It is not an active worksheet, a required GM procedure, or a source for automatically advancing events. The replacement world reference is the current human-readable guide; the GM determines what weather, agricultural conditions, and consequences actually occur.
