# Seasonal and Environmental Model

**Status:** Phase 2 World Engine model established
**Baseline:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC
**Execution:** GM-reviewed

## Purpose
Represent environmental conditions as constraints on the world without creating an automatic weather generator.

## Domains
- season timing
- weather conditions
- agriculture
- harvest work
- livestock and fodder
- river levels
- road conditions
- mountain passes
- shipping
- storms and fog
- flooding
- drought
- fire risk
- winter preparation

## Core rules
1. Seasons are variable and do not reliably follow month names.
2. Regional tendencies are established, but exact local patterns remain open.
3. No specific weather event is established for 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC unless another approved record states otherwise.
4. Weather should create practical consequences rather than arbitrary punishment.
5. A severe environmental event requires a coherent cause, affected area, duration, and consequence.
6. Weather does not automatically become supernatural.
7. Environmental pressure must propagate through agriculture, transport, trade, health, and military logistics only where relevant.

## Environmental state
Track, where established:
- region;
- current condition;
- duration;
- severity;
- affected systems;
- local knowledge;
- uncertainty;
- source records.

Qualitative conditions are preferred.

## Evaluation
**regional condition → exposed systems → preparedness → disruption → adaptation → consequence**
