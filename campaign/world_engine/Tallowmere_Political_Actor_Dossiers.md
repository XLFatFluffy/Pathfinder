# Tallowmere Political Actor Dossiers

**Snapshot:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC  
**Status:** Consolidated GM reference based on established records.  
**Use:** Living World Engine actor profiles. Unknown resources, secrets, and future actions remain unknown.

## House Denvor — TLM-ACT-HOUSE-DENVOR

**Type:** Minor noble house / local ruling house  
**Leader:** Lord Oswin Denvor  
**Heir:** Ser Edwyn Denvor  
**Seat:** Greywatch, Tallowmere  
**Authority:** Tallowmere and modest surrounding lordship under hereditary charter confirmed by House Kharadorn.

### Interests and goals
- Preserve hereditary legitimacy and local governing authority.
- Keep Tallowmere secure, prosperous, and commercially functional.
- Meet charter obligations without surrendering ordinary civic autonomy.
- Maintain workable relationships with merchants, the Chartered Council, and Kharadorn.
- Preserve the family's reputation and continuity.

### Internal positions
- Oswin favors cautious, negotiated administration and predictable obligations.
- Catrin maintains civic, charitable, and noble relationships and is less patient with officials who mistake courtesy for weakness.
- Edwyn favors greater investment in defenses and infrastructure and is more willing to test boundaries.
- Anwen has informal influence through commercial and civic relationships outside the traditional noble circle.

### Resources and constraints
Hereditary legitimacy, Greywatch, local customary dues, surrounding land, household retainers, a local garrison, and recognized authority over the harbor. Constrained by defense costs, trade dependence, Kharadorn obligations, family policy differences, and the need for civic cooperation. Exact wealth, troop numbers, revenue, and stores are not quantified.

### Relationships
- Kharadorn: formal charter obligations and strategic oversight; negotiated autonomy.
- Chartered Council: advisory and bargaining relationship; practical cooperation matters.
- Avenholt: vassal house.
- Velleran: commercial neighbor and occasional regional partner, not an established enemy.
- Merewick: separate crossing-charter authority with whom road and traffic coordination is necessary.

### Active engine links
TLM-ISS-001 through TLM-ISS-007, especially harbor allocation, defense review, and household priorities.

### Likely responses
Denvor policy is likely to favor negotiation, continuity, and preservation of local authority. Edwyn may press for investment where Oswin would delay. The family is not a single mind. Any response depends on who acts, what evidence they possess, and the cost to the city.

## House Avenholt — TLM-ACT-HOUSE-AVENHOLT

**Type:** Minor noble house / Denvor vassal  
**Leader:** Lady Ysabet Avenholt  
**Heir:** Ser Corvin Avenholt  
**Seat:** Ashcombe Hall, Ashcombe Manor  
**Authority:** Modest agricultural estate and tenant holdings under House Denvor.

### Interests and goals
- Preserve the manor and estate income.
- Maintain reliable relations with Greywatch.
- Protect agricultural productivity and local standing.
- Manage tenant complaints concerning drainage and boundary maintenance.
- Corvin wishes to improve defenses and raise the family's standing; Ysabet is cautious about expenditure.

### Resources and constraints
Productive estate, tenant farms, pasture, small managed woodlot, household retainers, and a small watch post near the northern road. Its garrison is adequate for local security, not a force capable of challenging Tallowmere. Exact income, manpower, debts, and service obligations remain unquantified.

### Relationships
- Denvor: liege and charter authority.
- Tenants: local obligations and unresolved drainage/boundary complaints.
- Other houses: no special alliance or feud established.

### Engine note
The difference between Ysabet's caution and Corvin's ambition is a documented internal policy tension, not a succession crisis. Tenant complaints may become an issue if evidence, neglect, or a costly decision causes them to worsen.

## House Velleran — TLM-ACT-HOUSE-VELLERAN

**Type:** Minor noble house / charter-holding local lordship  
**Leader:** Lord Beric Velleran  
**Heir:** Lady Maud Velleran  
**Seat and principal town:** Harthbridge  
**Authority:** Compact territory and Harthbridge town charter, confirmed by a regional authority within Kharadorn's wider sphere.

### Interests and goals
- Preserve Harthbridge's market and toll privileges.
- Keep inland trade moving through the town.
- Maintain local standing without provoking larger powers.
- Decide whether to invest in roads or preserve existing toll income.
- Compete commercially with Tallowmere while cooperating where regional security and grain movement require it.

### Internal positions
- Beric is cautious and protective of the town's existing position.
- Maud is more commercially ambitious and manages much of the town's commercial correspondence.

### Resources and constraints
Town charter, market yard, toll privileges, compact territory, local watch, household retainers, and commercial relationships. Exact income, force size, and superior obligations remain unknown.

### Relationships
- Relland: subordinate minor house.
- Denvor: commercial rival and practical regional counterpart, not an established feud.
- Superior charter authority: within Kharadorn's wider sphere; precise chain and obligations remain unresolved.

### Engine note
Harthbridge's rivalry with Tallowmere is commercial and jurisdictional. It does not justify hostility, sabotage, or military escalation without a new cause.

## House Merewick — TLM-ACT-HOUSE-MEREWICK

**Type:** Hereditary charter-holding family  
**Current holder:** Sir Davren Merewick  
**Seat / holding:** Fordmere crossing, ferry, toll office, inn, and market yard  
**Authority:** Separate hereditary crossing charter recognized within Kharadorn's wider sphere.

### Interests and goals
- Keep the crossing profitable and dependable.
- Preserve the family's chartered rights.
- Avoid a competing bridge that diverts traffic.
- Maintain the ferry and crossing approaches.
- Protect toll collection and reputation among merchants.

### Resources and constraints
Crossing and ferry income, toll office, inn/stables/market-yard economy, and a small guard. Floods, repairs, traffic patterns, and competing routes affect income. Exact toll rates, charter text, garrison size, and superior obligations are unknown.

### Relationships
- Denvor: coordinates road access and traffic when city commerce depends on Fordmere; not a liege relationship.
- Merchants and carters: customers and recurring critics of tolls during delays.
- Competing bridge interests: none currently established.

### Engine note
A flood, bridge repair, toll complaint, or proposed competing route can change the family's position. Do not create a bridge project or legal challenge unless an actor actually proposes it.

## House Relland — TLM-ACT-HOUSE-RELLAND

**Type:** Minor noble house / Velleran subordinate  
**Leader:** Lady Mereth Relland  
**Heir:** Ser Corren Relland  
**Holding:** Modest manor and tenant holdings along the South Road, within broader Velleran-chartered territory; exact location and boundary provisional.

### Interests and goals
- Preserve the family's local standing and reputation.
- Maintain income from rents, grazing rights, grain, and road frontage.
- Avoid reopening the concealed misconduct of the previous generation.
- Corren prioritizes restoring income and local reputation; his knowledge of the old incident is incomplete.

### Resources and constraints
Modest estate and limited retainers; enough for local security and estate administration, not a military power. Exact wealth, debts, troop count, and current estate condition are unknown.

### Established history and knowledge
Approximately ten years before campaign start, Ser Deylan Marrick and retainers abused road-patrol authority to extort a travelling merchant family, then murdered the parents. Lord Edvar Relland's household concealed the misconduct. Lady Mereth knows that retainers were accused privately and quietly removed but does not know the full circumstances. Corren knows only that several retainers were dismissed after an incident.

House Relland does not know the ratfolk PC survived or that the vampire PC rescued him. It is not currently hunting the PCs.

### Relationships
- Velleran: liege house.
- Denvor: no established direct relationship beyond regional geography.
- Ratfolk PC: concealed personal history; PC knowledge is distinct from house knowledge.

### Engine note
The past incident is an unresolved accountability matter, not an active investigation. If revealed, responses may include denial, inquiry, legal action, political protection, or internal reform, depending on evidence and incentives. Do not assume Lady Mereth ordered the original crimes.

## House Kharadorn's Tallowmere Charter Office — TLM-ACT-KHARADORN-CHARTER

**Type:** Great House institution / charter oversight  
**Local representative:** Vardek Flintmark, Charter Warden  
**Clerk:** Belda Ironmark  
**Location:** Charter House near Harbor Office.

### Interests and authority
- Review tribute accounts and report arrears or irregularities.
- Inspect harbor-defense stores and relevant works.
- Confirm availability of ships, supplies, and transport lawfully promised under the charter.
- Receive petitions about charter breaches or Kharadorn overreach.
- Report to Kharadorn authorities.

Vardek cannot rewrite the charter, command the city watch, seize private property without lawful authority, or adjudicate ordinary civil/criminal disputes. The office has no permanent field army; Vardek's ordinary escort is four trained guards when warranted.

### Position
Vardek prefers verifiable accounts and predictable exercise of Kharadorn rights. He respects Denvor's local authority and believes a prosperous, cooperative city is more useful than one damaged by overreach. He will not conceal a genuine charter failure.

### Engine note
The current harbor-defense review is legitimate strategic oversight, not an established pretext for occupation. Findings, demands, costs, and any disagreement must arise from actual inspection.

## Tallowmere Chartered Council — TLM-ACT-CHARTERED-COUNCIL

**Type:** Advisory and negotiating institution  
**Seats:** Seven recognized seats; Lord Oswin presides when present. Town Reeve Marrec Holt keeps records.

### Established members
- Harbor Fellowship: Captain-Owner Tavin Rusk.
- Warehouse Guild: Meret Sorn.
- Market Guild: Beren Harlowe.
- River Carters' and Bargemen's Company: Pellan Reed.
- Crafts: Dagna Coppervein.
- Property holders: Edran Pellor.
- Civic household: Lady Anwen Denvor.

### Authority and leverage
The Council advises on market privileges, harbor works, taxation proposals, roads, walls, labor disputes, and prosperity. It cannot depose the Denvors, appoint their officers, overturn lawful judgments, or compel acceptance. Its leverage is practical: investment, shipping, labor, repairs, and cooperation.

Quorum is four of seven; recommendations are recorded by majority of those present. The lord may accept, reject, delay, or act independently within lawful authority.

### Current divisions
Members differ on harbor capacity, fire-safety costs, market access, river traffic, skilled labor, property levies, and civic representation. Their positions are documented in the Chartered Council record and should not be flattened into a single merchant faction.

### Engine note
The Council can delay, negotiate, or withdraw cooperation, but its members have different constituencies and cannot be assumed to act as a unified bloc.
