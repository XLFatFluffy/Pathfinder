# Tallowmere Rumor Truth and Information Register

**Snapshot:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC  
**Visibility:** GM-ONLY  
**Status:** Initial truth assignments and deliberate unknowns for the rumors in Current_Situation_and_Rumors.md.

This file separates objective GM truth from what people say. A rumor's reliability describes how accurately it reports the underlying fact, not whether the speaker is honest. Rumors may be distorted through repetition.

## Rumor register

### RUM-TLM-001 — A warehouse clerk has been altering cargo tallies

**GM truth:** TRUE, but limited. A junior tally clerk at one privately operated warehouse has adjusted several entries to conceal ordinary breakage and spoilage. The clerk was trying to avoid a personal wage deduction and disciplinary action, not operating a major theft ring.

**Important boundary:** This has no established connection to the recent warehouse fires, the Grey Net, the Bent Seal, or any wider conspiracy. The clerk's identity and exact warehouse are not yet established.

**What is known:** A few counting-house workers have noticed inconsistencies. No formal accusation has been made.

**PC discovery:** Requires a relevant ledger, witness, audit, or investigation. The rumor alone is not proof.

### RUM-TLM-002 — A merchant consortium is quietly buying riverside property

**GM truth:** DISTORTED. Two separate commercial partnerships have made inquiries about leases and storage access near Rivergate. No coordinated consortium has been established, and no verified purchase of a large block of property has occurred.

**Important boundary:** This is ordinary commercial positioning at present. Do not connect it to the warehouse fires or a secret plan to control the city.

**What is known:** Property agents have received inquiries. The identities and final intentions of the prospective tenants remain incomplete.

### RUM-TLM-003 — Someone has been entering sealed passages beneath an old quay

**GM truth:** TRUE, but misunderstood. A dockworker has used an old service access to retrieve tools and recover small items lost behind a blocked storage wall. The passage is a limited, unsafe maintenance space, not a newly discovered route through the Underways.

**Important boundary:** This does not establish a secret smuggling tunnel, a hidden faction, or access to the wider subterranean network. The exact access point is not fixed in this record.

**What is known:** Dockside workers have seen signs of recent movement. No reliable witness has identified the person.

### RUM-TLM-004 — A Kharadorn envoy is coming with demands beyond the charter

**GM truth:** FALSE AS OF CAMPAIGN OPENING. No additional envoy with expanded demands is currently scheduled. The rumor has grown from Vardek Flintmark's legitimate harbor-defense and charter review.

**What is true:** Kharadorn expects accurate accounts and will report genuine failures. The review may still produce recommendations or lawful demands under the existing charter, but none has yet been issued.

**What is known:** The Charter Warden's office is active. The rumor exaggerates the scope and certainty of the next step.

### RUM-TLM-005 — A ship reported lost in a storm has been seen farther along the eastern shore

**GM truth:** UNRESOLVED. No reliable identification has been made. The sighting may involve a similar vessel, a delayed ship, a false report, or a genuinely missing vessel.

**Boundary:** Do not decide the ship's fate or create a rescue plot until evidence or a GM decision establishes it.

### RUM-TLM-006 — A minor landholder is struggling to pay debts

**GM truth:** TRUE IN GENERAL, IDENTITY UNRESOLVED. At least one small rural landholder is under pressure from ordinary seasonal borrowing and delayed receipts. No default, foreclosure, or noble forfeiture is established.

**Boundary:** This is not currently identified as House Avenholt, Velleran, Relland, or any newly created continental house. Do not assign the debt to a named actor without evidence.

### RUM-TLM-007 — A ratfolk workshop has produced an unusually fine piece of alchemical apparatus

**GM truth:** TRUE, modestly. Miraq Ashwhisk and another craftworker completed a well-made precision retort and condenser assembly for a private customer. It is a notable piece of practical craft, not a magic item and not evidence of a new alchemical formula.

**What is known:** A few craftspeople and Underways traders have seen or heard about the apparatus. The customer's identity and whether the piece has been delivered are not public facts.

**PC opportunity:** The ratfolk alchemist may recognize the quality of the work or find a reason to speak with Miraq. No automatic sale, formula, or special mechanical benefit is implied.

### RUM-TLM-008 — Someone is paying for old maps of the inland sea and Dragonstone approaches

**GM truth:** TRUE, but the motive is commercial and historical research. A factor working for Lady Seralyth Vaelorian's Western Pass commercial review has purchased or commissioned copies of old inland-sea charts, including historical approaches around Dragonstone. The charts are old and incomplete; they are not reliable navigation guides or evidence of a plan to seize the island.

**What is known:** Brokers and sailors have noticed inquiries. They do not necessarily know the buyer's principal.

**Boundary:** This does not expose the dwarven underground route to Dragonstone. That route remains a GM-only secret and is not represented on the purchased surface charts.

### RUM-TLM-009 — The warehouse fires were deliberate

**GM truth:** UNRESOLVED. The causes of the small fires and near-misses have not been established. Carelessness, faulty equipment, unsafe storage, and deliberate action remain possibilities, but none is confirmed.

**Boundary:** Do not use the cargo-tally alteration or any other rumor as proof of arson. Keep investigations evidence-led.

### RUM-TLM-010 — A noble household is seeking discreet magical services

**GM truth:** UNRESOLVED. No household or service request is confirmed. The rumor may conflate an inquiry for a physician, apothecary, alchemist, or rare specialist with magical services.

**Boundary:** Do not assign this rumor to a named house or NPC until a source is identified.

## GM use and information boundaries

This is a reference for the objective truth, uncertainty, and information boundaries behind the opening rumors. It does not determine who has heard a rumor automatically.

- Keep the truth of a rumor separate from what a particular NPC or PC has heard or believes.
- When a character investigates, use available evidence, source reliability, access, and the character's plausible contacts to determine what they learn.
- Do not connect unrelated rumors into one conspiracy merely because they appear in the same register.
- Revise an entry only when play or an explicit GM decision establishes new facts or changes who knows them.
