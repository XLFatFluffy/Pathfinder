# Unknowns and Discovery Model

**Status:** Phase 3 model established
**Baseline:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC
**Execution:** GM-reviewed

## Purpose

Track things that are unresolved, hidden, disputed, or not yet established without silently converting uncertainty into fact.

## Unknown classes

- **UNRESOLVED:** the campaign does not establish the answer.
- **HIDDEN:** an answer may exist in-world but is not currently known to the relevant actors or players.
- **DISPUTED:** conflicting claims exist.
- **UNVERIFIED:** a claim or report exists but lacks sufficient confirmation.
- **DISCOVERABLE:** evidence may plausibly establish the answer through future play or world development.

Unknown status is not a prediction of what will eventually be true.

## Discovery record

A discovery may track:

- discovery ID;
- subject;
- prior information state;
- discoverer;
- evidence or source;
- new information;
- confidence;
- visibility;
- affected actors;
- affected state records;
- resulting consequence.

Discovery changes knowledge first. It does not retroactively rewrite the underlying world.

## Rules

1. Unknown does not mean false.
2. Hidden does not mean predetermined.
3. Rumor does not become fact through repetition.
4. Discovery requires a plausible information path.
5. A PC discovering something does not make the information globally known.
6. An actor may act on a false belief without changing objective truth.
7. The engine must preserve competing interpretations when the evidence does not resolve them.

## GM boundary

The World Engine may identify what is unresolved and what evidence could resolve it. It must not invent a secret answer solely to fill an empty field.

A hidden truth may remain undefined until the campaign establishes it.
