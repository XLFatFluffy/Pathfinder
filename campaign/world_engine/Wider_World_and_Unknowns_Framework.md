# Wider World and Unknowns Framework

**Status:** Phase 4 framework established from current campaign records  
**Campaign date:** 1 Harvestfall, 243 AC

This record defines the boundary between the established known world and regions or subjects that remain poorly understood. It is a scope record, not a license to invent unexplored geography.

## Established scope

The campaign records establish a continent with:

- six Great Houses and the Crown;
- the Kharadorn dwarven empire and its clans;
- established major regional territories and seats;
- settled northern territories and the Ancient Barrier;
- the Frozen Expanse beyond the Ancient Barrier;
- western, central, eastern, and southern regional systems;
- major trade corridors whose precise courses remain uncharted;
- minor houses and independent regional powers;
- the Dothraki as a known and feared orc people.

The current world-state is therefore broader than Tallowmere. Tallowmere is the starting location, not the boundary of the campaign world.

## Known versus unresolved

The campaign may distinguish between:

### Established and broadly known

- Major settlements and fortresses.
- The principal Great Houses and their seats.
- The Crown and Imperial Seat.
- Major regional powers already documented in the campaign records.
- The existence of the Dothraki.
- The existence and historical importance of the fallen dragon-riding dynasty.
- The Great Rebellion and the post-Rebellion political settlement.
- The existence of the dwarven empire and Khar-Dum.
- The existence of the Ancient Barrier and the Frozen Expanse beyond it.

### Known but incompletely mapped or described

- Minor settlements and local holdings.
- Regional political boundaries.
- Trade-route courses.
- Local port jurisdictions.
- Some minor-house holdings.
- Travel times and precise distances.
- The detailed extent of many regional systems.

### Poorly understood or unresolved

- The extent of the world beyond the established continental scope.
- Distant or poorly understood regions.
- The builders and original purpose of the Ancient Barrier.
- Conditions and geography beyond the Barrier beyond what existing records establish.
- Detailed Dothraki territory and internal organization.
- The future relevance and fate of surviving dragons.
- Other unresolved subjects recorded in the World Engine unknowns register.

## Map discipline

No new exact borders, coordinates, routes, settlements, or political polygons should be added merely because a region needs to appear complete.

Authoritative artwork and calibrated atlas records govern visual placement.

Regional descriptions are not surveyed geometry.

Political regions should not overlap unless a specific established condition, such as occupation or war, justifies the overlap. General geographic regions may overlap.

## World Engine treatment

The World Engine should treat incomplete world knowledge as a meaningful state rather than a defect to be repaired.

When an actor lacks information, the engine may track:

- what the actor believes;
- what the actor has heard;
- what evidence exists;
- what remains unknown;
- what information could plausibly be discovered.

The engine must not turn an unknown region into a fully described location simply because a simulation requires an answer.

## Discovery and expansion

New world information should enter through one of the following:

- an authoritative campaign record;
- an explicit GM decision;
- a discovered in-world fact;
- a simulated event whose consequences establish new information;
- a clearly labeled proposal awaiting approval.

A newly discovered location should not automatically receive invented political borders, population, routes, economy, military strength, or history.
