## Recording rules

- Use a stable event ID and the campaign calendar.
- Record the trigger, prior state, involved actors, what each actor knew, actions, outcome, and resulting state changes.
- Separate objective GM truth from public accounts, rumors, and disputed versions.
- Record PC involvement and what the PCs actually learned.
- Events proposed by the engine remain PROPOSED until the GM approves them.
- A world-turn report is not itself an event; record the specific changes it produces.
- Do not record routine daily life unless it changes an issue, actor, project, relationship, resource, or campaign opportunity.
