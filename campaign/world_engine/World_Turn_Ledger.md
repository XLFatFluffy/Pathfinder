## World-turn procedure

1. Confirm the in-world date and amount of time advanced.
2. Review only actors and issues affected by that interval.
3. Check motivation, capability, opportunity, knowledge, opposition, risk, and alternatives.
4. Resolve actions from established goals and resources; do not roll random events without a causal basis.
5. Distinguish objective changes from rumors and incomplete reports.
6. Prepare a GM report with visible, regional, wider, and GM-only sections.
7. Identify any choices that require the GM.
8. Wait for GM approval before recording proposed events as canon.
9. Update the event ledger, issue register, actor dossiers, project register, timeline, and current world state as needed.

## Campaign-opening rule

The campaign begins on **1 Harvestfall, 243 AC**. The first world turn will be a baseline opening simulation for that date, not a simulation of time elapsed before the PCs arrive. It will establish what is already in motion, what actors are doing that day, what information is circulating, and what the PCs could plausibly encounter.

Do not advance the calendar to run this opening snapshot. Any later turn must cover only time that actually passes in play or time explicitly advanced by the GM.
