## 3. Wider political developments
Include only actors with a credible interest, capability, opportunity, and information. Do not force a major event merely to fill this section.

## 4. Projects advanced or stalled
Record progress and obstacles. Do not invent completion percentages.

## 5. Issues changed
Record clock/status changes and the cause. Issues may stall or reverse.

## 6. Rumors and reports
For each rumor, record source, reliability, distortion risk, and who can hear it.

## 7. GM-only developments
Hidden actions and objective truth. Keep separate from public information.

## 8. Decisions requiring GM input
List only decisions the engine cannot resolve without choosing new canon or determining an outcome at the table.

## 9. Proposed events
List proposed event records. Nothing becomes canon until the GM approves it.

## 10. Database changes
