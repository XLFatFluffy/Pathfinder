# World Turn 000 — Pre-Campaign Baseline

**Date:** 1 Goldleaf, 243 AC  
**Simulation status:** Baseline  
**PCs:** Absent

## Purpose

This is the starting state for the one-month pre-campaign simulation. It establishes the conditions from which subsequent turns must proceed.

## Continental baseline

- Crown authority is stable. King Aldric Calderan is pursuing gradual administrative strengthening.
- The Great Houses remain politically autonomous within the post-Rebellion settlement.
- No Great House war, rebellion, succession crisis, or coordinated anti-Crown conspiracy is established.
- House Veyr is preparing Northern Watch autumn provisioning and remains dependent on southern support.
- House Edarth is reviewing banner-house obligations.
- House Vaelorian is reviewing Western Pass commercial terms.
- House Merrowen is gathering information on eastern pilotage and harbor practices.
- House Kharadorn retains its established strategic position over Dragonstone and the Inland Sea.
- Southern regions remain politically fragmented.

## Tallowmere baseline

- Harbor berth allocation is an active seasonal dispute.
- Warehouse fire precautions remain an active issue with unknown cause.
- Northfield harvest is expected to be adequate but uneven.
- Ratfolk market access remains an active civic issue.
- Denvor household priorities remain under discussion.
- Kharadorn harbor-defense review is active.
- Skilled labor, credit, and supply pressures remain fragmented.
- The Longmead boundary dispute between Harrowden and Wrenford is active at low intensity.

## Simulation rule

Turn 001 begins from this exact baseline. No later turn may silently reset these conditions.
