# World Turn 003

**Date:** 15–22 Goldleaf, 243 AC  
**Previous state:** World Turn 002  
**PCs:** Absent

## House Veyr

Southern deliveries arrive sufficiently to prevent an established shortage, though some arrive later than preferred. Veyr protects existing reserves rather than increasing discretionary consumption.

**Result:** Provisioning is substantially adequate. Concern about southern reliability increases modestly.

## House Edarth

Harrowden and Wrenford submit arguments concerning customary rights at Longmead. Edarth favors preserving harvest stability and does not issue a final ruling.

A temporary understanding allows continued use of the disputed area while the claim remains under review.

**Result:** Longmead is temporarily contained, not resolved.

## Crown

Othmar Rusk produces a preliminary recommendation to distinguish ordinary road-service obligations from duties requiring compensation.

**Result:** Modest administrative progress. No major increase in Crown central authority.

## House Vaelorian

Seralyth begins negotiations with commercial interests concerning route guarantees. No major agreement is concluded.

## House Merrowen

Eastern port reports confirm substantial variation in local pilotage practice. Merrowen begins considering a framework that preserves local customs while improving predictability.

## System changes

- Veyr provisioning: stable, with increased concern about reliability.
- Longmead: temporarily contained.
- Crown road review: preliminary recommendation.
- Vaelorian Western Pass review: negotiation phase.
- Merrowen pilotage review: policy consideration begins.

## Major non-events

No war, assassination, rebellion, invasion, naval mobilization, famine, or territorial seizure occurred.

## Continuity

Turn 004 begins from this state.
